# Manual test — P10e the scene stage: full frame, prose subtitles, the history curtain, the posture dial (in SillyTavern)

Run in a campaign with at least two imported soldiers that have sprites (expression files in SillyTavern) and a location with a P10c background (the mess hall is one). Use a 1920×1080 window. Turn **Debug mode** on only if a step says so. Nothing here changes a rule: the scene looks different and the same things are offered. Nothing gives the commander a pronoun.

1. **A scene at 1920×1080.** Open a scene with one soldier. The room fills the whole screen, her knee-length sprite fills the height (head and shoulders high, the knees under the bottom shadow), and nothing but a small top line, her reply, one rounded input line and a small dial is drawn over it. No boxes, no `[ BRACKETS ]` of the stage's own. With two or three soldiers the sprites stand left, centre and right, overlapping by no more than a shoulder; the one who spoke last is brighter. Switch the slot (evening, night): the sprite takes the light of the room.
2. **A reply with actions and quotes.** Send a message. The reply lies at the bottom: `*actions*` in italics (no asterisks), quoted speech in her accent colour, plain prose in the normal colour; nothing is missing. A reply the model left unbalanced (a quote or an asterisk with no partner) shows the marks as plain text and nothing disappears. With the typing speed above 0 the newest sentence is softly highlighted while the reply types out, and the highlight is gone when it ends. Under Reduce motion the reply shows at once.
3. **A long reply.** Get a reply of more than eight lines (or ask for a long scene). The block keeps its last eight lines under a fade with "▲ more above". Roll the wheel up over the reply, or click the mark: the history opens (step 4). SYS › Conversations › "Open the history for replies longer than N words": set it to 100 and the next long reply opens the history by itself; 0 turns it off.
4. **The curtain.** In the history: every message in order, her name or YOU over each, prose as in the reply. Hover a message: ✎ edit (Ctrl+Enter saves, Esc cancels the edit and not the history). On the last reply: ‹ n / m › and ↻. The input is at the bottom of the history and you can answer from there. Esc, the history button or the wheel down at the end closes it; the sprite slides right and stays visible, the dock moves right of the curtain.
5. **↑ in an empty input.** Click the input line, press ↑ with nothing typed: the history opens. With text in the line ↑ does nothing.
6. **Look around, then recentre.** Hold the right mouse button on the picture and drag: the room slides sideways (it is wider than the screen). Release: the view stays. A double right-click centres it. The browser's menu does not open on the picture, and still opens on the chips and cards. Move the pointer with no button: nothing moves. Under Reduce motion dragging still works, without easing. The two dim hints at the bottom-left go away after you use each.
7. **Tab raises the PDA.** In a scene press Tab (not from a field or a button): the PDA slides up over the dimmed scene. Send a message or wait for a reply: it arrives underneath. Esc (or Tab) lowers it. With the history open, Esc closes the history first and the PDA second. Tab while typing in the input only moves focus.
8. **Busy hands.** Put the commander's hands out of use (a wearable such as paw mitts, from the BLOCKING menu). Tab: the PDA does not rise and "The device is out of reach." flashes over the picture for a moment; it never stays on screen.
9. **Cards in the dock.** Get two cards at once (for example a task and a punishment, or a moment and a milestone). They stand in one dock at the left, vertically centred, never over her face: one open, the other collapsed to its heading. Click the heading: it opens and the other collapses. Press × on the open card: it is set aside and "1 waiting" appears; click it to bring the card back. A redraw (a new message) keeps the arrangement.
10. **The dial.** With the blocking live (a soldier at close tier or above) the dial is bottom right: the commander's body as a small three-quarter mannequin with the posture's name under it. Click its centre: a fan opens with the postures on offer (each as a small figure with its name), then places, contacts and what is worn; a refused one is greyed out and says why. Choose KNEEL: the figure kneels and the name changes; if a holder gave that posture her own name, her name shows. Without the blocking live the corner is empty.
11. **A physics run.** Start a hold (a contact or an item that holds). The hold's panel is a card of the dock, and a STOP WORD pill is pinned at the right end of the chip row, where it stays even if you collapse or set the card aside. Pressing either ends the hold.
12. **A chat switch.** With the view dragged to one side, the history open and a card set aside, switch to another chat (or another campaign) and open a scene there: the view is centred, the history is closed, no card is set aside, and both hints are back.

Report anything that is cut off at 1280×800 or 2560×1440, a head under a card or the history, text that is missing from a reply, a menu that opens on the picture, a Tab that raises the PDA while typing, a card or chip the old screen had that you cannot find, or a pronoun for the commander.
