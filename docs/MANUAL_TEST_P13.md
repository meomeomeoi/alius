# P13 — acceptance checklist (LEASHSWAP mode)

Automated coverage per acceptance criterion (all in `npm test`), then the steps that must be done by hand inside SillyTavern. The automated tests run in jsdom and with fake models: they cannot judge how the chips, the panel and the end screen read, whether the model keeps to the directives, whether the rewind really hides the chat, or whether the metadata stays small.

## Automated
1. **Both selection modes work; the corporate assignment follows the ordering:** `tests/warden/select.test.ts`, `tests/ui/wardenSetup.test.ts`, `tests/ui/wardenReveal.test.ts`, `tests/warden/agenda.test.ts`.
2. **Overrides fire by the formula; COMPLY / ARGUE / DEFY each resolve; `compromise_reached` resolves without defiance:** `tests/warden/conflict.test.ts`, `override.test.ts`, `argue.test.ts`, `argueController.test.ts`, `judge.test.ts`, `expectationEvents.test.ts`, `tests/ui/wardenOverride.test.ts`, `tests/llm/intentOverride.test.ts`.
3. **All commander punishments apply; the Leash on the commander forces or suppresses; squad Leash requests need her co-sign:** `tests/warden/punish.test.ts`, `leash.test.ts`.
4. **Lethal escalation needs two warnings on different days; death follows the rule (rewind restores the day-start snapshot, campaign end closes the campaign with an epilogue):** `tests/warden/escalation.test.ts`, `death.test.ts`, `rewind.test.ts`, `controller.test.ts`, `tests/ui/endScreen.test.ts`.
5. **All three removal routes work, start the investigation, apply squad reactions and remote control; both verdicts resolve:** `tests/warden/intrigue.test.ts`, `kompromat.test.ts`, `hack.test.ts`, `removal.test.ts`, `verdict.test.ts`, `memory.test.ts`, `tests/ui/intrigueSection.test.ts`, `tests/analysis/wardenProviders.test.ts`.
6. **Without the mode nothing changes:** `tests/warden/unaffected.test.ts` (a seeded week of day ends, activities, a roster change, scenes with a reading, DMs, a dilemma, a Leash request and a fight, pinned to a golden made on the commit before P13), plus every existing seeded test left unchanged and each hook task's own `no mode: no draw` test.
7. **Project rules:** `tests/content/pronouns.test.ts` (no pronoun for the commander in the new content), `tests/analysis/hidden.test.ts` and `wardenProviders.test.ts` (suspicion, an unfound kompromat fact and an unknown expectation never reach a reading), `tests/ui/analyzeCoverage.test.ts`, `tests/warden/directiveIds.test.ts`, `tests/architecture.test.ts`.
8. **Old saves:** every new field is optional and `version` stays 1: `leashswap`, `ended`, `Unit.status 'reassigned'`, the `tech` / `idle` / `quota` / `wardenRule` fields.

## In SillyTavern
Note the SillyTavern version, the browser and the window sizes at the top of your report. Use a fresh chat for each part, and watch the chat's metadata size (the day-start snapshot is a second key, `alius_day_start`, and roughly doubles it).

- [ ] **Setup — the mode.** WORLD PARAMETERS has a MODE section: DEFAULT and LEASHSWAP, and under LEASHSWAP the death rule (story / normal default to REWIND THE DAY, hard to END THE CAMPAIGN). The UNITS step has a warden select (the chosen units and CORPORATE ASSIGNMENT). Pick a unit with a name that is not first in the squad; finish setup and check that she is the warden. Do it again with CORPORATE ASSIGNMENT and check that the unit with the strongest corporate loyalty holds the leash.
- [ ] **The reveal.** With the prologue: the `warden_reveal` scene is with her alone, in the corporate voice, after the lineup. With Veteran mode: a one-time `WARDEN ASSIGNED` card at the first hub render, once. The model's first replies do not treat her role as old news until it is revealed.
- [ ] **The agenda.** The WARDEN panel (HOME) shows one known expectation and `? UNKNOWN EXPECTATION` rows; no kind or weight of an unknown one is anywhere in the page, the analysis panel or the console. A model agenda replaces the fallback once, before any override.
- [ ] **An override at the base.** Give an order that cuts across a known expectation (for example, an expedition without her, with a keep-me-close warden). The chip reads `WARDEN OVERRIDE — …` and the order did not run. Try COMPLY (her alternative runs, once), DEFY (the order runs; defiance and punishment follow), and ARGUE: a scene with her alone opens, and only a real compromise (say it in the scene) lets the order run without defiance; a scene that ends without one leaves only COMPLY and DEFY.
- [ ] **An override in a dilemma and in combat.** A major dilemma shows the chip on its card and the answers resolve as above; a minor one never does. In combat HOLD spends no CP, and ARGUE is one roll with no scene.
- [ ] **The judge.** Turn on SYS › Relationships › the warden judge, give her a personal expectation and give an order that touches it: it may be overridden; with the setting off, or in combat, it is never asked. A failed call changes nothing.
- [ ] **Punishments and the Leash.** Defy until each punishment has appeared (a reprimand, privileges revoked: no leave and no DMs for a day, confinement, a public humiliation, a pain burst, a compel, a suppress) and check the corporate notice and the panel's restrictions. After a compel only COMPLY works at the next override; a suppress ends the day. Ask for a squad Leash request until she denies one: nothing is spent, the log copies her, and the refusal stands in combat.
- [ ] **Two warnings, then the controller.** Defy until the first warning (a cut-in), wait a day, the final warning, wait a day, the controller is triggered. With REWIND THE DAY the day begins again and the scenes played since are hidden (they can be unhidden from SYS); the toast reads `REWIND — day N begins again.` With END THE CAMPAIGN the END screen replaces the hub: `CAMPAIGN CLOSED`, the day and cause, the epilogue (the model's, or the chronicle lines if the call fails) and EXPORT; nothing else can be pressed. Switch the chat meanwhile: nothing is written to another chat.
- [ ] **Intrigue.** In a scene without her, ask a soldier to stand with the commander: an ally is won or refused (a corporate soldier may report it at once: the commander is found out). DIG with an ally or Cunning 3: kompromat appears in the panel as `KOMPROMAT: n / 3` and the memory view. The INTRIGUE section shows allies, the kompromat count and the hack progress, never a suspicion value; each closed button names its reason.
- [ ] **A discovered step.** Take hack steps until one is noticed: the progress starts over, the commander takes a pain burst, and the warden's satisfaction and defiance move.
- [ ] **Each removal route.** (1) Three clean hack steps: `controller_dark`, the investigation opens (a 10-segment band), remote control starts. (2) REPORT TO OVERSIGHT with three kompromat: the dilemma opens (queued behind another decision if one waits), SUBMIT reassigns her or is a discovery. (3) Make her desert (or let a mutiny fight take her out): the death route, loud, a 4-segment band. Each route's squad reactions follow their values (an autonomy-minded unit gains, a corporate one loses).
- [ ] **Remote control.** The panel shows `QUOTA` and `OBEDIENCE` and no override or negotiation exists; fail a mission and take a pain burst; go a week with no mission completed and take a reprimand. The directive `warden:remote` is in the prompt.
- [ ] **Both verdicts.** When the clock fills, the verdict rolls once. Cleared: no warden, no co-sign, the panel is gone, the commander plays on. Convicted: a new warden is assigned by the corporate ordering (satisfaction 40, a new agenda call); with nobody eligible the commander is executed and the campaign ends even under REWIND THE DAY.
- [ ] **Romance with the warden.** In a romantic-tier state with her, the `warden:romance` directive is in the prompt and the model keeps the controller in the scene; the other expectations (`no_romance_with_others`) are broken by flirting with someone else in a scene.
- [ ] **Text and analysis.** Read every notice, chip and the panel: no pronoun for the commander anywhere. Press every chip and button with the analysis tool on: each opens a reading, none shows the suspicion, a chance or an unknown expectation.
- [ ] **Narrow window.** At 360–400 px the chip wraps and stays reachable, the panel's INTRIGUE buttons wrap with their reasons, and the END screen does not push sideways.
- [ ] **Old save and default mode.** Load a save from before P13 and start a default campaign: nothing from this package appears, and a week plays as before.
