# P10d "Base Plan" Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The hub's cross-section becomes a **top-down plan of the outpost**, drawn in SVG from data:
- two floors and the roofs, switched by 1F / 2F / ROOF buttons and by clicking a staircase;
- an irregular building of offset blocks with branching corridors, dead ends and one loop; the compound around it (training yard, fence, towers, gate, roads, the lived-in odds and ends);
- every soldier has **her own cabin**, labelled with her name, scattered over the upper floor; free cabins read VACANT;
- places the game does not have yet (showers, storage, laundry, briefing, armory, gym, library, …) are on the plan, dark, hatched and not clickable, and come alive when a pack gives them a hub room;
- room labels and unit markers are **HTML over the SVG** (readable at any size, clickable, analyzable);
- the time of day lights the plan: occupied rooms glow at dusk and night, emergency lights in the corridors and floodlights at night.

**Groundwork for 0.2 (author decision 2026-11-20).** The plan is the first interface with *space* (neighbours, doors, corridors, distance); five things are laid now so that P15a–P17e build on the plan instead of rebuilding it:
1. **Doors are a graph.** `planGraph`, `neighbours(reg, roomId)` (through a door, and through a shared wall) and `route(reg, from, to)` (rooms passed, length, floors). For the visitor of a staging (P17), a summons (P17a), footsteps and what the next cabin hears (P17d).
2. **Affordances live on plan rooms.** `planRooms[].affordances` (P17 §2's list and P15a's future ones: `desk`, `bunk`, `curtain`, `lock`, `line`, `cupboard`, `exposed`, `crowd`, `thin_walls`, `water`, `mirror`, …): data only in P10d, no reader. P17 reads them here, not on `hubRooms` / `locations`.
3. **A person, not a unit.** `roomOfPerson(state, reg, here, personId)` answers for a unit and for the commander (`'commander'`): in P10d the commander is always in the commander's cabin and is **not drawn**; P16 (`held`), P17a (`held_night`) and P17b (the cage) move the commander by adding a derived reason, and the marker is one flag.
4. **Marks over the plan.** `registerPlanMarks(provider)`: a provider returns `{ planRoomId, kind, label?, analyze? }[]` (a tint, a prop, a chip on a room). The core registers none; P15/P16/P17 draw her territory, a room forbidden to the commander, a locked door, the place of a staging, her props (cage, mat) through it.
5. **A cabin follows its style.** The cabin's furniture takes a variant from her P10c room style, read by field (`floor: 'tatami'` → futon and low table; `ceiling: 'rich'` → a big bed; `ceiling: 'dark'` → a dark cabin); her props (P17c) arrive through the marks of item 4.

The reference is the canvas "Alius — карта хаба (SVG)" (claude.ai artifact, slug `0666c761-e1f3-4cc6-986c-7ad3624bf56d`) and its generator `scripts/art/plan-mockup.py` (template `plan-mockup.tpl`) (geometry, furniture, site and roof decor). The geometry is copied from it; the look follows it with the game's palette variables.

**Architecture:**
- **Data.** A new described registry `planRooms`: every room, corridor and staircase of every floor, in plan units (viewBox 1000 × 625 = the 16:10 stage). An entry links to a hub room (`hubRoomId`) when the place exists in the game; without one it is drawn closed. `hubRooms` lose `rect` (geometry now lives in `planRooms`); everything else in them (whereabouts, activities, hosts, panels) is unchanged.
- **Engine.** A pure module `src/engine/basePlan.ts`: `cabinOf(state, reg, unitId)` (derived, no state, no rng), `planRoomsOn(reg, floor)`, `roomOfUnit(...)` (which plan room a whereabout is drawn in), `litRooms(...)`.
- **UI.** `src/ui/hub/plan/` replaces the cross-section: `svg.ts` (an SVG helper on `createElementNS`), `geometry.ts` (union outlines), `furnish.ts` (furniture by `kind`), `site.ts` and `roof.ts` (the compound and the roof decor, constants), `planView.ts` (the stage: SVG + HTML overlay of rooms, labels, markers, stairs, floor buttons). Colours come from CSS (`css/15-hub.css`) through classes and palette variables, never from inline colours.
- **Removed.** The P10 hub art (`art.hub`, `base.png`, `src/ui/hub/art.ts`, the blueprint grid): nothing ships it and a picture cannot follow the data. The manifest key stays accepted and is ignored (old packs still load).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 5 (strict), esbuild, zod 3, vitest (+ jsdom for UI). No new dependency.

## Global Constraints

- **English and safe text.** Everything the player reads is English and goes through `h()` / `textContent`; the SVG is built with `createElementNS` and holds no text (labels are HTML).
- **Engine purity.** `src/engine/basePlan.ts` is pure and draws no rng; whereabouts stay derived (P10 rule); no new state field; old saves are untouched.
- **Read by field, never by id.** Code reads plan rooms by `floor`, `kind`, `hubRoomId`, `cabin`, `stairTo`, `open`; hub rooms as P10 does (`hosts`, `commanderCell`, `sceneLocationId`). No code names a plan room or hub room id.
- **Art never gates anything.** Furniture for an unknown `kind` is skipped; a plan room whose hub room is missing is drawn closed; a hub room with no plan room still works through the AWAY / ELSEWHERE strips (and a content check reports it).
- **P7a.** Every label, floor button, staircase, cabin and closed room carries `data-analyze` (`hubRooms:<id>`, `planRooms:<id>`, `glossary:cabin`, `glossary:base_floor`); new glossary entries `cabin`, `base_floor`, `closed_room`.
- **Motion.** The emergency-light blink and the alarm-free glow ask `motionReduced`; readable mode drops the glow and the specks.
- **Memory check (CLAUDE.md).** Nothing is remembered: the plan is a view; cabins are derived; no event, decision or state is added. No prompt text changes.
- **Pronouns.** No text gives the commander a pronoun.

## Where the plan rules

- **Plan units.** `rect {x, y, w, h}` in a 1000 × 625 box (the hub stage is 16:10, so 1 unit = 0.1 % of the width = 0.16 % of the height). The HTML overlay places rooms at `left = x/10 %`, `top = y/6.25 %`.
- **`planRooms` entry:** `{ id, label, shortLabel?, floor: 'ground' | 'upper' | 'roof', rect, kind, description, hubRoomId?, sceneLocationId?, cabin?: 'unit' | 'commander', stairTo?: floor, doors?: [{x, y, side: 'n'|'s'|'e'|'w', size}], labelAt?: {x, y}, affordances?: string[] }`. A door's `side` is the way its leaf swings; the door lies on an edge of its room.
  - `kind`: furniture and roof finish (`corridor`, `lobby`, `stair`, `cabin`, `mess`, `kitchen`, `medbay`, `workshop`, `garage`, `comms`, `yard`, `roof`, `storage`, `laundry`, `showers`, `gym`, `armory`, `briefing`, `map`, `library`, `rec`, `office`, `lockers`, `generator`, `lookout`, …). `corridor`, `lobby` and stairs without doors are **open**: their walls are the outline of their union (no wall where two meet).
  - **open / closed:** a room is open when `hubRoomId` resolves, or it is a cabin, a corridor, a lobby or a staircase; otherwise closed (dark, hatched, `glossary:closed_room` + its `description`).
  - `sceneLocationId` overrides the hub room's (the commander's cabin talks in `quarters_commander`, a unit cabin in `quarters_unit`).
- **Roofs are derived:** the high roof is the union of the upper floor; low roofs are the ground-floor parts not under it (kinds `gym` and `garage` corrugated). The `roof` floor holds only what is clickable there (the hub's ROOF deck) and the roof decor (`roof.ts`).
- **Cabins (derived, stable):** let `k` be a unit's index among **all** units in `state.squad` (append-only: a departed unit keeps her index). Living units in squad order take `cabins[k % n]` (`n` = plan rooms with `cabin: 'unit'`, in registry order: authors order them so the first ones are far apart); if that cabin is held by a living unit with a smaller index, the next free one in registry order. More living units than cabins: the rest share the last cabin found (a content check warns when `n` is below the roster capacity). A departed unit's cabin reads VACANT until the order comes round to it. No state, no rng, no change when someone leaves.
- **Where a marker is drawn:** a whereabout in a hub room is drawn in the plan room of that hub room on its floor; a unit in the room with `hosts: night` / `confined` (the quarters) is drawn **in her own cabin**; the commander's cabin draws no unit. A plan room's markers sit in its `labelAt` row (default: the bottom-left corner, beside the label).
- **Floors:** the view keeps its floor per hub host in a `WeakMap` (like the open room: a redraw keeps it, a reload starts on the ground floor). Floor buttons show how many soldiers are on each floor (`2F · 3`). Clicking a staircase (`stairTo`) switches to that floor. Keys: `PageUp` / `PageDown` on the hub change the floor (not while typing; not from the device).
- **Room panels:** clicking an open room opens P10's panel for its hub room; for a cabin the panel lists only her (TALK at her quarters), for the commander's cabin the P10b notes and the room's activities (`commanderCell`).
- **Light:** `data-slot` on the hub root (already there). Lit rooms (someone is drawn there, or the commander's cabin) glow at evening and night; emergency lights in every corridor at night (a half-disc glow that never crosses a wall); tower floodlights at night. All of it by CSS on classes.
- **The graph:** a door joins the two plan rooms on either side of its leaf (probed 2 units off the middle of the door, across its wall; nothing there = `outside`); open spaces (corridors, lobbies, open stairs) that touch are joined without a door; a staircase joins its floor to the staircase of `stairTo` that overlaps it. `neighbours(..., 'wall')` = rooms of the same floor sharing at least 8 units of wall with no door between. `route` is a breadth-first search with corridor lengths as weights (Dijkstra over rect-centre distances); it returns `null` when there is no way.
- **The commander** is in the commander's cabin unless a later package adds a reason; P10d draws no commander marker.
- **Marks:** providers run on every draw, in registration order; an unknown `kind` draws a plain outline (no error); a mark's chip carries its `analyze` or `glossary:base_floor`.
- **Narrow screens:** below 700 px wide the labels shrink to their short form (`shortLabel`, else the first word) and the floor buttons stack; the stage keeps its 16:10.
