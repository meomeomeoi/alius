# P16 Practices (Tasks, Her Discipline, Demonstrations, Service, Denial, Holding) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** what a holder (a soldier at frame ≥ `play`, P15) actually does with the commander, day to day:
- she gives **tasks** (generated in her voice from seeds; the engine owns stakes, checks and costs);
- she **punishes** the commander when a task, a rule, a protocol or a denial is broken, or a refusal is made at `role`;
- she **shows** her hold before others (demonstrations), keeps the commander in **service** (protocol, routine, verdicts, favour) and under **denial** (a permission rule);
- she **holds** the commander in her quarters (a booking kind, the sensory mode, the alarm decision, a run that leaves without the commander);
- the commander asks for a task or a punishment, hands things over, asks her to choose;
- the 2026-11-20 revision: the consent ladder, the slap, the ceremony, sealed practices, no duration, unfairness, anticipation, named postures.

All of it is off unless the dynamic is on (`dynamicOn`) and someone holds at frame ≥ `play`.

**Architecture:**
- **Engine:** one new folder, `src/engine/practice/` (state leaf, gate, seeds and validation, requests and issue, tasks and checks, her discipline, demonstrations, service, denial, holding, alarms, initiative, the revision's parts, intents, view). Actions in `src/engine/actions/practice.ts`, imported by `src/app.ts` **after** `actions/dynamic`.
- **No rng draw anywhere in P16.** Every chance is `practiceRoll` (a `dynamicRoll` with a `practice:` key). So the P15 long-run sim and every golden keep their rng.
- **Lower layers take hooks, they import nothing of the practice:** a commander-restriction reader in `warden/punish.ts`, a unit-restriction reader in `discipline/restrictions.ts`, a practice undo hook in `discipline/record.ts`, a dispatch guard (inline, as `booking_due`), the commander's room in `basePlan.ts`, the holder's whereabout in `hub.ts`, the threat list in `dynamic/reading.ts`.
- **One new call type**, `practice_generate`, with six sites `practice.<kind>`, bound by a new controller `src/practice/controller.ts`. One new flavor kind, `posture_name`.
- **`intent_extract`** gains a `practice` input block and seven intent types (plus the compromise ids), each offered only while its subject exists. Directives `practice:`-prefixed, derived.
- **The run without the commander** (user ruling): a flag on the expedition; combat starts at CP 0 with no regen, no orders, no compel; event nodes are decided by the squad.
- **UI:** `src/ui/practice/` (task card, punishment card, demonstration card, alarm card, permission chip, initiative chips, handover composer, ASK HER marks, the slap, the ceremony, the named-posture call, the held overlay, the CMDR record lines, the PRACTICES block in the DYNAMIC section).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 5 (strict), esbuild, zod 3, vitest (+ jsdom for UI). No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/packages/P16-practices.md` (§1–§11, the depth revision of 2026-11-19 and the revision of 2026-11-20 §R; where a later section conflicts with an earlier one the later wins). Read before Task 1:

- the **Implemented as** of P9, P9b, P9c, P9d, P9e, P13, P14, P15a and P15 (P15's **Non-obvious things to know** and its **Left to this package**);

## Global Constraints

- **Off is byte-identical.** The practices are **on** for a holder when `practiceLive(state, reg, holderId)`: `dynamicOn`, `herPracticeLive`, a living named unit, frame ≥ `play`, and not in her aftermath. `practicesOn(state, reg)` = some unit is `practiceLive`. While it is false for everyone:
  - `state.practices` is never written (absent on old saves and on campaigns that never had a holder at `play`);
  - no `practice:` directive, chip, card or call;
  - none of the seven intent types (plus the compromise ids) is offered (`tests/fixtures/intent_extract/prompt_without_reading.txt` stays equal);
  - the dispatch guard `held` answers null; the restriction readers answer null;
  - `tests/dynamic/unaffected.test.ts`, `tests/blocking/unaffected.test.ts`, `tests/warden/unaffected.test.ts`, `tests/special/unaffected.test.ts`, `tests/development/unaffected.test.ts`, `tests/life/unaffected.test.ts` and `tests/engine/setup.test.ts` stay green **unchanged**. A seeded test that moves **stops and reports**; it does not get a new golden.
- **No rng draw.** Every chance and pick is `practiceRoll(state, unitId, key)` = `dynamicRoll(state, unitId, 'practice:' + key)` or an index into a registry-ordered list by `Math.floor(practiceRoll × n)`. A test in every task that adds a chance asserts `state.rng` is unchanged across the action. `tests/sim/dynamicLongRun.test.ts` keeps asserting what it asserts; if one of its assertions fails once practices run in it, **stop and report**.
- **Old saves:** every new field is optional (`CampaignState.practices`, `ExpeditionState.withoutCommand`, `ExpeditionReport.withoutCommand`, `Inverse.practice`, `Booking.practiceId`, the new schema fields). No migration; the version is unchanged.
- **The engine owns every number** (overview P1/P2). The model writes the words of a practice (`text`, `summary`) and proposes its fields; the engine validates, clamps or rejects each field and **never reads `text`**. Directives carry the `summary`, never the `text`.
- **Read by field, never by id:** seeds by `kind` / `tags` / `track` / `tier` / `needs`; punishments by `severity` and `effects[].type`; ceremony steps by `check`; booking kinds by `held`; rule templates by `contentTags` and `asks`; postures through `entryAllowed`; styles through `practiceOf` (`style.primary` / `.secondary` are closed enum values, the only "ids" code may name, as P15 does); domains by capability. Every new registry is in `DESCRIBED_REGISTRIES`; every new glossary id is in `CORE_GLOSSARY_IDS`; the one new setting has `setting_practice_wording`. ### R2. The gate and the state

- `practiceLive(state, reg, holderId): boolean` (above) and `practicesOn(state, reg): boolean` in `src/engine/practice/gate.ts`. `practiceHolders(state, reg): string[]` lists the live holders in squad order.
- `CampaignState.practices?: PracticeState`, created by `ensurePractices(draft)` on the first write; `practicesOf(state)` reads it (`undefined` while absent). `nextPracticeId(draft, prefix)` → `<prefix><n>` from `practices.seq` (prefixes R3).
- **`src/engine/practice/state.ts` is a leaf** (types, the closed enums, `practiceRoll`); it imports only types and `dynamic/witness.ts` (a leaf).
- A holder who stops being live keeps her open practices **frozen**: no new request, no check runs for her, no punishment opens; her open hold ends at once (R11). When she is live again they resume. A **category switched off** withdraws (status `void`, no deltas) every open practice tagged with it (`registerWithdrawal` in `bonds/content.ts`) and ends a hold or a denial rule that needs it. **Removal and death** (`forgetPractices(draft, unitId)`, joined to `removeFromService` after `forgetDynamic` and to the dead-unit sweep, idempotent): her requests, tasks, punishments, demonstrations, the hold she keeps, her permission, postures, handovers, routine, offences and the record lines that name her are dropped (`void` events, no deltas); a task that names her as target is `void`.
- **Imports:** `relations/apply.ts` imports `practice/intents` and `personal/dayStep.ts` / `leash/use.ts` import `practice/forget` (a leaf), as P14c and P15 do; the import ban covers the reader hooks.
- **The restriction readers** (`registerCommanderRestriction`, `registerRestrictionReader`) take the registries (`reader(state, reg, …)`) and read a holder's rows and cold shoulder only while she is `practiceLive`: switched off, fallen to `equal` or in her aftermath, they answer null until she is live again (the rows stay).

### R5. Generation: requests, `practice_generate`, issue (spec §1)

- **The engine decides that a practice comes, and of what kind.** It writes a `PracticeRequest` (day step R7, an offence R8, the commander's initiative R15, a chain R6) and emits `practice_requested { requestId, holderId, kind }`. The seed candidates are picked then (`pickSeeds`: 3–6 gated seeds of that kind, weighted by `weight`, by `practiceRoll`, tier ≤ the track level + 1, `needs` held, `romantic` only at bond tier `romantic`; never a seed with a disabled tag or one of her limits).
- **The controller generates** through `app.generations.generate('practice.<kind>', requestId)`; `build` rebuilds `PracticeGenerateInput` from the state (her card digest ≤ 600 chars, `practiceOf`, frame, domains (capabilities as words), track levels as words, the enabled categories only, her limits (never named: only "some things are hers to refuse"), the `recent` forms (`Relation.dynamic.recent`), context (day, slot, open tasks' summaries, Want band, Impunity band, present units' names), the seeds (`gist`, `stakes`, `check`), the frequency setting, and for a punishment the chosen entry's label and severity). Without a profile or with `practiceWording: 'plain'` the fallback runs at once.
- **Output** `{ kind, seedId?, tags[], track, tier, stakes, check, deadline?: { days, slot? }, proof, audienceIds?, text ≤ 400, summary ≤ 80 }`. **`validatePracticeOutput(state, reg, request, raw): { output: ValidOutput; issues: string[] } | null`** (pure):
  - `kind` must equal the request's kind, else null (`invalid_output`);
  - `tags`: every tag enabled and not in her limits, at least one; a disallowed tag is dropped (an issue); none left → null;
  - `track` must be open (`trackCap > 0`… a track whose category is on), else the seed's track, else null;
  - `tier` clamped to `1 … trackLevel + 1`; `stakes` clamped to the frame's ceiling (`TUNING.practice.stakesCeiling`: `play` minor, `role` major, `rule` severe); `check` must be in `CHECK_KINDS`, else `model`; `checkArg` for `no_scene_with` must be a present-or-squad living named unit id, for `rule_kept_until` an active rule of hers, else the check becomes `model`;
  - `deadline.days` clamped to `TUNING.practice.deadlineDays` (1–7), `slot` must be a slot; `audienceIds` filtered to eligible audience (R9) — for a demonstration none left → null;
  - `text` and `summary`: markup and control characters stripped, cut; a commander pronoun in either → null (the fallback runs); an empty summary → `plainSummary(seed)`.
  - A `seedId` that is not a gated seed of the kind is dropped (the model may invent without a seed, spec §1).
- **`issuePractice(draft, ctx, requestId, output: ValidOutput | 'fallback'): EngineEvent[]`** creates the task / punishment / demonstration / protocol rule / denial rule / hold (by kind) and removes the request. The fallback takes the first candidate seed: `text` = `fallbackLine(kind, gist)` (`TUNING.practice.fallback[kind]`, `AUTHOR TO WRITE: {gist}.` per kind), `summary` = the gist, `plain: true`, its stakes/check/proof/tier. Action `practice/issue { requestId, output? }` (no output = fallback).
- **The day step falls back** for a request older than one day (`requests[].day < day`), so a closed app or a hung call never blocks a practice.
- **P14 sites** (`src/llm/sites.ts`): `practice.task`, `practice.punishment`, `practice.demonstration`, `practice.protocol`, `practice.denial`, `practice.hold`; call type `practice_generate`; surface `comms` (the record key is the request id); reroll **`before_commit`** (`committed(key)` = the practice was answered); record **`full`** (a chip where it is delivered); label `Her practice` (generic). Flavor kind `posture_name` → site `flavor.posture_name` (`none`, `quiet`).
- **Delivery** (spec §3): a request made outside a scene is delivered as a DM from her (`pushMessage`, `initiated: true`; the DM text is the practice `text`, or for a sealed one the envelope line `TUNING.practice.sealedLine`); the card shows in her thread and, when the holder has a hand (`handOf` non-null) and her primary style is `ritual` or `regal`, it renders as a note in her hand (`renderNote`, P10b). A request made in a scene with her shows its card in the scene, and the `practice:issued:<holderId>` directive (`next_reply`) gives the summary to the model.

### R6. Tasks and the consent ladder (spec §3, §R.1)

- **Accept form by stakes** (`acceptFormOf(task)`): `trivial` → `button`; `minor` → `button_cost` (the card shows the cost first, in bands: the slots, resources and bookings that lapse, from `taskCost(state, reg, task)`); `major` → `words` (the card's `[ ACCEPT ]` only focuses the scene input or opens her thread; the task becomes `open` when `intent_extract` reports `practice_accepted { taskId }`; the card also shows who will know: the audience and proof as words); `severe` → `signed` (as `words`, then the card shows what it closes or hands over (`closes`: the summary of an irreversible effect or a handover) and a `[ SIGN ]` hold-to-press button through `registerSignature`); sealed → `sealed` (R16).
- **Answers:** `practice/answer_task { taskId, answer: 'accept' | 'refuse' | 'talk' }`. ACCEPT on a `button`/`button_cost` task opens it; on `words`/`signed` it throws ("Say it in your own words.") — only the intent or (for `signed`) `practice/sign { taskId }` after the words opens it. REFUSE: `refuseLikeDemand(draft, ctx, holderId, { evidence: summary, severity })` (P9 path; `refuseLikeDemand` gains an optional `severity` defaulting to `moderate`: stakes `major` → `major`, `severe` → `extreme`) — its `refused_demand` already feeds `refusalCost('demand')` through P15's feed — plus, at frame ≥ `role`, an offence `refusal` (R8). TALK: opens a scene with her (`startScene` in the controller, origin `event`, `quarters_unit`, `consumesSlot: false`); `compromise_reached { taskId }` replaces `summary` by the agreed one (the model gives `text`, the engine keeps the summary of the intent's `text` cut to 80) and lowers stakes by one.
- **The record (R.1):** every `signed` and `sealed` accept appends `{ day, holderId, line }` to `practices.records` with `line = recordLine(task)` = `` `${cap(summary)}. Day ${day}.` `` (sealed: `"Signed blank. Day N."` from `TUNING.practice.sealedRecord`, `AUTHOR TO WRITE`) and emits `practice_signed` (memory rule, R20). The CMDR card lists the lines under her "Held by" line (newest 5).
- **Chains (B1):** a kept task with `chainId` (or the first kept task of a seed, which starts a chain `chainId = task.id`) asks for the next step the day after with chance `TUNING.practice.chainChance` (0.5, `AUTHOR TO TUNE`): the request carries `chainOf`, the next task's tier floor is the last tier and its ceiling `trackLevel + 1`.
- **Third parties:** `targetUnitId` (a living named unit at base); while she is present the directive `practice:target:<unitId>` tells her what the commander was asked; when a `kept` or `broken` task's target was present, `addSign(draft, ctx, targetId, holderId, 'step_behind')` and the target's `witness` attitude word is in her line.
- **Field tasks:** `field: true` only with checks `on_roster` / `not_on_roster` (the validator clamps); judged at `expedition_ended` against the report's party.
- **Cap and frequency:** at most `TUNING.practice.taskCap[frame]` (play 1, role 2, rule 3) open tasks per holder; no new task request while one of hers at `role`+ is open unless she holds `household_protocol`.
- **Hold, xp, reading:** kept → relation event `task_kept` (feeds +3 through P15), `addTrackXp(track, TUNING.practice.xp.kept × (tier))`, `addFavour(+1)`, `updateReading` sample `yielded` (motive per her reading, weight `alpha.practice`); broken → `task_broken` (−5), offence `task_broken` with the task's stakes, sample `resisted`. Both push the seed's gist key into `Relation.dynamic.recent` (≤ 8).

### R7. The practice day step (spec §3 frequency)

`practiceDayStep(draft, ctx)` is registered with `registerDayEndStep('dm_rumors', …)` from `actions/practice.ts` (after `actions/dynamic`, so it runs after `dynamicDayStep`). Fixed sub-steps, each a no-op for a non-live holder:
1. **deadlines** (R6 checks at the deadline: kept/broken/`void`);
2. **routine** (R10);
3. **requests fallback** (R5);
4. **false reports** (R6/R8);
5. **favour kindnesses** (R10);
6. **unfairness** (R16);
7. **handovers** (calendar bookings for tomorrow, expiry, R17);
8. **new practices**: per live holder, at most one request a day: chance `p = base[frame] × frequency[setting] × assertiveness().demandMult × want[band] × appetite` (`TUNING.practice.frequency`: base play 0.15 / role 0.25 / rule 0.35; want calm 0.8 / restless 1 / aching 1.3 / desperate 1.5; appetite = the highest `as` among her practice categories: craves 1.4 / enjoys 1 / tolerates 0.6; all `AUTHOR TO TUNE`), hit when `practiceRoll(holderId, 'new') < p`. The kind: by `TUNING.practice.kindWeights` (task 6, protocol 2, denial 2, hold 1, demonstration 1) over the kinds she may issue today (R9 gate for demonstration, `denial` enabled and she has `as ≠ none` for it, `restraint` for hold, `service` for protocol, the task cap), picked by `practiceRoll(holderId, 'kind')`;
9. **expiry** of restrictions, cold shoulders, permissions, handovers and closed-practice trimming.
A day step for nobody live returns `[]` and writes nothing.

### R8. Her discipline (spec §4, §R.2 narrated, P15 hand-off)

- **Offences** come from: `task_broken`; a P9d `rule_broken` of a rule she proposed (P9d or practice protocol/denial); `protocol_slip` (P15 intent) on her; `denial_broken`; a refusal at frame ≥ `role` (P15's `refusal_noted` with `what ∈ {claim, demand, defy}`, and `milestone_refused`); an overruled countermand (her override answered DEFY: P15's `overrode_her` from the override path); `physics_slip` / `physics_caught` in a run with her (P15a); a posture slip (R14); a false report found; the commander asking (R15); unfairness `no_guilt` (R16); defying her hold (R12). `noteOffence(draft, ctx, holderId, offence)` runs at most once per holder per offence `ref` and day, and only while she is live.
- **Severity** (`severityOf`): stakes rank + 1 (trivial 1 … severe 4) + style (`cruel` +1, `affectionate` −1, primary only; the secondary adds `TUNING.practice.severity.secondaryStyle` (0.5) with its sign; the terms are summed as a float and rounded once at the end, half up) + frame (`play` −1, `role` 0, `rule` +1) + repeat (+1 when she has ≥ 2 offences in 14 days), clamped 1–5. A pending kindness `softer` lowers it by 1 once.
- **The entry:** `pickHolderPunishment(state, reg, holderId, severity)`: the entries of that severity that pass `contentAllowed` (her limits) and whose effects are possible now (`slot_held` not while a run, a fight or a scene with another; `confession` needs another living named unit at base; a public entry needs R8 public gate); several take turns by her punishment count (no draw); none → the next lower severity; none at all → no punishment (the offence still costs its hold through its own event).
- **Opening:** a request of kind `punishment` (R5) carrying the offence; `issuePractice` writes the `OpenPunishment` (`offered`) and emits **`punishment_issued`** (a threat source, R18). The card: `[ ACCEPT ] [ NEGOTIATE ] [ REFUSE ]`.
- **ACCEPT** (`practice/answer_punishment { punishmentId, answer: 'accept' }`): the entry's effects run (below); the engine relation event `punishment_accepted` lands on her through `applyEventToUnit` at the severity of the punishment (1–2 `minor`, 3 `moderate`, 4 `major`, 5 `extreme`): its `eventDeltas` row gives back part of what an offence costs (the repair, about half the `task_broken` row: R19) and P15's feed adds hold +4; `addTrackXp('discipline', TUNING.practice.xp.discipline × severity)`; sample `yielded`. **`physical_discipline_shown`** decides whether the directive shows the execution or says it is only reported.
- **NEGOTIATE** opens a scene with her (as TALK); `compromise_reached { punishmentId }` lowers severity by 1 and re-picks the entry; the card comes back `offered`.
- **REFUSE:** `refusalCost(draft, ctx, holderId, 'defy')` (P15: hold, `refusals`, the contest at `rule`), her Impunity record `addRecord(holderId, TUNING.practice.refuseRecord (5), 'practice_refused')`, sample `resisted`; **she may escalate once**: when the punishment was not already escalated and her style is not `affectionate`, a new request with severity + 1 and `escalated: true`; an escalated punishment refused ends there.
- **Effects** (`runPunishmentEffect`): `slot_held` → a hold (R11) of 1 / 2 slots or the rest of the day; `privilege_revoked` → `practices.restrictions` (read by the two hooks below); `protocol_extra` → a protocol request (R10) with `noDuration` false and days; `cold_shoulder` → `practices.cold[holderId] = day + days` (she starts no DM, her replies are none (the `dm_reply` is skipped by the controller), a scene with her is refused by the unit-restriction hook; both Wants rise: `raiseWant(holderId, TUNING.practice.coldWant)` a day); `confession` → a task (`model` check, `targetUnitId` the chosen unit (the first eligible by squad order), tags `public_humiliation`, stakes the punishment's); `mark` → P9c `personal.mark = { unitId: holderId, untilDay }` when none is set (`mark_set`), else nothing; `stress` → `addCommanderStress(amount, 'holder_punishment')`; `injury_chance` → chance `chance − perEndurance × body.endurance − perTrained × commanderSkill(state, <any trained physics>, max)` (both routes), `practiceRoll` < chance → `injureCommander(days)`; `physical` → the narrated execution (directive only) and a threat; `slap` → a slap (R13).
- **Public execution** needs `public_humiliation` enabled **and** her `audience ≠ private_only`: the punishment is executed in a group scene (her, the commander, up to two of the R9 audience) and the audience reacts as in a demonstration (R9); `addSign('demonstration')` for each.
- **Restriction hooks** (lower layers import nothing of P16):
  - `registerCommanderRestriction(fn)` in `warden/punish.ts`: `commanderRestriction(state, use, activityId?, toUnitId?)` asks the warden first, then each registered reader; `dm/send` passes `toUnitId`. P16's reader: `no_dms_others` blocks a DM to anyone but her; `no_free_activities` blocks a companion activity except the ones in `NOT_LEAVE`; `room` (arg a scene location id) blocks a scene at that location; a cold shoulder blocks a DM to her ("She is not answering.").
  - `registerRestrictionReader(fn)` in `discipline/restrictions.ts`: `restrictionReason(state, unitId, use)` asks the registered readers after its own; P16's reader refuses `use === 'scene'` for a holder in her cold shoulder.
- **TAKE IT INSTEAD (inverse scapegoat):** a holder with `countermand` may order a P9 punishment of a soldier through the commander: the day step's R7 slot "new practices" may (instead of a request) open `OpenPunishment` with `forUnitId` set and `entryId` a P9 `punishments` id (the lowest-severity one allowed, by severity order), only when the soldier has a P9 offence pending (`state.discipline.defiance[soldier]` held, or a `refused` order on record this week). Card: `[ DO IT ]` (runs `applyPunishment(… source: 'menu', channel: 'pda')` on the soldier: the P9 path, UNDO as P9), `[ TAKE IT INSTEAD ]` (the soldier is spared: a new engine relation event `spared_for` lands on her (its row: Trust +5 at every severity, `AUTHOR TO TUNE`) and the P9 deed `protected` is counted; the commander receives a `holderPunishments` entry of equal severity (`pickHolderPunishment`), each named unit at base `addRecord(+TUNING.practice.takeInstead.record (2), 'took_it')`, `punishment_taken_for`), `[ REFUSE ]` (a refusal of her order: `overrode_her` minor through `hitHer`).
- **UNDO (spec §4, P9's rule):** an ACCEPT or a REFUSE answered in a scene that is still open, or today in a DM, can be undone (`practice/undo { punishmentId }`). The answer is run inside `captureInverse(draft, holderId, …)`; P16 adds `Inverse.practice?: PracticeUndo` (`{ punishmentId; status; hold: number; favour: number; tracks: Record<string, TrackState>; restrictions: number; cold?: number; heldBy?: string; escalationRequestId?: string }`) and `registerPracticeUndone(fn)` beside `registerUndone` in `discipline/record.ts` (same pattern: record.ts imports only the type). `undo.ts` exports `revertInverse` for the axes part. Hold is restored by **`restoreHold(draft, unitId, value)`** in `dynamic/hold.ts` (the one writer besides `addHold`, used only by UNDO; documented there). `canUndoPractice` refuses when the answer led to a `frame_changed`, a `milestone_offered`, an injury or a run, or a slot was spent since.
- **P15's directive** "she will answer it" for `refusal_noted` stays; the punishment card is the answer.

### R9. Demonstrations (spec §5)

- **Gate** (`demonstrationGate(state, reg, holderId)`): her `audience ≠ private_only` and `≠ indifferent`; frame ≥ `play` (`role`+ for a tier above 2); `public_humiliation` for anything humbling (a seed or output tagged with it) — a demonstration tagged only `service` needs `service`.
- **Audience** (`pickAudience`): `select_few` → the leader (P9b `leaderOf`) and the units close to the commander (`isClose` from P15: Trust or Affinity toward the commander ≥ 50), at base, living, named, not her; `show_off` → the units whose whereabout at the stage slot is the stage room (the mess at `evening`: `whereaboutAt`), any; at most 2 on cards (squad order), the rest narrated. `indifferent` → no demonstration; instead, when one of her `in_person` tasks is kept in a group scene, each other participant gets `practiceRoll(observer, 'seen:'+taskId) < TUNING.practice.demonstration.seenByChance` (0.2) → `addSign('step_behind')`.
- **Runs as** a group scene: the card `[ GO ] [ REFUSE ]` (REFUSE = a task refusal at its stakes); GO opens the scene (origin `event`, location: the mess hall in the evening, else her quarters; participants her + the audience on cards, `consumesSlot: true`). The directive `practice:demonstration:<holderId>` carries the form (summary), the audience's witness words and, when her `talkativeness ≠ 'silent'`, "others address her; she answers for the commander" (R.7).
- **Serving several (spec §6):** two holders' holds or bookings in one slot are P9c's booking conflict (R11). When a `scope: dynamic`, `actor: commander` service activity for one live holder runs in a scene where another live holder is present, the other is treated as a demonstration audience of one: `knowInstantly` (or `reactToDynamic` again) with her own witness attitude, so a `possessive` / `cruel` rival competes as P15 R14 says.
- **At scene end** (an after-action on `scene_ended` for the scene that holds an open demonstration): for each audience member, on card or narrated present at the stage: `knowInstantly(observer, holderId, 'demonstration')`; an observer who already knew gets `reactToDynamic` directly (export it if it is not). Outcomes: `addHold(holderId, TUNING.practice.demonstration.hold (3), 'demonstration')`, `addTrackXp` (`public_humiliation` if tagged, else `service`; `TUNING.practice.xp.demonstration` 6), `relieveWant` by appetite (craves 20 / enjoys 10 / tolerates 5), `addCompromat(holderId, summary, audience)`, `countDeed('demonstrated')`, `demonstration_staged` then one `demonstration_seen { observerId }` each. **Outside (B4):** only a demonstration tagged `public_humiliation` cancels a P9b feat suppression (`hierarchy.outsideSuppressedUntil = day` when set) and its memory rule spreads `slow` (R20).

### R10. Service, verdicts, favour, anticipation (spec §6, depth revision, §R.5)

- **Protocol:** a request of kind `protocol` becomes a P9d rule proposal of a template tagged `service` (`proposeRuleHashed(…, { practice: true })` with a roll from `practiceRoll`, filtered to `service` templates the holder may propose: `relation_rules` capability at ≥ `play`, P15), worded by the output (`rewordRule(ruleId, text)`), delivered as P9d does (DM card). At `role` the stakes ceiling is higher and up to `TUNING.practice.protocolMax[frame]` (1 / 2 / 3) service rules may be active. Kept/broken: P9d `rule_kept` / `rule_broken` and P15 `protocol_slip`; a broken one is an offence `rule_broken` (stakes `minor`).
- **Routine:** a holder with `household_protocol` (P15 capability) and `service` ≥ `enjoys` gets **one** routine task (`routine: true`, `stakes: 'trivial'`, `check: 'model'`, no deadline, costs no slot) from her first protocol request. The day step counts it **kept** each day (`routine_kept`, `addTrackXp('service', 1)`) unless `task_broken { taskId }` was reported that day. `[ I DIDN'T ]` (`practice/confess { taskId }`) breaks it for today with an offence of stakes `trivial` and severity − 1. Routine needs no other player action (spec's open decision: draft "none").
- **Her verdict:** at most one per holder per scene (`verdictScene`). Engine verdict first: when a P15a pair "her activity + the commander's activity" with `judged: true` ran in the scene (`scene.blocking` for the commander had a `scope: dynamic` `actor: commander` activity whose `fits` covers her activity's `pairTags`), `verdictOf(state, reg, holderId, scene)` = `pleased` when `fits` matched and `presence` matched her expectation profile's wish (`expectationsOf`), `adequate` when one of the two, `displeased` when neither. Otherwise the model's `service_rated { verdict }` (offered while she holds `service` ≥ `tolerates` and a service activity or chip ran in the scene). The relation event `service_rated` carries `kind: <verdict>` (P15 feed reads `kind: 'pleased'` → +2: the encoding stays). Favour: `TUNING.practice.verdict.favour` (pleased +2, adequate 0, displeased −2); `pleased` → `regardRise('pleased_high')` when favour is ≥ 10 after it (P15 reserved).
- **Moment gaps → favour (P15a hand-off):** an after-action on `moment_answered` from a holder's moment: matched +2, partial 0, opposite −3 (`TUNING.practice.momentGap`).
- **Favour kindnesses / refusals:** day step R7.5: at favour ≥ `kindnessAt` (8), `practiceRoll(holderId,'kind') < 0.25` → one kindness by her style (closed list, by `practiceRoll`): `slot_back` (her next hold or booking is cancelled with no cost), `protocol_relaxed` (one active service rule `suspended` for a day), `want_relief` (her Want −15 (she is pleased)), `gift` (P9c `giveToken` when she has none given), `softer` (her next punishment −1); emits `favour_kindness { unitId, kind }` (a DM line through the controller). At favour ≤ `refusalAt` (−8) her answers in R13/R15 read one step harsher.
- **Anticipation (R.5):** when the commander's blocking activity is set (`blocking_changed` of an `actor: commander`, `scope: dynamic` activity) while her current activity's `pairTags` are covered by its `fits`, and no open task or permission of hers asks for it, the action is **anticipated**: the scene's verdict for her rises one step (stored on `verdictScene` as a bonus), favour +1, `regardRise('value', 1)`, her reading `updateReading` with motive `her` (`yielded`, weight 1); event `practice_anticipated { unitId }`. Read only from the blocking, never from the model.

### R11. Holding (spec §8, depth revision)

- **Starting a hold:** a request of kind `hold`, a `slot_held` effect, or an accepted hold task. `startHold(draft, ctx, holderId, span: 1 | 2 | 'day', opts: { sensory?: boolean; hidden?: boolean })` books one `held` booking per slot (`book(… kindId: <the held kind>, source: 'practice')`; `BookingSource` gains `'practice'`, `Booking.practiceId`) from the next free slot today (never `night` unless extended), writes `practices.held`, emits `hold_started`. Another holder's booking in those slots gets P9c's `conflict` (and `resolveConflict` decides); any other booking of the commander's in those slots lapses (`booking_lapsed`, P9c consequences). Only one hold at a time: a second hold is refused (the request falls back to a task).
- **While held** (`heldNow(state): HeldState | null` for the current day and slot):
  - the dispatch guard **`held`** (inline in `dispatch.ts` after `booking_due`, from `heldGuard(state, action)`) refuses `activity/*` (except `scope: dynamic` service activities with her), `life/time_together`, `scene/start` without her (with her: only at `quarters_unit`), `intrigue/act`, `expedition/start` (→ an alarm, R12), `night/open` (→ an alarm); it allows `calendar/consume_slot` (time passes held), DMs and the actions of the hold itself;
  - **whereabouts:** `roomOfPerson(…, 'commander')` returns her cabin (`cabinOf`) — a derived reason, nothing stored; `hub.ts` `place` puts the holder in her quarters for the held slots (reason `holding`, added to `Whereabout['reason']`, after `duty`);
  - **sensory** (`sensory: true`): the device dims (`sensoryNow(state, reg)` = a hold with `sensory` **or** a commander blocking whose `filter` effect is `sensory_dark`); `visualFlags(s, media, { sensory })` gains `sensory`; the shell sets `alius-sensory` on the root: no portraits, no header, sound at `TUNING.practice.hold.sensoryVolume`, lines of text only. At the hold scene start a `sensory` hold sets the commander's worn `eyes` slot to the first `blockingItems` entry with a `filter sensory_dark` effect that `entryAllowed` allows (depth revision: the hold becomes P15a blocking), and removes it at the end;
  - `[ ASK TO BE LET GO ]` (`practice/ask_release`): her answer by style and frame (`releaseAnswer`, no draw beyond `practiceRoll`): `affectionate` / `playful` at favour ≥ 0 release; `ritual` / `drill` release only at the named end; `cruel` / `possessive` / `vindictive` extend by one slot at Impunity ≥ 60 (`hold_extended { reason: 'asked' }`), else refuse; `cold` refuses in silence; others refuse.
  - **"Forgot":** when the hold's last slot ends, `practiceRoll(holderId, 'forgot:'+day) < TUNING.practice.hold.forgot` (`cruel` 0.3, Impunity ≥ 60 +0.2) extends it by one slot (`hold_extended { reason: 'forgot' }`; never past the evening unless the slot is already evening, then into `night`). P17's visitor is not built.
- **Ending:** at the end of its last slot (`slot_advanced` / `day_started`), when she leaves service, is injured, goes on a run, stops being live, when `restraint` is switched off, or by her answer: `hold_released { reason }`. A hold that ran to its end with no defiance: relation event `staging_held` is **not** used (P17); instead `addTrackXp('restraint', TUNING.practice.xp.hold × slots)` (this makes P15's `first_night_held` reachable) and `addHold(+TUNING.practice.hold.gain, 'hold')`.

### R12. The alarm: duty against the hold, and the run without the commander (spec §8 B5, user)

- **Alarms** (`ALARM_KINDS`): `launch` (the player dispatches `expedition/start` while held: the guard refuses with code `held_alarm` and the UI dispatches `practice/raise_alarm { kind: 'launch', payload }`), `deadline` (at hold start and at each slot while held, when an offered mission's `deadlineDay ≤ day`: raised by the engine, once per mission), `night` (a queued night entry while held into the night). One alarm open at a time (`practices.alarm`).
- **Her answer** (`alarmAnswer(state, reg, holderId, kind)`, deterministic): score = `values.mercy × 1 + values.corporate × 0.5` (`valuesOf`) + style (`possessive` −1, `cruel` −1.5, `affectionate` +1, `drill` +0.5) + frame (`play` 0, `role` −0.5, `rule` −1) + `practiceRoll(holderId,'alarm:'+id) − 0.5`; ≥ `TUNING.practice.alarm.release` (0.5) → `release`; ≥ `late` (−0.5) → `release_late`; else `keep`. A holder **in the party** of the launch always answers `release`. Emits `hold_alarm { alarmId, kind, answer }`.
- **release:** the hold ends (`hold_released { reason: 'alarm' }`); hold −`TUNING.practice.alarm.releaseHold` (2); the squad never knew; the UI re-dispatches the original action.
- **release_late:** the hold ends after one more slot is spent (`consumeSlots(1)` when slots remain; a launch that is then too late fails its own departure check, and the card says so); each named unit at base `addRecord(+TUNING.practice.alarm.lateRecord (3), 'waited')`; a fact for the party (memory rule `held_alarm_late`, R20).
