# P14c Her Life Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The women get a life that does not revolve around the commander:
- a **temperament** (talkativeness, volatility, humor) that every 0.2 package reads;
- **days off-screen**: hash-picked life events that become hidden facts and reach the player through a paced **news queue** (a DM, news she brings to her next scene, or a hub line);
- **hobbies** as a cycle (aptitude, practice from where she spends her slots, plateaus, interest, cooling, dropping, taking up again, traces);
- **opinions** from a topic library, formed lazily, voiced once when something brings them up, moved one step by persuasion;
- **quiet meetings** (`time_together` with a pastime), first moments and running jokes.

All of it follows the **prompt-hygiene rule** (spec §0): triggered, once, taken out when voiced, with cooldowns.

**Architecture:**
- **Engine** (`src/engine/life/`): pure and deterministic, with **no rng draw** (every chance and pick is `fnv1a` over a key salted with `life.salt`).
  - `state.ts` is a leaf: types, tuning reads, `lifeOf`, `ensureLife`, `lifeMode`, `temperamentOf`.
  - The modules are `events.ts` (pick and requires), `hobbies.ts` (practice, skill, interest), `news.ts` (queue and delivery), `opinions.ts`, `meetings.ts` (pastimes, firsts), `jokes.ts` and `intents.ts` (routing of the seven model-reportable types).
  - One day step on the `dm_rumors` hook, after P14b's.
  - Actions live in `src/engine/actions/life.ts` (`life/*`, `profile/*`).
- **Model side** (`src/life/controller.ts`, chunk b–c):
  - flavor kinds `life_share` and `life_trace` (the DM and the note text);
  - one new mechanic call type `opinion_form` (a lazy opinion);
  - derived `life:` directives refreshed in `applyContext` like P9c's.

  Every call goes through `app.generations`. Every fallback is the registry's own line, picked by hash.
- **UI** (chunk d):
  - TEMPERAMENT, HOBBIES and OPINIONS editing in the wizard calibration and the dossier;
  - THOUGHTS and HOBBIES views in the dossier;
  - the commander's hobby skills on the CMDR card;
  - a hub line under her marker;
  - the pastime picker for `time_together` and the in-scene "LET'S …" menu;
  - the SYS › World frequency;
  - analyze providers that read through `Knowledge.life`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 5 (strict), esbuild, zod 3, vitest (+ jsdom for UI). No new dependencies.

## Global Constraints

- **Off is byte-identical.** The setting `state.settings.lifeEvents` is `'off' | 'rare' | 'normal' | 'often'`; absent or invalid means **off** (`lifeMode`). With it off:
  - no day step runs, nothing is picked, practised, queued, formed or offered;
  - `intent_extract` gets no `life` input, so its prompt is byte-identical (`tests/fixtures/intent_extract/prompt_without_reading.txt`);
  - no `life:` directive exists and `whereabouts` is unchanged;
  - `tests/warden/unaffected.test.ts`, `tests/special/unaffected.test.ts`, `tests/development/unaffected.test.ts`, every user of `tests/helpers/unaffectedWeek.ts` and `tests/engine/setup.test.ts` stay unchanged. Their campaigns carry no setting, no temperament, no hobbies and no opinions.

  A seeded test that moves **stops and reports**; it does not get a new golden.
- **New campaigns.** `SetupResult.lifeEvents?` is written into `settings` only when present, and the wizard sends the player's choice (default `normal`). Old saves stay off until the player picks a frequency in SYS › World (`life/set_frequency`).
- **No rng draw, ever.**
  - Every chance is `lifeChance(life, key, p)` = `fnv1a(\`${life.salt}|${key}\`) % 1000 < round(p × 1000)`; every pick is `fnv1a(...) % n` over a list in registry order.
  - `life.salt` is the setup `seed` when the wizard turns life on, else `fnv1a(JSON.stringify(state.rng))` at `life/set_frequency`. Both are deterministic, and neither reads `ctx.rng`.
  - With life `normal`, a seeded week ends with the same `state.rng` as with it off (Task 20 pins it).
- **Old saves.** Every new field is optional: `Unit.temperament`, `.temperamentLocks`, `.hobbies`, `.aptitudes`, `.opinions`, `Commander.hobbySkill`, `CampaignState.life`, `settings.lifeEvents`. There is no migration and the version is unchanged. Every reader of temperament goes through `temperamentOf` (defaults `terse` / `steady` / `dry`); a guard test refuses `.temperament` reads elsewhere.
- **Grunts have none of it** (no temperament, hobbies, opinions or life events). Only `kind === 'named'` living units take part.
- **Prompt hygiene (spec §0).**
  - News, opinions, jokes and pastimes reach the main model only as **derived** `life:` directives, set when a trigger holds and worded "if it fits, … once".
  - A directive is gone after `intent_extract` reports it voiced (`news_told`, `opinion_voiced`, `joke_returned`), after its scene, or after its time.
  - A life fact the commander has not been told is **not** retrieved into the prompt (tag `untold`, skipped by the facts provider). The only standing addition is the temperament words in her line of the `units` block (spec §9).
- **No pronoun for the commander** in any text the player or the main model reads: registry `description` / `hub` / `lines` / `opening` / `line` / `traceText`, glossary, directives, call prompts, toasts and labels (lore §0; `tests/content/pronouns.test.ts`). The soldiers are "she".
- **Read by field or type, never by id:**
  - life events by `requires` / `share` / `size` / `category` / `becomes`;
  - hobbies by `locations` / `teachable` / `milestones[].trace`;
  - topics by `group` / `axis` / `triggers`;
  - pastimes by `locations` / `slots` / `requires` / `talk`;
  - rooms by `sceneLocationId`;
  - wager games by `kind`, defects by `category`, relationship states by `bondTier`.

  Code never names a life event, hobby, topic or pastime id. The one exception is the pastime the `her_hobby` flag marks, which is read by the field `hobby: true`.
- **Ids.**

  1. Bookkeeping: record new deaths into `life.deaths` (a unit whose `status` is `dead` and not yet recorded, with `grunt` from its kind).
  2. **Weekly cap.** Per woman per 7-day week (`week = floor(day / 7)`): rare 0.5 (one event every other week, `(week + hash(unit)) % 2 === 0`), normal 1, often 2. At most one `notable` a week, at most one event a day.
  3. **Today's chance.** `lifeChance(\`${unitId}|life|${day}\`, perWeek / 7 × TUNING.life.dayBoost)` (dayBoost 1.4, so the weekly cap is usually reached).
  4. **The pool.** The entries whose `requires` hold, filtered by cooldowns:
     - her own last day of this kind + `cooldownDays` (small 21, notable 45; `yearly` 360);
     - the same kind for any woman within `TUNING.life.squadKindDays` (5) is out;
     - a `yearly` event fires only when `hash(unit|vat) % 360 === day % 360`.
  5. **Pick and record.** The pick is `fnv1a(\`${salt}|${unitId}|life|${day}\`) % pool.length` over the pool in registry order. It writes the fact, sets the hub line (`life.units[id].hub = { eventId, day }`, not for `keeps`) and applies `becomes`. It emits `life_event { unitId, eventId, size }`.

The chance is `talkative` 0.7 / `terse` 0.4 / `silent` 0.15, × (Affinity ≥ 40 ? 1 : 0.5). A hit enqueues the item (`news_queued`) and emits `life_shared`.
- **Bookkeeping hooks.**

  - `scene/start` adds the participants.
  - `busy` keeps the last 2 days.
  - `upkeep` with `supply_shortage` sets `life.shortageDay`.

**State:**

```ts
interface HobbyState {
  id: string; skill: number /* 0..100 */; interest: number /* 0..100 */; state: 'active' | 'cooling' | 'dropped';
  since: number; lastPracticeDay?: number; coolingSince?: number;
  plateau?: { at: number; since: number }; milestones: string[]; ceiling?: true; mastered?: true;
}
// Unit
hobbies?: HobbyState[];
aptitudes?: { high: string[]; low: string[] };
```

**Rules** (TUNING values from the spec table):
- `milestoneAt(salt, unitId, hobbyId, n)` = `[10, 30, 60, 100][n]` × (1 + ((`fnv1a(salt|unit|hobby|id|n)` % 301) − 150) / 1000), rounded. The fourth milestone stays ≤ 100.
- **Practice gain** = 1 × aptitude (low 0.5, standard 1, high 1.6) × min(interest / 60, 1.3). A `her_hobby` meeting is one practice and a push.
- **Plateau.** It starts when skill reaches `prev + 0.7 × (next − prev)`. Skill then stops until a **breakthrough**: each practice slot on the plateau rolls `lifeChance(unit|hobby|id|day|slot, 0.06 × aptitude)`, or a push breaks it at once. Pushes are a `her_hobby` meeting, a `becomes { hobby: id }` event, a shared practice slot with another woman who has the same active hobby in the same room, and `hobby_praised`. A breakthrough sets skill to the milestone and grants it.
- **Ceiling.** Low aptitude stops at the third milestone (`ceiling`, interest −10 once, −20 for `moody`).
- **Interest** (a weekly tick when `(day − since) % 7 === 6`):
  - novelty +3 for 3 weeks; plateau −4; stress ≥ 50 −3; a newer active hobby −2;
  - events: milestone +15, push or praise +8, sharing +5 (a delivered or told item, a meeting).
  - `< 25` → `cooling` (`coolingSince`); cooling for 30 days → `dropped`; `≥ 40` again → `active` (`hobby_rekindled`).
- **At most three `active`.** A fourth pushes the lowest-interest one to `cooling`.
- **New hobbies:**
  - an event whose `becomes.hobby` she lacks;
  - a friend: a shared routine slot in a room where the other woman practised a hobby she lacks, `lifeChance(0.02)`;
  - a quiet meeting where the commander shows one is out of scope: the commander has no hobbies to show.

  A new hobby starts at skill 0 with interest 50 (novelty).
- **Decay.** A `dropped` hobby loses 1 a week, never below its last milestone.
- **Mastery.** The fourth milestone sets `mastered`, writes a fact "the base's {label} hand" known to the squad (`knownBy` all named living, not hidden), and emits `hobby_mastered`. When development is not off it also writes a `DevFact` (`source: 'life'`, `kind: 'trait'`). Mastery does not stop the cycle.
- **Starting skill** at import: `fnv1a(salt|unit|hobby|id) % (milestoneAt(1) + 1)`, inside the first two steps, with milestones already passed granted silently (no fact, no queue).
- **Commander skill.** `Commander.hobbySkill?: Record<hobbyId, number>` (0..100). A `her_hobby` meeting adds 2, or 4 when the hobby is `teachable` and her skill ≥ her second milestone ("she teaches"). The first teaching emits `hobby_taught` (rule `taught_commander`). It is shown only as a descriptor: `<10 none`, `<30 a beginner`, `<60 getting there`, `≥60 good at it`. It has no mechanical value.

### The news queue (spec §3.6)

```ts
interface NewsItem {
  id: string /* ln<n> */; unitId: string; kind: 'event' | 'hobby';
  ref: string /* life event id or hobby id */; what: 'event' | 'milestone' | 'breakthrough' | 'started' | 'dropped' | 'mastered';
  milestoneId?: string; factId?: string; category: string; day: number; notable?: true; urgent?: true; caused?: true;
}
interface LifeState {
  salt: number; nextId: number;
  units: Record<string, LifeUnit>;
  queue: NewsItem[];
  delivered: Array<{ day: number; unitId: string; category: string; hobby: boolean; caused?: true }>; // 14 days
  outbox: Array<{ itemId: string; unitId: string; channel: 'dm' | 'note'; day: number }>;
  news: Array<{ itemId: string; unitId: string; until: number; passing?: true }>; // pending news directives
  kinds: Array<{ kind: string; unitId: string; day: number }>; // 7 days, the squad-wide kind gap
  deaths: Array<{ unitId: string; day: number; grunt: boolean }>;
  busy: Array<{ unitId: string; slot: number; activityId?: string }>;
  shortageDay?: number;
  pending?: { unitId: string; pastimeId: string; locationId: string; day: number; slot: Slot }; // a `time_together` waiting for its scene
}
interface LifeUnit {
  last: Record<string, number>; // event id → day
  week?: { n: number; events: number; notable: number };
  hub?: { eventId: string; day: number };
  firsts?: string[];
  jokes?: Record<string, { count: number; last: number }>;
  jokeDay?: number; meetingDay?: number; taught?: string[];
  scene?: { sceneId: string; topics: string[]; topic?: string; joke?: string }; // the open scene's triggers and picks
  pendingTopics?: string[]; // topics opened by an event or a trace, waiting for `opinion.form`
}
```

**Delivery** (the day step, after the picks):
- **Caps.** At most `perDay` items squad-wide (rare 1 every 2 days on odd days, normal 1, often 2), at most `hobbyWeek` (2) hobby items in the last 7 days of `delivered` (player-caused breakthroughs count), and no woman twice within 2 days.
- **Variety.** The category of the last delivered item is never repeated next. If the only candidates share it, the day is skipped.
- **Order.** `notable`, then the oldest `day`, then the category least recently delivered, then `fnv1a(salt|id)`.
- **Urgent items** (`urgent: true` events: `grunt_lost`, `anniversary_of_loss`) bypass the caps on their day but still count.
- **Staleness.** An item older than 7 days is never delivered as news. It becomes a **passing mention**: a `news` entry with `passing: true` until her next scene ends, else 3 days, then dropped (`news_stale`).
- **(user) Channel.**
  - `dm` when the global setting `unitDms` is on (mirrored into `settings.unitDms` by the app, as `beatCooldownDays` is) and either she is `talkative`, or Affinity ≥ 40 and `lifeChance(item|dm, 0.5)` passes;
  - else a **news directive** (`news` entry, until her next scene ends, else 3 days);
  - a hobby milestone whose trace is `note` → `note` (an outbox entry; the controller words it);
  - everything else follows the first two rules.

  Delivery emits `news_delivered { itemId, unitId, channel }`.
- **Revealing.** A DM or note reveals the fact when the controller dispatches `life/share_done` or `life/trace_note`; a directive reveals it at `news_told`. Revealing deletes `hiddenFromCommander` and the `untold` tag and emits `life_noted`.
- **Outside the queue:** hub lines (passive), player-caused breakthroughs (told in the scene: the meeting's directive carries it; `delivered` gets a `caused` row) and urgent events.

### Opinions (spec §4)

Registry `opinionTopics` (65 topics, 302 positions from `life.md` §1):

```
{ id, label, group: 'lore' | 'service' | 'everyday' | 'people' | 'abstract', axis?: { id: ValueAxis, sign: 1 | -1 },
  positions: [{ id, description, lines: [string, string] }] /* 3–5, ordered */,
  triggers: { locations?: string[]; activities?: string[]; lifeEvents?: string[]; keywords?: string[] }, description }
```

Draft `triggers` (the author has not written them; `AUTHOR TO WRITE`):
- `keywords` are the topic's label words plus 2–4 obvious synonyms;
- `locations` where obvious (`food` → `mess_hall`, `weapons` → `training_yard`, `the_squad` → none);
- `lifeEvents` are the events whose `becomes.opinionTopic` names it.

```ts
interface Opinion {
  position: string; strength: 'strong' | 'held' | 'loose'; text?: string /* ≤ 200 */; formedDay: number; voicedDay?: number;
  history?: Array<{ day: number; from: string; to: string }>;
  push?: { toward: 'first' | 'last'; amount: number };
}
// Unit
opinions?: Record<string, Opinion>;
```

- **Default position.** With `axis`, `sign × valuesOf(unit)[axis.id]` > 0 → the first position, < 0 → the last, 0 → the middle (`floor(n / 2)`). Without an axis, the middle.
- **Forming** (`life/opinion_formed { unitId, topicId, position, text, strength: 'held' | 'loose', source }`) is refused if she already has one. A model-formed opinion is `held`; a fallback is `loose` with `lines[fnv1a(salt|unit|topic) % 2]`. The engine validates the topic and the position and cuts the text to 200 characters.
- **Triggers in a scene.** `life/topics_triggered { topicIds }` records topics on `life.units[id].scene.topics` for each present woman:
  - the scene controller sends the scene's location and origin-activity topics at open;
  - keywords found in each commander line (`topicsInText(reg, text)`, whole words, case-insensitive);
  - topics of her life events of the last 7 days.

  For a topic she has no opinion on, the controller then asks `opinion.form`.
- **Voicing pick.** On the first trigger of a scene, for each present woman with a formed opinion on a triggered topic, `voicedDay` is absent or older than 21 days, and no topic is picked yet this scene, pick `topics[fnv1a(salt|sceneId|unit) % n]` and store `scene.topic`. That gives at most one topic per scene per woman.
- **`opinion_voiced { topicId }`** (only for the picked topic):
  - sets `voicedDay`, clears `scene.topic`;
  - writes the Knowledge fact "{Name}'s view on {label}: {text or position description}" (about her, `knownBy [her]`, not hidden, tags `opinion`, `<topicId>`);
  - emits `life_noted`.
- **THOUGHTS** lists opinions with `voicedDay`.
- **Persuasion.**
  - `opinion_challenged { topicId, severity, positionId? }`: a `minor` or `moderate` severity counts 1, `major` or `extreme` counts 2. The direction is toward `positionId` if it is given and differs from hers, else toward the farther end of the list (ties: the first).
  - Pressure in the other direction subtracts.
  - At `persuadeAt` (6; ×2 for `strong`) the position moves one adjacent step (`history` gets a row, `push` resets, `opinion_changed` is emitted).
  - A `strong` opinion at its threshold first rolls `lifeChance(unit|topic|harden|day, 0.5)`. A hit hardens it: the push resets, a fact "{Name} will not change her mind about {label}" is written (knownBy her, not hidden) and `opinion_hardened` is emitted. A miss moves it.
  - The challenge itself runs through `applyEventToUnit` with an `eventDeltas` row of zero deltas and an `appraisal` (`wanted: 'no', initiator: 'actor', harm: 'none', outcome: 'bad'`), so P14b records it when development is on. A `moody` woman also takes −1 Affinity on a `major` challenge.
- **`opinion_agreed { topicId }`** on a `strong` opinion applies its row (`minor { affinity: 1 }`, diminishing through `applyRelationDelta`); on any other opinion it does nothing.
- **(user default) Opinions do not drift through P14b's weekly review**: no axis kind is added. A moved opinion is shown in THOUGHTS; it is not a `DevMoment`.
