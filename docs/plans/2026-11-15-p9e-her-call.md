# P9e "Her Call" Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Sometimes, during a run, a soldier with a will of her own **acts on her own**:
- the engine picks who acts and how it turns out;
- the commander gets a notice card;
- a bad turn marks a command mission so that P9b's scapegoat case opens on her if the run later fails;
- the commander can pre-empt it for one soldier with an "ask me first" standing order, which costs Trust when given.

**Architecture:**
- **Engine.** One new pure module, `src/engine/power/herCall.ts`, owns the rules:
  - eligibility, actor weights and odds are pure and draw no rng;
  - the roll draws from the rng;
  - the consequences are applied in the same module.
- **Hook points.**
  - `src/engine/world/expedition.ts`: `enterEvent` resolves the node, `eventPool` filters the templates, and a new `acknowledgeHerCall` closes the node.
  - `src/engine/power/scapegoat.ts`: the mark gets a `source`.
  - `src/engine/actions/{expedition,power}.ts`: two new actions.
- **Data.**
  - A new optional `herCall` block on `dilemmaTemplates`.
  - Optional state fields: `ExpeditionState.herCalls`, `ExpeditionReport.herCalls`, `PendingNode.herCall`, `Mission.decidedBy.source`, `PowerState.askFirst`, `scapegoatQueue[].source`, `DilemmaState.scapegoatSource`.
  - `TUNING.herCall`.
- **Non-engine layer.** P9b's `src/power/` and `src/ui/power/`, the expedition screen and the OPS board get the notice card, the chip, the directives, the ASK ME FIRST control and the providers.
- **Chunks.** They follow `docs/ROADMAP-next.md`.
  - **Chunk (a), Tasks 1–5:** the engine.
  - **Chunk (b), Tasks 6–9:** content, UI, directives, providers and docs.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 5 (strict), esbuild, zod 3, vitest (+ jsdom for UI). No new dependencies.

## Global Constraints

- **English and safe text.** Everything is in English. UI text goes through `h()` / `textContent`, never `innerHTML`.
- **Engine purity.**
  - `src/engine/**` stays pure (`tests/architecture.test.ts`).
  - `herCall.ts` must not import `world/expedition.ts`. The party is passed in, so there is no import cycle.
  - `power/state.ts` stays a leaf.
- **The engine decides** (P1–P3).
  - Who acts, whether she acts and the outcome are engine computations. The player never rolls or picks; the model never decides and never writes numbers.
  - P9e adds **no new intent** and does not change the `intent_extract` input.
- **From the card, not from the world.** A unit may act only if one of these holds:
  - `leadRank(bondOf(u).leadStyle) ≥ trigger.minLeadRank`;
  - her command stance is one of `trigger.stances` **and** her Impunity is ≥ `trigger.minImpunity`.

  `squad_assertiveness` scales only the chance, through `assertiveness().privilegeMult`.
- **Read by field, never by id.**
  - Code never tests a template, stance, defect or option id.
  - Stance ids appear only as data, in `trigger.stances`.
  - Defects are read by `category`.
  - World options are read by effect type.
- **Rng discipline (spec acceptance 2 and 5).**
  - No eligible unit in the party: no draw.
  - An eligible unit at a her-call node: **one** draw (does she act).
  - She acts: **two more** (who, outcome).
- **Spec numbers (all draft):**
  - `chance` **0.35** × `privilegeMult`;
  - the squad leader's actor weight **×2**;
  - Impunity record on a good outcome **+4**;
  - **4–6** authored events;
  - every outcome `lean` axis within **−2…2**;
  - exactly **one good and one bad** outcome per template.
- **Ids.**
  - Directive ids: `power:herCall:<unitId>` and `power:ask_first:<unitId>`. They start with `power:` and never with a prefix that P9, P9d or P11 reserve.
  - Glossary ids: `her_call`, `ask_first`.
- **Text.**
  - No pronoun for the commander in any text (lore §0); `tests/content/pronouns.test.ts` must pass.
  - Every new chip, card, stat and option carries `data-analyze` and has a glossary entry or registry `description`.
  - Authored texts are drafts marked `AUTHOR TO WRITE`.
- **Old saves.**
  - Every new field is optional and the version stays 1.
  - An absent `decidedBy.source` reads as `'vote'`.
- **Build output.** `npm test` rebuilds `dist/index.js` and `style.css`; commit both with every task.

## Where the plan rules on the spec (read before Task 1)

Rulings marked **(user)** were answered by the user on 2026-11-15. Task 9 copies all of them into the spec's **Implemented as**.

- **Standing order "ask me first" (user: added in v1).** This answers the spec's open question "pre-empt".
  - **The action.** `power/ask_first { unitId, on: boolean }`, base only: the expedition guard in `dispatch` already refuses non-`expedition/` actions during a run.
  - **`on: true` (a named living unit, not already ordered).**
    - Stores `power.askFirst[unitId] = day`.
    - Applies `decision_violated` (severity `TUNING.herCall.askFirst.severity`, draft `'minor'`) through `applyEventToUnit`, confidence 1, evidence `'ordered to ask first'`. Its axes are `againstHerValues(draft, reg, unit)`, exported from `votes.ts`, exactly as an overrule applies it. Then `evaluateAutomatic`.
    - Emits `{ type: 'ask_first_set', unitId, on: true }`.
  - **`on: false`** is free: it deletes the entry and emits `{ type: 'ask_first_set', unitId, on: false }`.
  - **Errors.** Setting the order twice, or lifting one that is not set, throws `She is already under that order.` / `There is no such order.`
  - **Effect.** While the order holds she is **never eligible** for any her-call template. She draws nothing and is not in the actor weights. A party whose only eligible soldiers are under the order sees no her-call templates in its pool.
  - **Cleanup.** `forgetPower` deletes her entry.
  - **Mid-run.** The order can only be given at the base. A run that started before it was given still reads it live, at each node entry.
- **Field only (user).** Her-calls happen only at expedition event nodes. Base her-calls go to the spec's open items.
- **A good outcome is always hers (user).** No `outsideFeats` entry and no `noteFeat`; it is never credited to the commander.
- **How hard a bad outcome bites (user).** About as much as the worst option of an ordinary minor event: lost carried supplies 2–3, party stress +10, party HP −10 %. The case comes on top. Content in Task 6.
- **Which templates the pool offers (acceptance 5).**
  - `eventPool(state, reg, regionId)` keeps a her-call template only if all of these hold:
    - there is an expedition;
    - its mission's template is not `tutorial`;
    - `herCallCandidates(state, reg, herCall, ableParty(state))` is non-empty.
  - So a party with nobody eligible sees exactly P9b's pool, and `rng.pick` draws identically: the whole run, rng state included, is byte-identical to the same run with the her-call templates removed from the registries.
  - The check is deterministic and draws nothing.
- **A her-call node where she does not act.**
  - **When.** Either the chance roll fails, or the template was pinned (a fixed map's `eventId` or `objectiveEventId`) and nobody is eligible.
  - **What happens.** The node plays the template's **`fixed` block as an ordinary minor event**: the existing `expedition_event`, the commander picks.
  - **Why this works.** The loader already requires `fixed` on every minor expedition event, so the spec's "else skipped" never happens in practice.
- **Draws.** The spec's "exactly two draws when she acts, none otherwise" counts who and outcome. The chance roll is a third draw before them:
  - no eligible unit → 0 draws;
  - an eligible unit and the roll fails → 1 draw;
  - she acts → 3 draws, in the order chance, actor, outcome.
- **Actor weight.**
  - Formula: `(1 + TUNING.herCall.leadBonus[leadStyle]) × TUNING.herCall.band[impunityBand(I)] × (leader ? TUNING.herCall.leaderMult : 1)`.
  - Drafts: `leadBonus` = `{ yielding: 0, balanced: 0, assertive: 1, dominant: 2 }`; `band` = `{ cowed: 0.5, low: 0.75, high: 1, brazen: 1.5 }`; `leaderMult` 2.
  - "The leader" is `leaderOf(state)`.
  - The pick is `rng.float() × total` over the candidates in party order, like `resolveUnknownType`.
- **Outcome odds.**
  - Each outcome's score is `s(o) = Σ effectiveValues(unit)[axis] × o.lean[axis]`.
  - `odds = clamp(base + perPoint × (s(good) − s(bad)) + Σ tilt, min, max)`.
  - `Σ tilt` is the sum of `herCall.defectTilt[].shift` over her defects whose `category` matches; one tilt per entry, even if two defects share the category.
  - Drafts: base 0.5, perPoint 0.05, min 0.15, max 0.85.
  - The outcome is `good` when `rng.float() < odds`.
- **Chance.** `min(TUNING.herCall.chanceCap, TUNING.herCall.chance × assertiveness(state, reg).privilegeMult)`. Drafts: chance 0.35, cap 0.8.
- **When effects apply.**
  - **On node entry**, the moment she acts:
    - the outcome's effects (`applyWorldEffects` with source `{ kind: 'event', id: templateId, label: fixed.title }`);
    - the consequences below;
    - the log line `{Name} took the decision herself: {outcome.text}`;
    - the `herCalls` entry.
  - **The node then waits** as `pending: { kind: 'event', nodeId, templateId, herCall: { unitId, outcome } }` with phase `event`.
  - **Acknowledging.** `expedition/ack_her_call` closes it: the run travels on, and an objective node counts as done.
  - **Refusals while it waits.** `expedition/choose` throws `She has already decided: acknowledge it.` P6 `dilemma/request` for that node throws the existing `That event is not waiting on the map.`
  - **Retreat.** A retreat at the notice is allowed, like any event. The effects already happened.
- **Engine event.**
  - `{ type: 'her_call', nodeId, templateId, unitId, outcome, missionId, marked }` instead of `expedition_event`. So the app never asks the model for a generated dilemma for that node.
  - `her_call` and `ask_first_set` join `RESERVED_EVENT_TYPES`.
- **Consequences** (`TUNING.herCall`; all draft).
  - **Good:**
    - merit: push `{ day, unitId, points: meritGood }` (draft **6**, about half a fight) to `power.merit`;
    - `addRecord(+recordGood /*4*/, 'her_call')`;
    - `addFact`: `{Name} took a decision in the field herself, and it went well: {outcome.text}`, `about [unitId]`, `knownBy` the party, importance 2, tags `['her_call']`.
  - **Bad:**
    - `addRecord(+recordBad /*4*/, 'her_call')`;
    - the mark (below). No fact; the log, the report and the directive carry it.
- **The mark.**
  - **Where.** Only on a mission with `source === 'command'` whose template is not `tutorial`. A `side` / poi run is never marked.
  - **What.** `decidedBy = { source: 'her_call', unitId, nodeId, witnessIds }`. The witnesses are the other **named living party members** (the spec's draft: all of them).
  - **First mark wins.**
    - A mark that is already set (a vote's, or an earlier her-call in this run) is never overwritten.
    - `marked: false` in the event then.
- **The `decidedBy` type.** It becomes a union:
  - `{ source?: 'vote'; voteId: string; unitId: string; witnessIds?: string[] }`;
  - `{ source: 'her_call'; unitId: string; nodeId: string; witnessIds?: string[] }`.

  `markDecidedBy` (a vote) still writes no `source`; absent means a vote.
- **The case.** P9b's paths are unchanged.
  - **What opens it.** `takeScapegoatCase` (failed run) and `scapegoatAfterFailure` (expired deadline) open it from either source. `failedRunPenalty` applies to either source too: oversight punishes a failed run someone in the squad decided.
  - **What carries the source.** `takeScapegoatCase` returns `source` too. The queue entries carry `source?`. `openScapegoat(draft, ctx, { missionId, unitId, witnessIds, source? })` stores `DilemmaState.scapegoatSource = 'her_call'` (absent = vote).
  - **The text.** `openScapegoat` fills a new `{because}` placeholder from the template's `fixed.because[source]`. `scapegoat_mission` moves "{name} carried the squad's vote for it." into `fixed.because.vote` and adds `fixed.because.her_call`: "{name} made the call herself in the field." (`AUTHOR TO WRITE`). An absent `because` fills `''`.
  - **Retreat.** A retreat does not open the case. The mark stays on the mission returned to the board, as P9b's vote mark does, so an expired deadline still opens it. The next `expedition/start` clears it through `markDecidedBy`.
- **Log, report, directive.**
  - `ExpeditionState.herCalls?: Array<{ nodeId, unitId, outcome, templateId, odds }>`, copied into `ExpeditionReport.herCalls?`. `odds` is stored, so the provider explains the roll as it was.
  - The power controller sets `power:herCall:<unitId>` (`expiresAfter: 'scene'`) on `expedition_ended`, for each entry of `lastExpedition.herCalls`: `{Name} took the decision herself in the field; it went {well|badly}.` One per unit; her last call wins.
- **Ask-first directive.** Derived in `powerDirectives` while she is in the scene: `power:ask_first:<unitId>`, text `The commander has ordered {Name} to ask before she decides anything in the field.`

## Review Focus

1. **The actor dies, deserts or is terminated between the bad call and the end of the run, or the mission's deadline expires after she is gone.** In either case the case opens nothing and changes nothing. `openScapegoat` already refuses a subject who is not named and living, and the queue drops her. Test in Task 4 (`a her-call case whose subject is gone opens nothing`).
2. **Two her-call nodes in one run, or a her-call after a vote mark.** The first mark wins, the event says `marked: false`, and both entries are in `herCalls`. Test in Task 3 (`a second bad call never overwrites the first mark; a vote mark is kept`).
3. **The player retreats, or a reload happens, while the notice card is up.** The effects were applied once and never twice. After a reload the screen shows the same card from `pending.herCall`. `expedition/choose` and a P6 dilemma request are refused at that node. Tests in Task 3 (`choose is refused while a her-call waits`, `retreat at the notice keeps the effects and the mark`) and Task 7 (`the card renders from state alone`).
4. **A party whose only eligible soldier is injured, under ASK ME FIRST, or a grunt.** Nobody is eligible: no her-call template is offered and nothing is drawn. Only `ableParty` units count, and grunts have no bond. Tests in Task 2 (`grunts, injured units and ask-first units are never candidates`) and Task 5.
5. **A pre-P9e save mid-run, with a pending event and a P9b vote mark.** It loads. `decidedBy` without `source` reads as a vote. The pending event is answered as before. The scapegoat text uses `because.vote`. Test in Task 4 (`an old vote mark opens the case with the vote wording`).
