the slap · prototype

  A text scene with her. Type, send lines. At some point her hand comes up. Most of the time the commander simply takes it.

  Space (or the left button) — brace: the body tenses for a moment and lets go by itself; the peak on the impact is rare and hard. One try a slap.

  A sharp flick of the mouse sideways — turn the face away: defiance, and she sees it.

  N a slap now · H panel. Headphones help.
