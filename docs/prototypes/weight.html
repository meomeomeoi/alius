her sprite

"over": an art slot drawn from below (she stands over you). Without it the ordinary sprite rises from the bottom of the frame.

view

Hold Space (or the left mouse button): bear her. Move the mouse left and right: keep the level. Right click or Shift: shift your own weight (+strength, noisy; she may feel it). Esc: stop word. H: hide this panel.

weight · prototype

She rests her weight on you. Hold Space (or the left mouse button) to bear it; let go and you buckle. When she shifts, the whole frame tilts: move the mouse to keep the level; be level when her weight lands. Watch her shadow lean before she moves.
Shift / right click: shift your own weight for a rest — only when her screen's light says she is not watching, and listen for her tapping. Esc stop word · H panel.
