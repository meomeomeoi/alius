the hold · prototype · round 1

She has won the bout. Her arm locks round your head and your face is in her armpit. Air only comes when her arm lets it: breathe (Space nose, Shift mouth) when she eases off; every breath through the nose is her.
Fight: hold F to strain (it costs air) and let go of it when her grip slackens: she laughs to the others, resettles her arm, talks. Give in: just breathe and wait. Yield: stop fighting, go limp, and breathe her in deep at her hollow, or say it into her (type, Enter). T taps out.
What it all means depends on why you fought (the panel).
