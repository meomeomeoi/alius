A debate with her on a motion. Write your answers in the line at the bottom (Enter sends). The board on the right holds the points: hers and yours, weak, fair or strong; a point left unanswered stands, an answered one is struck, a weak answer only dents a strong point. With witnesses, the room decides by what they value; alone, the board.
