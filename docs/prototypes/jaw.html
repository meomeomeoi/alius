view

Mouse up and down: the jaw (down opens). Type with the ring gag; Enter speaks. Shift or the right button, held: ask for a rest. Esc: the stop word. H: hide this panel. Prototype only; nothing here is engine code.

jaw · prototype

The input is the mouth. Move the mouse down to open the jaw, up to close it. Her band shows on the mouth as a ghost frame; keep the jaw in it while it tires and wants to shut.
Her toes: no speech; below her band the teeth touch her.
The ring gag: type and press Enter: every letter comes out through the opening it was typed at.
Shift or the right button, held — ask for a rest · Esc stop word · H panel. Headphones help.
