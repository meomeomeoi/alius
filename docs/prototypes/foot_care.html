foot care · prototype · round 1

She sits back on her bunk and puts her bare foot in your hands, sole to you.
Massage. Hold the left button on her sole and move: circles knead, long strokes glide. Space (or the right button) presses harder, Shift lighter; the pressure stays where you put it (the bar by your thumbs). Her tempo and how firm she likes it are hers: her toes, her breath, her foot and her face show them (and, while you learn her, a dim word by your thumbs). She slows as she relaxes. A knot wants slower, firmer work.
Cream. C squeezes it into your palms (hold for more). It is cold at first. Spread it, rub it in; she will look at it in the light.
