sparring · prototype · round 1

  The training yard. She squares up in front of you.

  Her blows come from your left, your right, or straight at you. Watch her: her weight, her shoulder, the light on her side, her steps.
  In the last moment before it lands: a sharp flick of the mouse away from the blow dodges it (down for a straight one); Space blocks it on your arms (it still costs you).
  Too early and she follows you. Some of her moves are feints.

  A heavy blow you dodge leaves her open for a moment: click then for a clean hit. A click at any other time she blocks, and answers.

  Your strength runs out when you are hit; hers when you hit her. If both stand at the end, it is a draw.

  Esc stop word · H panel. Headphones help.
