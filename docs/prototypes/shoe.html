shoe service · prototype

She puts her foot forward. Her boots (or her heels) carry her day: ash, mud, oil, spores, dust.
Drag on them with the tongue: scrub or stroke to clean, along her grain; on clean leather, slow long strokes bring up a shine. A press held still: a kiss.
The mouth dries and fills with grit: Space swallows it; X or the right button spits it aside, if she is not looking.
She turns her foot, lifts the sole, changes feet. At the end she holds it to the light. Esc stop word · H panel.
