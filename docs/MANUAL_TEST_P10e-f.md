# Manual test — P10e chunk (f): the stage setup (in SillyTavern)

Play at **1920×1080**, with a campaign whose squad has at least one soldier with a real character card and expression sprites (the Expressions extension on). Keep a file manager open on `SillyTavern/data/<user>/characters/<her sprite folder>/` to see what is written. Nothing here gives the commander a pronoun. The author's art is never committed.

**Before you start.** Open SYS and scroll past `[ SETTINGS ]` to **STAGE**: each living named woman with a card is listed with her sprites (found or missing) and her slots filled (`n / 11`; `n / 12` once **Show explicit slots** is on), then the table of every woman × slot with each cell's state (`—` · `picture` · `fitted` · `zoned`). Hover any control with `?` held: the analysis tool explains it.

1. **Her page.** Click her name: her page opens over the shell. *Her sprites* lists the labels found and the ones the instruments ask for (`neutral` and the slap's faces), with what stands in for a missing one. *How she stands*: move scale, x and y and watch the live view; click her face to set the face point. *Her hand*: 48 samples at their measured size; pick one. *Her accent*: a palette colour or your own; "from the hash" clears it. SAVE: `alius/stage.json` appears in her folder. Start a scene with her: she stands as set, her cards are in the chosen hand (unless the campaign chose one for her in SQUAD) and with her accent.
2. **A woman with no sprite folder.** On a card with no expressions folder the page says so and still opens; the first picture saved creates `characters/<folder>/alius/`.
3. **Load a picture into each kind of slot.** EDIT a slot of each prototype: `over` (weight), `face_close` (slap: LOAD, SAVE, REMOVE only), `legs_seated.rest` / `legs_seated.foot_raised` / `feet_floor` / `sole_close` (her activity), `legs_astride` or the pair `legs_astride.left` + `.right` (throne; `.right` has no controls), a `shoe_close.*` and a `shoe_sole.*` (shoe). LOAD refuses a file that is not an image or is over 20 MB with a toast and sends nothing. Until SAVE the list marks the slot unsaved; SAVE writes `<slot>.<ext>` and, when there is a fit, `<slot>.fit.json`; the table's cell moves to `picture`, `fitted` or `zoned`.
4. **Fit it.** Each slot's controls are its prototype's panel (`npm run proto -- <name>` side by side): the same sliders, ranges, steps and defaults, the feet and shown pickers, `floorShade` on the seated legs at rest.
5. **Zone it.** On `sole_close` and the shoe slots, MARK ZONES: a plain click places a point zone, a press and a drag an ellipse, the wheel turns it (5° a notch, 1° with Shift), Shift draws a circle. On a shoe picture with two feet: her right foot's set first, then her left's. SAVE: the cell reads `zoned`.
6. **Check it.** CHECK opens the slot's mini-game over the shell with her real sprite and the **unsaved** draft. Play a few seconds; the stop word, Esc and CLOSE end it. The open campaign has not moved (day, slot, her relation), nothing was sent into the chat, no model was called. A slot whose partner file is missing (a shoe's close-up without its sole, one leg astride) uses the draft for both and says so in the preview's head.
7. **Replace a png with a webp.** Save `over.png` with a fit, then LOAD `over.webp` into `over` and SAVE: the folder holds `over.webp` and `over.fit.json`, no `over.png`, and the fit is kept.
8. **Hand-drop a file into `alius/`.** Copy `feet_floor.jpg` into her `alius/` folder by hand, and a file `wallpaper.png`. Reload the page (or switch chat and back): `feet_floor` reads `picture` and plays in her frame; `wallpaper.png` is ignored. Break `stage.json` by hand (delete a brace) and reload: one warning in the browser console names the file; the game and the other slots are unaffected; her look falls back to the hash.
9. **An Expressions override with a subfolder.** Set her Expressions override to `<folder>/casual` and save a slot: the file lands in `<folder>/alius/`, never `<folder>/casual/alius/`. Her expression picker does not list anything of `alius/`.
10. **Explicit slots.** With **Show explicit slots** off, `over_explicit` is neither listed nor counted; switch it on: it is listed and editable. In play it is still opened only by her campaign's `explicit_scenes` gate.
11. **Switch the chat with the sheet open** (and once with a slot's editor and once with the preview open, both with unsaved edits): everything closes at once, without asking; nothing is saved, nothing is dispatched into the new campaign. STAGE lists the new squad.
12. **The fixes in play (8b).** In a scene, end her frame's forgotten `use` with GET UP; in foot service let her verdict land at the end; in shoe service spit grit aside; in her activity let a relief be seen: each now reaches the game (before 8b they did nothing).

Also check at 1280×800 and 2560×1440 that no control of her page or a slot editor is cut off (`npm run test:layout` measures the same with placeholder art: fixtures `stage` and `stageslot`).

**Open items for the author** (the full list with the reasons is the **Open items** of **Implemented as: chunk (f)** in `docs/packages/P10e-scene-stage.md`): the faces asked for (`neutral` and the slap's only); a hand-dropped second type of one slot ignored silently; `zoned` meaning "marked" for the shoes; the draft standing in for a missing partner file in CHECK; no fit of its own for `legs_astride.right`; the size of a point zone on real art; SQUAD's hand picker not showing her setup's hand; `over_explicit`'s CHECK being the only way to see gulp until P17b.
