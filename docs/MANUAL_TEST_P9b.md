## In SillyTavern
Note the SillyTavern version and the models used at the top of your report. To set a number, SYS → *Export campaign*, edit the JSON (`power.hierarchy`, `relations.<unitId>.impunityRecord`, `resources`…) and *Import* it back.

- [ ] **The preset turns voting on.** At setup, WORLD PARAMETERS › preset *Assertive squad*: *Squad assertiveness* reads high, *Squad votes* reads on; the three other presets leave voting off. Both are locked after the start.
- [ ] **Each kind opens the panel.** With voting on: PLAN a command mission and launch it (kind *Mission*); UPGRADE a facility (*Base upgrade*); impose a mild measure from a dossier's DISCIPLINE menu (*Punishment*; the brig does not vote); with supplies short, press a RATIONS mode in BASE (*Rations*). Each opens the VOTE panel (corporate voice) with a row per voter, the commander's row, FOR/AGAINST weights and a one-line reason. A mission from a point of interest and the tutorial run do not vote.
- [ ] **CARRY OUT and ACCEPT.** A passed vote offers CARRY OUT and runs the action; closing the panel drops the vote and nothing runs. A failed vote offers ACCEPT (free) and the three overrules.
- [ ] **Overrule costs.** Overrule a failed vote by ORDER: the action runs; every soldier who voted against lost Trust and Affinity, her Impunity rose (CMDR/dossier), and in a scene with one of them the main model pushes back openly until the day ends. PUNISH needs a dissenter and a measure; LEASH is greyed with no authorization left and spends one otherwise.
- [ ] **Ballot wording.** SYS › *The mechanic model words the reasons on a squad vote* on: the lines are in character and one sentence each; off: the template lines. *Note how often the model drifts from the vote, mentions the commander with a pronoun, or invents facts.*
- [ ] **A leader appears with a dominant card.** Import a card that is clearly in charge (`LEADS: DOMINANT`) and win some fights with her; after a week the dossier shows `STANDING: …` and the SQUAD LEADER badge. A balanced or yielding squad never shows one.
- [ ] **Countermand chips.** With a leader, assign TRAIN or REST with another soldier a few times: sometimes the line `{Name} COUNTERMANDS …` with [ACCEPT] [OVERRULE] appears on the hub and in OPS, and no slot is spent. ACCEPT drops it; OVERRULE runs the order once and costs the leader Trust.
- [ ] **Endorse and challenge.** On the leader's dossier press ENDORSE: the badge reads SECOND-IN-COMMAND. CHALLENGE opens the five stats with their odds in words (the numbers with raw values on); after one, a scene with her opens and she speaks of the outcome the engine decided; CHALLENGE is greyed with the days left for two weeks.
- [ ] **CC on oversight and a feat suppresses it.** File a Leash request while a leader exists: the LOG tab's oversight entry reads `· CC: {Name}`. Win a fight with cover, or settle a decision with a social check: new requests carry no CC for 14 days.
- [ ] **Both scapegoat branches.** Vote for a command mission and let it expire or lose the run. The decision panel names RESPONSIBLE and WITNESSES and lists both answers' effects. TAKE THE BLAME: standing with the corporation falls, a Leash authorization is lost, she and the witnesses warm to the commander. SHIFT IT: she is in the brig for two days (no Impunity change), stressed, and the squad turns against the commander. With voting on, the choice goes to a vote first.
- [ ] **An old save plays as before.** Load a save from before P9b and one with voting off: no vote opens, no leader appears for modest bond profiles, rations are equal, and the day end behaves as in P9.
