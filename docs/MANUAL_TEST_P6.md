# P6 — acceptance checklist

Automated coverage per acceptance criterion (all in `npm test`), then the steps that must be done by hand inside SillyTavern.

## Automated
1. **Generated dilemmas that break a validator rule never reach the player; the fallback works with no mechanic profile:** `tests/engine/dilemma/validate.test.ts` (one crafted fixture per rule 1–6, dominance, `every fixed block of a generating core template passes rules 1 and 3…`), `effects.test.ts` (the mapping, garbage and hostile text), `flow.test.ts` (`an invalid first answer asks for a retry…`, `an invalid second answer presents the authored fallback…`, `a rejected answer never reaches dilemmaOptions`, `fallback works for every generating core template`, `present needs the open request id`, `a second choose is refused`), `tests/llm/dilemmaGenerate.test.ts` (fixtures `valid`, `fenced`, `truncated`, `invalid`, `dominated`, `no_cost`, `hostile`), `tests/loyalty/dilemmaController.test.ts` (`a failed call, a missing profile and the setting off all answer with fallback`, `a chat switch during the call…`), `tests/app.loyalty.test.ts` (`with no mechanic profile the authored fallback is presented`, `a requested dilemma is generated, presented and blocks an activity until chosen`).
2. **The same option raises one unit and lowers another according to values:** `flow.test.ts` (`the same option raises one unit and lowers another…`), `effects.test.ts` (`the same unit_event raises a unit with matching values…`), `tests/engine/world/effectsBase.test.ts` (`unit_event outside a run applies at once…`).
3. **Refusals at the specified rates in combat (seeded) and as directives in scenes:** `tests/engine/loyalty/refusal.test.ts` (`a wavering unit refuses about 15 % of 4000 seeded orders and a breaking one about 35 %`, `the higher of band and state friction wins`, scene orders), `tests/engine/combat/compel.test.ts`, `tests/loyalty/loyaltyController.test.ts` + `directives.test.ts` (exact directive texts), `tests/ui/sceneOrder.test.ts`, `tests/ui/combatCompel.test.ts`.
4. **Desertion is always telegraphed; all three responses work; deserters return:** `tests/engine/loyalty/desertion.test.ts` (`over 300 seeded days no attempt ever happens without a sign at least one day older`, talk down / let go / suppress, mission start, returns), `signs.test.ts`, `tests/ui/confrontation.test.ts`, `tests/app.loyalty.test.ts` (`a desertion night shows the confrontation, not the knock`, `reload with an open confrontation shows it again`, `a mission-start desertion opens the same panel as a modal`).
5. **The mutiny encounter triggers only under the specified conditions and resolves to each outcome:** `tests/engine/loyalty/mutiny.test.ts` (conditions incl. `300 seeded days never start a mutiny outside these conditions`, subdued / escaped / retreat / yield, `a mutiny result is resolved once`, JSON round trip), `tests/engine/combat/mutineers.test.ts`, `tests/app.loyalty.test.ts` (`fight starts a combat against her and its end resolves the mutiny once`).
6. **Leash actions apply exact costs, consume authorizations, refresh weekly, are logged; Terminate removes the unit for good:** `tests/engine/leash/weekly.test.ts`, `use.test.ts` (costs, `a request with no authorization changes nothing`, `a removed unit leaves no dangling references`, terminate), `tests/ui/leashForm.test.ts`, `tests/ui/squadLoyalty.test.ts` (oversight log), `tests/app.loyalty.test.ts` (`filing a Compel in a scene goes through the form…`, `a terminated unit is removed from the active chat members`).
7. **A defect arc flows end to end for at least 3 defects:** `tests/engine/arcs.test.ts` (`the flow works end to end for echo_bleed, imprint_fracture and hunger_loop`, availability, wants/refuses, Compel, scaling in combat), `tests/ui/squadLoyalty.test.ts` (arc paths).
8. **Tests for the validator, dominance, loyalty bands, desertion probability and Leash cost modifiers:** `validate.test.ts`, `refusal.test.ts`, `desertion.test.ts` (`desertionChance`), `use.test.ts` (`leash costs`).
9. **Review focus:** an unpayable or invalid Leash request (`use.test.ts`, `compel.test.ts` `a retried combat forgets its compels`), a late/repeated/garbage dilemma answer (`flow.test.ts`, `dilemmaController.test.ts`), a unit that leaves while things point at her (`use.test.ts`), reload or chat switch mid-confrontation (`mutiny.test.ts` JSON round trip, `app.loyalty.test.ts`), old saves and removed content (`weekly.test.ts` `a pre-P6 save is seeded once`, `flow.test.ts` `a vanished template resolves the dilemma`, `arcs.test.ts` `an arc whose defect vanished…`).

## In SillyTavern
- [ ] Load a campaign saved before P6: the PDA header shows `LEASH 3` (or the corporation's number), nothing breaks, SQUAD shows a loyalty line per unit.
- [ ] **Real `dilemma_generate`** with a mechanic profile: end four days (or start the day of the first pacing); a `DECISION` modal appears (`OVERSIGHT IS DRAFTING YOUR OPTIONS…`, then 2–3 options). SYS › debug shows the call and its log. Try several: every presented dilemma has a gain and a cost per option; a rejected answer (debug on) is listed in a toast.
- [ ] **Dilemmas off** (SYS › Decisions): the same moment shows the authored block and makes no model call.
- [ ] No time passes while a decision waits: an activity or `[END DAY]` tells you to answer it; closing the modal and trying again brings it back; a reload brings it back.
- [ ] A decision that moved named units: a toast names them and `Talk it over?` offers a free scene.
- [ ] **Beat 4 as a generated dilemma:** on the objective node of the story mission the expedition screen shows the three-way choice (generated, flags exactly one per option); picking one completes beat 5.
- [ ] **A refusal directive in the prompt:** open a scene with a wavering or breaking unit, switch `[ORDER]` on and send a command a few times; when she refuses `REFUSED` and `[ COMPEL ]` appear. Prompt Itemization (or SYS › debug › prompt) shows `… REFUSES the commander's order this scene…`; the main model plays the refusal and does not give in to persuasion.
- [ ] **Compel in a scene:** `[ COMPEL ]` opens the OVERSIGHT REQUEST form (request number, subject, remaining authorizations, a consequence in words; numbers only with SYS › Show raw values). `[ FILE REQUEST ]` shows TRANSMITTING… / OVERSIGHT REVIEW… / APPROVED over ~2 s (instant with reduced motion); the next reply is directed as compelled; `LEASH n` drops; the SQUAD LOG lists the request. `[ WITHDRAW ]` files nothing; at 0 authorizations FILE is greyed with the reason.
- [ ] **Compel in a fight:** after `order_refused` press `[C] COMPEL`; the order goes through for free, `LEASH n` in the header of the combat shows the pledged use; lose with easy retry and retry: nothing was charged; win: one authorization, Trust and Affinity are paid once.
- [ ] **A desertion night by mouse** (make a unit `breaking`, wait for the sign and the night): the confrontation replaces the knock; try each answer in separate runs — `[TALK HER DOWN — AUTHORITY/EMPATHY]` (once), `[LET HER GO]` (she leaves the squad and the group chat; a cut-in), `[SUPPRESS]` (form, Trust −25, squad stress). Reload at the night: the same panel returns.
- [ ] **A mission-start desertion:** depart with a breaking unit in the party: a modal stops the departure; nothing starts.
- [ ] **A deserter returns** after ~5 days: an ally knocks (`[TAKE HER BACK]` needs a free bed), a hostile one appears as a rumor and raises the oversight clock.
- [ ] **A mutiny fight:** a `despises` unit after a Leash use gives signs and then a night mutiny; `[FIGHT]` starts a combat against her with the commander exposed; the result resolves once (subdued: she is `resentful` and injured; lost or retreat: she escapes hostile). With every other unit injured only `[YIELD]` and `[SUPPRESS]` are offered.
- [ ] **Terminate:** after a subdued attack the SQUAD `[ LEASH ]` menu lists TERMINATE; the form, then approval or denial (a toast); an approved unit is gone from the squad and the group chat for good.
- [ ] **A defect arc from unlock to completion:** a trusted/confidant unit knocks about her defect; SQUAD › `[ DECIDE ARC ]` lists the paths (cost, `SHE WANTS THIS`, `SHE REFUSES — LEASH ONLY`, greyed with the reason); decide, visit the medbay with her as companion on the needed days; the arc status shows `ARC: MANAGE 1/1`; a Cure removes the defect; Manage halves the downside in the next fight while the upkeep is fresh.
- [ ] **Chat switch** during a dilemma call, a confrontation or a scene with directives: nothing leaks into the other campaign; the request is answered when the first chat is open again.
