# P7a — acceptance checklist

Automated coverage per acceptance criterion (all in `npm test`), then the steps that must be done by hand inside SillyTavern.

## Automated
1. **Hover, focus + `?`, and long-press open the panel; first-time vs repeat animation durations match the settings; `off` shows the text at once:** `tests/ui/analyzer.test.ts` (`hover waits the hover delay, then opens near the cursor`, `uses the hover delay setting`, `? on a focused chip opens it, F1 too`, `a long-press opens pinned and does not click`, `a tap clicks and opens nothing`, `moving 11 px cancels the long-press…`, `first analysis shows ANALYZING for 600 ms, the second for 150`, `short is 150 both times, off and reduced motion show text at once`, `disabled ignores hover, keys and long-press`), `tests/settings.test.ts` (the three settings), `tests/ui/shell.test.ts` (`the Analysis section changes the three settings`).
2. **Pin and drill-down work, with breadcrumbs and back navigation; depth capped at 3:** `analyzer.test.ts` (`moving away closes it, Shift keeps it pinned`, `the [PIN] button pins, and [PINNED] closes`, `a link drills down, auto-pins and adds a crumb`, `the fourth level is not offered`, `crumbs truncate the trail`, `Backspace and [<] go back, past the root closes`, `a click outside a pinned panel closes it`), `tests/ui/analyzePanel.test.ts` (`breadcrumbs call crumb with their index`, `no drill-down links at max depth`).
3. **`glossary:` and `<registry>:` subjects render static text and an effects summary generated from structured effects:** `tests/analysis/model.test.ts`, `tests/analysis/effects.test.ts` (every effect of the core pack summarizes to a clean line), `tests/content/glossarySchemas.test.ts`, `tests/content/glossaryCore.test.ts` (starter glossary, descriptions, causes), `tests/content/links.test.ts`.
4. **Readings for axes, stats, stress and resources show the specified data; with raw values off only bands appear:** `tests/analysis/axisReading.test.ts` (`bands only with raw values off`, `raw adds the number and the signed trend`, `trend arrows follow the ledger of the last 3 days`), `stressReading.test.ts`, `statReading.test.ts`, `resourceReading.test.ts`, `settingReading.test.ts`, `decisionReading.test.ts`, `tests/engine/ledger.test.ts`, `tests/engine/combat/statSteps.test.ts`.
5. **Hidden-state test:** `tests/analysis/hidden.test.ts` runs over every registered provider (`no provider shows a sentinel number with raw values off`, `no provider shows a sentinel text`, `every bare-source provider has a sample address`, `the harness itself notices a leaking provider`), `tests/analysis/knowledge.test.ts`, `decisionReading.test.ts` (`explains requires and check, never effects`).
6. **Coverage test on all existing main screens:** `tests/ui/analyzeCoverage.test.ts` (header, SQUAD, BASE, INV, combat, expedition node map, dilemma options, setup wizard, SYS: every marker has `data-analyze`, every address resolves, every marked element is keyboard-reachable, minimum counts).
7. **No network or LLM calls:** `tests/architecture.test.ts` (`the analysis layer and the analyze UI never reach the LLM or SillyTavern`, `the engine never reaches into the analysis layer`), `analyzeCoverage.test.ts` (`no network or LLM call happens while every marked element on every screen is analyzed`).
8. **Review focus:** bad or stale addresses (`subject.test.ts`, `model.test.ts`, `analyzer.test.ts` `a bad address shows NO DATA and does not throw`, `hidden.test.ts` `a throwing provider is skipped`); a panel that outlives its target (`analyzer.test.ts` `destroy cancels pending timers`, `refresh closes a transient panel and rebuilds a pinned one`, `tests/ui/shell.test.ts` shell wiring, `a pinned panel survives a combat re-render and rebuilds its reading`); keys that belong to someone else (`? in an input types`, `Backspace in a text field does nothing to the panel`, `Esc closes the panel and stops propagation`, `the combat screen keeps keyboard control after Esc closed a panel`, `a panel opened inside a modal … Esc closes the panel, not the modal`); numbers leaking through trends, sources or history (`hidden.test.ts`, `stressReading.test.ts` `sources group by label and hide amounts without raw`); touch (`a long-press opens pinned and does not click`, `a tap clicks and opens nothing`, `moving 11 px cancels the long-press`).

## In SillyTavern
- [ ] **Hover:** open Alius, hover `TRUST …` on a SQUAD unit: after about a quarter of a second `┌─[ ANALYSIS ]` appears next to the cursor, plays `ANALYZING ▒▒▒░░` (about 0.6 s the first time, a blink afterwards), then the text, a **CURRENT READING** block with the band and trend, and **SEE ALSO**. Move away: it closes. Near the right and bottom screen edges the panel flips to stay fully visible.
- [ ] **Pin and Shift:** hold `Shift` while leaving a chip, or click `[PIN]`: the panel stays and the cursor can enter it; `[PINNED]`, `Esc` or a click elsewhere closes it.
- [ ] **Drill-down, three levels from Trust:** click a link (Trust → Loyalty band → Leash …), watch the breadcrumbs `A › B › C`, `[<]` and `Backspace` go back, the third level shows no further links, going back past the root closes the panel.
- [ ] **Keyboard:** `Tab` to a chip or stat, press `?` (and `F1` — check it does not open the browser's own help; if it does, `?` is the supported key), then `Esc`. In the scene input and in a SYS number field typing `?` types a question mark and `Backspace` edits text.
- [ ] **Esc priorities:** open a Leash request form (SQUAD › LEASH › COMPEL), hover a chip inside it, pin the panel and press `Esc`: the panel closes, the form stays; a second `Esc` closes the form. In a fight `Esc` with a panel open closes only the panel; the combat keys (`1`–`6`, `G`, …) keep working afterwards.
- [ ] **Touch / long-press** (a phone or the browser's touch emulation): press and hold a stat for 0.4 s: the panel opens pinned and the tap does not click the button underneath; a short tap clicks as usual; scrolling with the finger on a chip opens nothing.
- [ ] **Reduced motion:** with the OS setting `reduce motion` on (or SYS › Analysis animation = off) the text appears at once; with `short` the line plays 0.15 s every time.
- [ ] **Readings before and after a scene that moved Trust:** read the Trust panel, play a scene with praise or an insult, read it again: the `Trend` arrow and the last-events list changed, with one-line causes.
- [ ] **Raw values on and off** (SYS › Show raw values): with it off Trust, Affinity, Attraction and Stress show only bands and arrows (no numbers in the reading, the trend or the stress sources); with it on the numbers and signed deltas appear. Stat values, resources and decision ranks are numbers either way.
- [ ] **Back-filled screens:** hover or press `?` on something on every screen — header (credits, supplies, plyx, leash, clock, injury, shortage), SQUAD (state, axes, loyalty, stress, role, level, stats, gear, defects, values, signature, skills, loadout, arc paths, Leash menu), BASE (facility levels, upgrade, roster, recruit), INV (items, rarity, affixes, compare deltas, workshop buttons), a fight (status tags, stress tags, synergies, HP, CP, skills, orders), the expedition map (known and unknown nodes), a decision (an option explains its check and requirement, never its effects), the new-campaign wizard (starts, crimes, roles, signatures, defects, sliders), and every row in SYS. Nothing reads `NO DATA`.
- [ ] **Disabled buttons:** hover a disabled button (e.g. an unaffordable UPGRADE): note whether the browser still shows the panel (some browsers send no pointer events to disabled buttons); `Tab` + `?` still works on enabled ones.
- [ ] **Off switch:** SYS › Analysis tool off: hover, `?` and long-press do nothing and an open panel closes at the next interaction.
- [ ] **Palette:** switch amber / green / white with a panel open: the panel recolours with the screen.
- [ ] An old save (before P7a) loads: no `ledger`, readings say `No recent changes`; after one activity the credits panel lists it.
