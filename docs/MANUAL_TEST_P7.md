# P7 — acceptance checklist

Automated coverage per acceptance criterion (all in `npm test`), then the steps that must be done by hand inside SillyTavern.

## Automated
1. **New campaign saves body, expression, seasoning and traits; unspent points are fine; the archetype is right at every threshold:** `tests/engine/commander/profile.test.ts` (`archetype of … is …`, `unspent points are valid`, `4/4/2 spends exactly 7 and is valid`, `a 5 at creation is rejected`, `spending 8 is rejected`), `tests/engine/setup.test.ts` (`commander setup (P7)`), `tests/content/traitSchemas.test.ts`, `tests/engine/commander/traits.test.ts`, `tests/setup/wizard.test.ts` (`Wizard: commander file (P7)`), `tests/ui/commanderFile.test.ts`.
2. **The validator rejects a body-only check; a delegate route resolves through Trust:** `tests/content/routeCheck.test.ts`, `tests/engine/commander/checks.test.ts` (`delegate complies at guarded trust…`, `one band below is a demand…`, `breaking loyalty refuses`), `tests/engine/dilemma/routes.test.ts`, `tests/engine/world/eventRoutes.test.ts`.
3. **Off-image multiplier at d = 0, 7, 21, for a frail and for a hardened commander:** `tests/engine/commander/image.test.ts`.
4. **Tastes move starting axes only for matching commanders; body and expression never touch Attraction directly:** `tests/engine/commander/presence.test.ts` (`body and expression never change attraction without a taste`, `a drawn_to_feminine unit gains attraction only with expression 1–2`).
5. **The context line has no numbers; the early-days directive appears and goes:** `tests/scene/context.test.ts` (`commander line and first-impression directive (P7)`).
6. **`vow_of_distance` removes every romantic state from reachable transitions and lowers the platonic thresholds:** `tests/engine/relations/vow.test.ts`.
7. **Old campaigns migrate and show the review modal once:** `tests/engine/commander/seed.test.ts`, `tests/app.test.ts` (`afterLoad seeds an old campaign once`), `tests/app-commander.test.ts`.
8. **`commander_import` returns valid descriptors; manual mode works without a mechanic profile:** `tests/llm/commanderImport.test.ts`, `tests/setup/commander.test.ts` (fixtures `valid`, `over_pool`, `hostile`, `prose_wrapped`, `truncated`).
9. **Review focus:** old saves (`seed.test.ts`, `app-commander.test.ts`); pool edge values (`profile.test.ts`, `growth.test.ts`); a check nobody can take (`checks.test.ts` `a stale delegate pick is rejected`, `a bypass that cannot be paid is disabled`, `dilemma/routes.test.ts` `a stale or closed pick is rejected and changes nothing`); a quiet night reaching screens that assume a knock (`quietNight.test.ts`, `tests/ui/status.test.ts`, `tests/ui/ops.test.ts`); hostile `commander_import` output (`setup/commander.test.ts` `hostile…`).
10. **Combat:** `tests/engine/combat/crisis.test.ts` (HP from endurance, dodge, presence action, scars), `tests/ui/combat.test.ts` (`presence action (P7)`).
11. **Analysis:** `tests/analysis/commanderReading.test.ts`, `tests/analysis/hidden.test.ts` (runs over the commander readings), `tests/ui/cmdrCard.test.ts`, `tests/ui/commanderFile.test.ts`.

## In SillyTavern
- [ ] **New campaign, by hand:** NEW CAMPAIGN → DOSSIER → `02 COMMANDER FILE`. The body shows words (`Average`, `Slow`…), `POINTS LEFT 2`; `+` stops at four stat points and at 0 left; the BUILD chip follows the sum; pick an expression and a seasoning; pick two regular traits (a third and an excluded one are greyed with a reason on hover) and a special one. REVIEW lists BODY / EXPRESSION / SEASONING / BUILD / TRAITS as words.
- [ ] **PREFILL FROM PERSONA:** write a persona description in SillyTavern, press it: `READING THE PERSONA…`, then the form is filled and the chosen traits show a `Why:` line. With an empty persona (or no mechanic profile and the fallback switched off) it says `MANUAL — …` and keeps your values. Pressing `Back`/`Next` while it is running must not change the form afterwards.
- [ ] **RANDOM COMMANDER:** always gives a valid file, never a special trait.
- [ ] **Unit tastes:** in PROFILE CALIBRATION the section `HOW SHE READS THE COMMANDER` offers every taste as a chip, at most three, with the suggested ones marked; REGENERATE works. `Re-sync from card` offers `How she reads the commander (tastes)` and never changes Trust, Affinity or Attraction.
- [ ] **CMDR card:** SQUAD starts with `CMDR — <name>`: crime, STRENGTH / ENDURANCE / REFLEXES, EXPRESSION, SEASONING, BUILD, TRAITS and `REPUTATION — NONE ON FILE`, all as words; with SYS › Show raw values the numbers join in brackets. Hover each chip: an explanation, and for a trait its effect lines.
- [ ] **Body training:** OPS › Train › `Body: strength`: the toast says `a little better`; the third session raises the stat (`Your strength has improved`). A stat at the top says it cannot grow. Hover STRENGTH on the CMDR card: `Training: early / halfway / close`.
- [ ] **A Crisis with each presence kind:** start a fight with the commander exposed, three times with a frail/slight, an average and a fit commander. The button reads UNNOTICED / STEADY VOICE / RALLY, works once and then says `— USED`. Rally lowers every standing unit's stress by 10; Steady Voice makes the next paid order 1 CP cheaper; Unnoticed makes enemies skip the commander about half of the time. A `Commander dodges…` line appears now and then. Losing the fight can leave a scar (a toast).
- [ ] **The sealed crate (route picker):** an expedition event `Sealed crate`: `Pry it open by hand — CHOOSE HOW` offers STRENGTH, `Ask a unit (Trust: low)` and `Pay 2 supplies`. A weak commander sees the body route greyed with the reason; asking a unit shows the party's names; a refusal costs the stress and gives no loot; the bypass takes carried supplies.
- [ ] **A 3-slot run with endurance 2:** the day after, the morning is gone (`You slept through the morning.`) unless a friend (or closer) in the party covers (`covers for you`). A night event is played before the lost morning.
- [ ] **An insomniac's quiet night:** with `Insomniac`, spend the Evening with nobody knocking: the clock reads `NIGHT — QUIET`, OPS offers `[ SLEEP ]` (free) and the evening's activities; doing one makes the next day's Empathy count one rank less (hover EMPATHY).
- [ ] **`vow_of_distance` with romance switched on:** no romantic state, proposal or DM appears; a unit at Kindred with high Trust and Affinity can become `Sworn`; `Confidant` and `Kindred` come easier.
- [ ] **An old campaign:** load a P6 save: a modal `Commander file incomplete — review` appears once, prefilled with `Average / Average / Slow`. SKIP never asks again; CONFIRM applies the form and its traits' start effects; closing it with Esc asks again next session.
- [ ] **First impressions:** start a campaign with a frail, green commander and a hardened one; compare the new units' starting Trust bands. In the first days the scene narration treats the squad as still sizing him up; after about a week the directive is gone (hover a unit's Trust: the PRESENCE reading says `fresh`, then `fading`, then `faded`).
- [ ] **SYS › Relationships:** `First impression fades (days, 1–30)` changes how long this lasts; a bad value snaps back.
- [ ] **Analysis on every new chip:** the form's stats, pickers, trait cards and taste chips, the CMDR card, the presence button, the route buttons and SLEEP all explain themselves; nothing reads `NO DATA`.
