# P11 — acceptance checklist

Automated coverage per acceptance criterion (all in `npm test`), then the steps that must be done by hand inside SillyTavern. The automated tests run in jsdom with a fake adapter and canned model answers: they cannot judge how the paper forms *look*, whether the cold open types at a readable pace, whether the main model really winds a scene down when told to, or how the first evening *feels*. Those are the manual lines. The prologue text is a placeholder draft (`docs/content/prologue.md`); judge the flow, not the wording.

## Automated
1. **A new campaign runs meta setup → cold open → intake → exam → dossier → line-up → first words → tutorial → debrief → free play, with the same fields the old wizard produced:** `tests/setup/wizardPrologue.test.ts`, `tests/ui/setupPrologue.test.ts`, `tests/engine/onboarding/{state,actions,tutorial,evening}.test.ts`, `tests/onboarding/{controller,tutorial,evening}.test.ts`, `tests/ui/onboardingCard.test.ts`.
2. **The Veteran intake and the tutorial skip work and give an equivalent state:** `tests/setup/wizardPrologue.test.ts` (modes, `result().onboarding.veteran`), `tests/engine/onboarding/state.test.ts` (wizard steps `skipped`), `tests/engine/onboarding/tutorial.test.ts` (`skip_step` withdraws the patrol), `tests/engine/onboarding/actions.test.ts` (skipping the expedition also skips the debrief).
3. **Exchange caps and scene-goal directives apply in the line-up and first-words scenes:** `tests/onboarding/controller.test.ts` (`prologue:goal`, `prologue:wrap`, the `Move on?` modal), `tests/engine/scene.test.ts` (four participants, the `prologue` origin never takes a slot).
4. **Tabs unlock on their conditions with notices; existing campaigns are unaffected:** `tests/engine/onboarding/unlocks.test.ts`, `tests/ui/shellLocks.test.ts`, `tests/onboarding/evening.test.ts` (notices, an old campaign locks nothing).
5. **The objective tracker shows prologue objectives, then director and spine hints:** `tests/engine/onboarding/objectives.test.ts`, `tests/engine/world/directorHint.test.ts`, `tests/llm/directorTick.test.ts`, `tests/ui/hubModel.test.ts`, `tests/ui/ops.test.ts`.
6. **Both starts have a prologue built from the shared skeleton:** `tests/content/core.test.ts` (`P11 prologues and unlocks`), `tests/content/prologueSchemas.test.ts`.
7. **Rules of the project:** `tests/content/pronouns.test.ts` (no pronoun for the commander in `prologues`, `uiUnlocks`, the P11 glossary entries and the new UI modules), `tests/ui/analyzeCoverage.test.ts` (the four paper screens), `tests/ui/coach.test.ts`, `tests/architecture.test.ts` (`src/engine/onboarding/**` stays pure).
8. **Review focus:** reload mid-prologue (`tests/ui/onboardingCard.test.ts`, `tests/onboarding/controller.test.ts` reload case), wandering off the rails (`tests/engine/onboarding/tutorial.test.ts` day 3, `tests/onboarding/controller.test.ts` night fallback), a soldier lost or confined (`tests/engine/onboarding/{state,actions}.test.ts`, `tests/ui/onboardingCard.test.ts`), a start without a prologue (`tests/setup/wizardPrologue.test.ts`), old campaigns (`tests/engine/onboarding/unlocks.test.ts`, `tests/ui/hubModel.test.ts`).

## In SillyTavern
Note the SillyTavern version, the browser and the window size at the top of your report. Use a fresh group chat with at least two character cards, and run the lines once for each start (Vesper "Seed Stock", Kestrel "Quota").

- [ ] **Criterion 1 — meta setup, then fiction.** New campaign: DOSSIER shows only the start cards and `[ ] VETERAN INTAKE`. WORLD PARAMETERS, UNIT ASSIGNMENT and PROFILE CALIBRATION are plain forms. The step list reads `01 DOSSIER … 05 TRANSPORT 06 INTAKE INTERVIEW 07 MEDICAL EXAM 08 PERSONNEL DOSSIER`. Calibration shows no stance line (the crime is not chosen yet).
- [ ] **Cold open.** The transport text types out at a readable pace (about 60 characters a second) on a paper card, each line after the one before. With SYS › Reduced motion on (or the system setting) every line is there at once. It is the start's own text (District 7 for Vesper, Corridor K-12 for Kestrel).
- [ ] **Intake interview.** The clerk asks the article (crime cards: pick one) and the name for the record; the last line names your corporation, not the placeholder `{CORPORATION}`. Next is greyed until both are given, with the reason.
- [ ] **Medical examination.** The medic dictates one line per body stat, the expression and the seasoning, live: raise Strength and the line changes (`Strength: Strong. Write it down.`). Traits show as notes in the file (two at most). `PREFILL FROM PERSONA` and `RANDOM COMMANDER` work. No number but the points budget is shown.
- [ ] **Dossier.** The file lists everything entered. `[ EDIT INTAKE ]` and `[ EDIT EXAM ]` jump back, keep what was entered and Next returns to the dossier. If you can bind the current group, the target radios show. `[ SIGN ]` plays the stamp sound, shows `FILED` and deploys; with a failed deployment the stamp goes away again and the form can be signed once more.
- [ ] **Criterion 2 — the old way.** Start another campaign with `[X] VETERAN INTAKE`: the classic dossier (name, start, crime), WORLD PARAMETERS, COMMANDER FILE, units, calibration, REVIEW. It deploys with the same commander fields as a prologue run with the same answers (compare the CMDR card and COMMANDER FILE on both), the tutorial step shows `[ SKIP ]` and, skipped, takes you to the first evening.
- [ ] **Hub card and locked tabs.** After deployment the hub shows a `LINE-UP` card above the objective. In the PDA, OPS, MAP, BASE and INV read `OFFLINE` and do nothing when clicked or when `2`, `3`, `5`, `6` are pressed; SQUAD, COMMS, LOG and SYS work. In a room panel there are TALK buttons but no activity cards.
- [ ] **Criterion 3 — line-up and first words.** `[ BEGIN LINE-UP ]` opens a group scene in the garage with every soldier (up to four) and does not use a slot (the day clock does not move after it). Open SYS › debug or the prompt inspector: the `prologue:goal` directive is in the context. At the sixth exchange `Move on?` appears once; after it the next reply should wind the scene down. `Keep talking` leaves it open and does not ask again; `MOVE ON` ends it and the card moves to FIRST WORDS. Each soldier has `[ TALK ]` (three exchanges, then the same question) and `[ SKIP ]`; a soldier in the brig can only be skipped. After the last one the card moves to the shakedown patrol.
- [ ] **Reload mid-scene.** Reload the page during the line-up: the card shows `[ RESUME SCENE ]`, which brings the scene back; ending it still completes the step. Switch to another chat and back during first words: the same.
- [ ] **Wandering off.** Before the line-up end two slots or sleep to day 2: the card still works (the garage is closed at night, so the line-up happens in another open room); BASE opens on day 2 with a notice (OPS, MAP and INV with the patrol).
- [ ] **Tutorial expedition.** `[ LAUNCH TUTORIAL ]` opens the planner for the patrol (no deadline); the map is fixed: one combat, one event, the end. Coach tips appear at the top of the combat screen (rows, CP) and the expedition screen (stress), each with `[ GOT IT ]` and a hint to press `?`; focusing a tip and pressing `?` explains its glossary entry. A dismissed tip stays gone, also after a reload. Lose the fight: `RETRY` is offered even with Easy retry off. The event is a small decision with a way that is not a body check. Returning (or retreating, or abandoning) completes the step; nothing in the board, the director or a rumor ever offers the patrol again.
- [ ] **Unlock notices.** When the patrol returns OPS, MAP and INV come online with a `MODULE ONLINE: …` toast (terminal look) each. BASE does the same on day 2. After the prologue every tab is open.
- [ ] **Debrief and first evening.** The card offers `[ DEBRIEF ]`; talking with the party completes it (also if you debrief from the report screen). The first evening: one soldier (the one with the highest Affinity) sends a DM, the card and OPS › OBJECTIVES read `{name} wants to talk.` with `[ TALK ]`, and tonight she knocks (Night scene; ignoring it has the small cost of a dismissed visit). The step ends on the next day and the card disappears.
- [ ] **Criterion 4 — an old campaign.** Open a campaign made before P11: every tab is open, no prologue card, no notices, the objective reads as before.
- [ ] **Criterion 5 — objectives.** During the prologue the hub objective is the current step's. After it, play a few days: the director may add a one-line lead (`LEAD` in OPS › OBJECTIVES, with `DUE IN n DAYS` when given); a proposed story beat reads as `STORY`; a soldier waiting on a demand reads as `UNIT`. At most one lead is open and a new one replaces it.
- [ ] **Criterion 6 — both starts.** Compare the Vesper and Kestrel runs: different cold open, clerk lines, line-up goal and patrol region (District 7 / Corridor K-12), same flow.
- [ ] **Dead ends.** Let a soldier die or put one in the brig (DISCIPLINE) mid-prologue, or deploy with no unit selected: the prologue goes on without her (a squad of zero skips the scenes and the patrol), nothing waits for her.
