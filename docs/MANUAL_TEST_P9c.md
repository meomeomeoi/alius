## In SillyTavern
Note the SillyTavern version at the top of your report. Most of this needs **imported soldiers**: import a card whose bond section says `LEADS: ASSERTIVE` or `DOMINANT` (a card without one never takes part). To get a soldier to level 3–4 quickly: raise her Affinity and her Impunity (SYS → *Export campaign*, edit `relations.<unitId>.affinity` / `impunityRecord`, *Import*). Switch the content category **Jealousy games** on for the level-4 and rule steps and **Public humiliation** / **Body modification** for the steps that name them (SYS › Content or the WORLD PARAMETERS › CONTENT step). Play several days: the rolls are small chances.

- [ ] **Device access follows closeness.** In a dossier the DEVICE block shows `ACCESS L{n}` with what it allows. A friend of a balanced soldier shows decor only (L1); a close one adds pinned notes (L2); an assertive lover or a bold soldier adds booking (L3); a dominant lover adds messages (L4). In COMMS her contact has her chosen name and the sticker shows on the device. `[PEEL]` removes a part and she does not put it back for a week; if the relationship ends the decor fades and goes.
- [ ] **A level-4 edit.** With Jealousy games on, send a DM to another soldier while a dominant lover has access: sometimes the text comes back with `EDITED BY {NAME}` and `[KEEP] [REVERT]`. KEEP sends her wording; REVERT sends yours and costs Trust/Affinity with a rise in her Impunity. At most once a day per soldier. A Leash request or a recruit order can be stopped the same way (KEEP files nothing). Reload while the chip is up: it comes back, freshly worded, and closes once.
- [ ] **DM edit wording and its fallback (user).** SYS → *DM edit wording* on: the edit is worded by the mechanic model in her voice. Off, or with the model down: the authored `editLines` text appears with your original in it. Either way nothing new is claimed as fact.
- [ ] **A booking.** A soldier with access 3 DMs "tomorrow evening" and the SCHEDULE (OPS) lists it. In that slot nothing else can use it (the activity, expedition, END SLOT and a scene without her say `{Name} has booked this slot: go to her or cancel it.`); the scene opens by itself once, at her place, and lowers her Want. `[CANCEL]` costs like refusing a demand, more when she does not fear the reaction. Confine her after she booked: the slot is free and the booking lapses at no cost.
- [ ] **Two bookings clash.** Let two soldiers book one slot (or edit the save): the SCHEDULE offers `[KEEP HERS]` on each or `[LET THEM SETTLE IT]`; the loser takes stress. An unresolved clash settles itself when its slot comes.
- [ ] **The smother hold.** After a breakdown a hold starts: the hangar stays locked and the next slot opens her scene through a booking.
- [ ] **Courtship and a claim.** An assertive or dominant lover starts DMs and night knocks twice as often, and her proposal shows `[ACCEPT] [DEFLECT] [SET A CONDITION]`: a condition becomes her promise in the dossier. Sometimes she claims the commander in public: the relationship goes public, rivals in a romantic tier lose Affinity, friends show respect; `[LET IT STAND]` always, `[REFUSE PUBLICLY]` only with Public humiliation on.
- [ ] **Tokens.** A close soldier offers a token once, ever (a DM, ACCEPT / DECLINE; it expires in 3 days). The dossier TOKENS block wears at most two (the third says why not). Worn: the giver warms by 1 a day, a scene with anyone says what the commander wears, rivals and friends react. `[TAKE OFF]` a worn token hurts the giver and the directive says so for a day. With a `no_other_tokens` rule active, wearing another's token breaks it.
- [ ] **Wagers.** A bold soldier challenges by DM (`[ACCEPT] [DECLINE]`); from a dossier `[CHALLENGE]` offers the five games and only the stakes that are allowed. Body modification off: the mark stake is never listed, and a running mark ends. Coin, rock-paper-scissors and high card resolve at once and show a result card; a tie in the last two voids. Sparring spends a slot (not at night) and the best handicap wins sometimes; the expedition bet resolves at the next run with her.
- [ ] **A day of obedience (user: her own business only).** Lose a wager for obedience: that day her demand, her booking, her proposal, a decision and a squad vote show her pick pre-selected with `DEFIES {NAME}`; choosing otherwise costs Trust and Affinity and raises her Impunity. Activities, the base and expeditions are untouched. If she loses: she refuses nothing and asks for nothing that day. Both end at the day's end.
- [ ] **A squad secret.** After a run where a defiant-loyal or fearful soldier came back at a quarter of her HP, a scene with the others never mentions it unprompted and a question gets a dodge. Later it comes out: Insight in a scene with a low-Trust knower, a slip from an openly defiant or resentful one, or a knock at night. The paper card shows the text with names behind bars and `[CONFRONT EVERYONE] [PRETEND NOT TO KNOW] [USE IT]`. Reload before answering: the card comes back.
- [ ] **What each answer does.** Confront: every knower's Impunity falls, the squad cools a little and the fact is public. Pretend: nothing changes but the commander knows; the squad is still told not to reveal it. Use (user): for two weeks each soldier who knew it has less Impunity (the P9 reading shows `Held over her`), and a scene with her says the commander can hold it over her.
- [ ] **A trader at the crossing.** On a run the event offers `Forbid the trade` (stress and the squad deals behind the commander's back: a secret) or `Buy the stock openly`.
- [ ] **A hidden secret stays hidden.** The analysis panel for *Squad secret* lists nothing until something came out, and never says how many are kept. A kept secret's text appears nowhere: not in a toast, the log, the chronicle or a reading.
- [ ] **An old save plays as before.** Load a save from before P9c: nothing is booked, given, wagered or kept, no new directive appears and proposals show P3's chips. A squad with no imported bond profile never takes part.
- [ ] **No commander pronoun anywhere.** Read the cards, chips, directives, glossary entries, the edited DMs and the secret card: the commander is "the commander" or "you", never he/him/his.
