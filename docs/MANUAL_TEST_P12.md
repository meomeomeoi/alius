# P12 — acceptance checklist

Automated coverage per acceptance criterion (all in `npm test`), then the steps that must be done by hand inside SillyTavern. The automated tests run in jsdom and with fake models: they cannot judge how the cards, chips, toasts, the combat button and the HANDLER panel *look*, what the real mechanic model writes for a fragment or reports as a leak or an accusation, or the app-level routing of a handler night; those are the manual lines.

## Automated
1. **Only one special trait; `bloom_paranoia` and `insomniac` exclude each other; `web_of_debts` needs a rival:** `tests/special/traits.test.ts`, `tests/ui/commanderFileSpecial.test.ts`, `tests/content/traitSchemas.test.ts`.
2. **Frequency: rates match the formulas, the log never reveals truth, `knowledge_leak` raises suspicion, discovery at 100 triggers the squad event, burning stops intercepts:** `tests/special/frequencyIntercepts.test.ts` (catch, distortion, partial, the frequency route for secrets), `tests/special/fragmentWording.test.ts`, `tests/llm/flavorFragment.test.ts`, `tests/ui/freqChip.test.ts` (a true and a false fragment render identically), `tests/special/frequencyLeaks.test.ts`, `tests/llm/intentExtractLeak.test.ts`, `tests/special/frequencyDiscovery.test.ts`, `tests/ui/opsBurnFocus.test.ts`, `tests/analysis/specialProviders.test.ts`.
3. **Paranoia: real warnings arrive earlier, false alarms are indistinguishable, acting on a false one applies the extreme event, nightmares fire, `contingency` intercepts the first hit from a turning unit:** `tests/special/commanderStress.test.ts`, `tests/special/premonitions.test.ts`, `tests/special/accusations.test.ts`, `tests/special/nightmares.test.ts`, `tests/special/contingency.test.ts`, `tests/ui/combatContingency.test.ts`, `tests/ui/premonitions.test.ts`.
4. **Debts: weekly visits, four choices per item, truthful items schedule world effects, pressure escalates to a crime leak, both discovery paths produce their dilemmas, the oversight path sets `defector_arc`:** `tests/special/handler.test.ts`, `tests/special/intel.test.ts`, `tests/special/consequences.test.ts` (including the ambush on a run), `tests/special/debtsDiscovery.test.ts`, `tests/ui/handlerPanel.test.ts`.
5. **Existing campaigns without special traits are unaffected:** `tests/special/unaffected.test.ts` (a seeded week with and without an empty `special` block), plus every existing seeded test left unchanged.
6. **Project rules:** `tests/content/pronouns.test.ts` (no pronoun for the commander in the new content), `tests/analysis/hidden.test.ts` (the hidden fixture carries the frequency, premonition and debts sentinels), `tests/ui/analyzeCoverage.test.ts`, `tests/architecture.test.ts`, `tests/power/herCallDirectives.test.ts` (the `special:` prefix).
7. **Old saves:** every new field is optional and `version` stays 1: `special`, `Commander.stress`, `ExpeditionState.ambushed`, `CombatState.contingency`.

## In SillyTavern
Note the SillyTavern version, the browser and the window sizes at the top of your report. Start a new campaign for each trait (or use the commander review of an old save) and run the lines below.

- [ ] **Criterion 1 — the cards.** COMMANDER step (and the review of an old save): the three special traits show a `SPECIAL` label and a `WHAT THIS CHANGES` list under the card, open while the card is pressed and closed otherwise. Take `bloom_paranoia` with `insomniac`: the second is greyed with `Excluded by …`. With a world that has only the commander's corporation, `web_of_debts` is greyed with `Needs a rival corporation in the world.` Hover the labels: the analysis panel explains them.
- [ ] **Criterion 2 — Secret Frequency.** Play scenes and send DMs. FREQ chips appear in the scene: `FREQ ▸ "…" (unverified)`. **Read what the model writes**: a broken half-thought in her voice, no digits, no pronoun for the commander; with SYS › Relationships › the fragment wording off, the template fragments read as fragments too. The INTERCEPTS log in the CMDR dossier lists them with `(unverified)` and never marks a true one. **Say something in a scene that only a fragment could have told you** (e.g. name a stance or a feeling the fragment hinted at): check that the mechanic model reports a `knowledge_leak` (read the debug log) and that a soldier with `Echo Bleed` or `Sensor Ghosting` later gets the directive `…senses static around the commander`. Push suspicion to the top (repeat): at the next day end a toast and a cut-in say she found out, her Trust and Affinity fall, and the others learn it over a few days (their Trust falls too). OPS › medbay › `Burn out the frequency implant` asks first; after it no chips appear and the focus is gone (greyed text `The implant is already burned.`).
- [ ] **Criterion 3 — Paranoia.** A `PREMONITION` toast arrives a day or two before a soldier's warning sign; the PREMONITIONS list in the CMDR card shows it and nothing marks it as real or false. **Accuse a warned soldier in a scene** (read the debug log: an `accused` event reported) and also punish and bench one: a false alarm costs her Trust hard (and a little stress for the commander), a real one helps the next talk-down. A nightmare shows a `BLOOM` toast the next morning, a lost morning now and then, and sometimes a soldier knocks (`… heard the commander through the wall.`). In a **mutiny** fight (or with a soldier in breakdown) give the order `[Y] Contingency` on an ally: the first hit meant for the commander lands on her (log line `CONTINGENCY: … takes the hit meant for the commander`). Without `bloom_paranoia` the order is absent. The `STRESS: <band>` chip on the CMDR card follows the nightmares, sleep and the medbay.
- [ ] **Criterion 4 — Web of Debts.** After about a week a knock at night: `A visitor at the gate.` in TONIGHT, and the HANDLER panel at night (not the door). Answer an item with each of the four answers and SUBMIT (it stays greyed until every row is set); the payment preview is the full table. After a few visits: a threat toast (the handler's line), then a cut payment, then a leak (a cut-in `EXPOSED`; everyone knows the crime). Run a mission **to the region of a mission you sold as `truth`**: the log says `They were waiting for you.` and the first fights are harder; a sold base report delays an upgrade or costs supplies; the DEBTS block in the CMDR card shows the handler, the corporation, the last visit, the count and the pressure only as `calm`/`pressing`/`threatening`. **Both dilemmas:** push oversight's suspicion to the top (many truths, a brutal oversight) and check the defining dilemma (double agent / confess / flee) and that each option opens the `defector_arc` thread (DOSSIER/world flags); let a soldier with `Threat Saturation` (or stress 60+) discover on a visit night and check her case (report / protect / demand) and that `protect` leaves a squad secret she keeps.
- [ ] **Chat switch.** Switch the chat while a fragment is being worded and while the HANDLER panel is open: nothing is written to the other chat.
- [ ] **Narrow window.** At 360–400 px the HANDLER panel's rows wrap, SUBMIT stays reachable and the WHAT THIS CHANGES list does not push the card sideways.
- [ ] **Old save.** Load a save from before P12: nothing breaks, the CMDR card shows `STRESS: calm`, no special block appears, a campaign without a special trait plays as before.
