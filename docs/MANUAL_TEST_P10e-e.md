# Manual test — P10e chunk (e): the mini-game instruments, her cards and the posture call (in SillyTavern)

Play at **1920×1080** with **the author's art** in place, and keep the prototype open beside the game for each walk (`npm run proto -- <name>`, then open `.superpowers/prototypes/<name>.html` in Chromium; `--sprite <file>` gives it the same sprite). The game is the port of that file: anything that looks, sounds or moves differently from the prototype and is not in its `fidelity.json` (`src/ui/scene/instruments/<name>/fidelity.json`) is a bug to report. Nothing here gives the commander a pronoun.

**The author's art is local only and never committed.** It lives in `content/local/art/` (ignored by git) with its manifest `content/local/art/art.json`: `{ "<her card's avatar file name>": { "<slot>": { "file": "<path under content/local/art/>", "sidecar": { "fit": {…}, "zones": {…}, "feet": "one" | "pair" | "two", "shown": "left" | "right" } }, "sprite": { "sidecar": { "face": { "x": …, "y": … } } } } }`. The slots: `over`, `over_explicit`, `face_close`, `legs_seated.rest`, `legs_seated.foot_raised`, `legs_astride` (or `legs_astride.left` + `.right`), `sole_close`, `feet_floor`, `shoe_close.boots|heels`, `shoe_sole.boots|heels`. Reload the extension after editing it. Every walk also runs once **without** the slot it uses, to see the fallback.

**Before you start.** A campaign with a soldier who has sprites and holds the commander (the dynamic on, her profile live, frame at `play` or above, tier `close`), the content categories each walk names switched on, and SYS › Scene › **Real-time contact** on (it is on by default; an old install was switched on once by the migration) with every **Contact:** interaction on. Sound on, at a normal level. Start each hold in the fiction or from the dial's fan (a contact, an item, a posture by her). **In every walk:**
- the **STOP WORD** pill bottom right stays on top and clickable, and one click ends the hold at once: the instrument is gone, the input line, the reply and the room look exactly as before (no dim, blur, tilt, tunnel, narrowed line or smeared letters left);
- typing a line into the input never acts for the body (Space and Shift typed into the field keep their spaces and capitals); only the mouse acts while the field has focus;
- the dial is dimmed while the instrument runs and its circle does not move;
- SYS › **Reduce motion** gives the still form (no shake, no write-on, no camera sway); SYS › **Readable mode** removes every blur and writes her lines in the type font;
- switching chat in the middle leaves no sound, no timer and nothing on screen, and nothing is sent into the new chat;
- with SYS › Real-time contact off (or that **Contact:** interaction off) the same hold draws the old physics card in the dock.

1. **Her cards and the posture call** (`cards.html`; no art). Get a task, a punishment and a milestone at once. The open card is glass with a stripe of her accent, her name in her hand, the kind in small caps; her words and every condition in her hand, each value underlined in stronger ink; no `[ BRACKETS ]`. The other cards are strips above it, newest on top: her name, the kind, the short name and the most urgent condition (a sealed task shows only ✉). A new card slides in with a soft card sound; an answer plays the card being placed. **Sign** needs a 1.2 s hold. Then let her call a named posture: her word large beside her, her foot tapping faster and faster as ink dots close up, her named postures rising as a fan of mannequin cards. Click the right one in time: it flies to the dial, the dial takes the posture, her `right` line. Next time click a wrong one (the right one is marked, her line names it), then let the last tap pass (her `slow` line). The stop word stays clickable over the fan.
2. **The slap** (`slap.html`; art: the `sprite` face point, `face_close` optional). With a punishment that is a slap: the camera snaps to her face, her hand rises (none for an unannounced one), feints twitch her shoulder. Press Space (or click) 42–224 ms before the impact: braced; earlier, later or not at all: none; flick the mouse hard sideways at the impact: the face turns (her offence, and cruel slaps again). Her face changes with the outcome, a red flash and ringing follow, and the cheek stays red for the rest of the scene and is gone in the next one. Without the face point her default face point is used; with Reduce motion it lands at once as `none`.
3. **Breath** (`breath.html`; contact `under_her_seat` or `smothered_breasts`; art: the room's ceiling). The dark comes down over the shape of the contact; her openings come as the engine plans them (rise, open, fall); hold Space (or the mouse button) through an opening to breathe, past 150 ms beyond it to over-hold (she notices at 450 ms and her next opening comes later). Her lines by style (ritual counts, neutral says "now.", playful fakes one), the letters of a line fading as the air runs low, the tunnel closing, the heart. Let a `breath_play` holder take you to a blackout: the blackout and the wake play, then P15a's wake card. Without ceiling art the room is seen.
4. **Weight** (`weight.html`; contacts `human_chair` (face down, her legs), `footrest`, `standing_on` (face up); art: `over` with its `fit`, the floor and the ceiling). Hold Space to bear her; keep the level's bubble in the middle with the mouse as the frame tilts; her shifts are telegraphed (cruel without warning), the cracks spread as strength runs out; Shift or the right button shifts your own weight (2.6 s apart; she may feel it). Face up: her sprite from below, or `over`. Without `over` her sprite stands in; without floor art the room.
5. **Scent** (`saturation.html`; contact `feet_on_face`). Breathe in with Space near her strongest place: words of her scent drift into the head, motes and threads on the breath; the head fills to saturation (the picture saturates) and overflows into "lost in her". Breathe out through the mouth (Shift): she may hear it. Answer her question with the pills (a lie she catches is remembered). Her scent is a stand-in (her profile has no scent words yet).
6. **The jaw** (`jaw.html`; contact `sole_to_mouth`, or the item `ring_gag`). The input line is the mouth: the mouse's height opens it, the hinges burn, the letters smear past fatigue 70, the drool. Her toes dip, push and pull back; her "suck." / "wider." (never past 0.9). With the ring, type a line: what is sent is what came out. Ask for a rest with the right button (cruel and vindictive refuse; a second one in a segment is dropped with a flash of the ring). Her foot has no art slot yet: her sprite from below stands in.
7. **Her activity and stillness** (`her_activity.html`; her activity `reading_reports` (or any framed one), the commander placed by her: under her desk, kneeling by her, facing the wall; art: `legs_seated.rest`, `legs_seated.foot_raised`, `feet_floor`). Her time plays in phases: her murmur, her sounds, her lines to herself and to the commander, calls and visitors. Keep still: the urges come (an itch, a cramp, a swallow); a fast mouse ripples, a sustained fast one fidgets; a relief may be seen or heard (her tell at once). Between phases choose how to spend the hours. In `use` she forgets the commander at the end: GET UP, or wait to be found by another soldier. Without the leg art her silhouettes are drawn.
8. **The throne** (`throne.html`; her activity `holding_audience` or any framed one, contact `human_chair`; art: `legs_astride` (one file mirrored, or `.left` + `.right`)). The weight inside her frame: her mug on the commander's back (0.5 s out of level spills), her signing, her rising (no weight), her visitors and their business, the thanks at the end; SYS › Scene › **Long holds** changes how hard it is. Without `legs_astride` her silhouettes.
9. **Foot service** (`her_activity.html`, the game inside; under her desk with her practices live and worship open; art: `sole_close` with its `zones`, else `legs_seated.foot_raised`). Her foot signals (tap twice, the sole, a push, a hook…) ask for a kiss, cleaning or a rub; the close-up opens; a press held still is a kiss, back and forth cleans (with her grain), a steady turn rubs. Her good, poor or wrong line; her verdict at the end lands once a scene. Without `sole_close` the raised foot or her sprite, with the automatic zones.
10. **Shoe service** (`shoe.html`; contact `boot_worship`; art: **both** `shoe_close.<kind>` and `shoe_sole.<kind>` of her footwear, `boots` unless her likes name heels). Clean the dirt of her day (dust, mud, ash, oil, spores; its own colour), swallow the grit with Space or spit it (X or the right button) while she is busy (her taps heard), shine clean leather with slow strokes; her inspection's band of light names the worst zone; her verdict. **Without both pictures the physics card draws instead** (no stand-in sprite); a picture that fails to load hands the hold to the card.
11. **Gulp** (`gulp.html`; needs `watersports` and `bodily_fluids`; no contact starts it yet (A3) and no control of the game dispatches `physics/start`, so until P17b's watersports contacts exist walk it on the layout page instead: `npm run shots -- gulp` (or open the layout page's `gulp` fixture, `scripts/layout-page.mjs`) beside `npm run proto -- gulp`; art: `over_explicit` (explicit content on), else `over`, the ceiling and the floor).
