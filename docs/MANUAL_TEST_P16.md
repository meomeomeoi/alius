# Manual test — P16 practices: tasks, her discipline, demonstrations, service, denial, holding (in SillyTavern)

Run in a fresh campaign with four imported soldiers, at least two of them with a practice section on the card (a `practice` block with a primary style, `audience` set to `show_off`, and the categories `restraint`, `service`, `denial` and `public_humiliation` as `enjoys` or `craves`) and a third without one. In SYS › Content turn on `restraint`, `service`, `denial` and `public_humiliation`; for step 15 also `physical_discipline_shown`. Turn **Debug mode** on (SYS › Debug lists every call to the mechanic model and its prompt; the main prompt, with its `practice:` lines, is in SillyTavern's prompt inspector). SYS › Dynamic has "Words of her practices": leave it on "the model words them" (step 2 also tries "plain lines").
The practices are slow and quiet: cards in her voice, a few chips, a strip. Nothing here shows her favour as a number, the words of a sealed envelope before it is opened, whether a punishment was fair, how long a hold lasts when she has not said, how many strokes or what number she thinks too low, the time she allows for a slap, her permission answer before it was asked, or why she decided the way she did on a duty. Nothing gives the commander a pronoun or says what the commander feels.

1. **A holder at play by the setup seed.** In WORLD PARAMETERS › DYNAMIC seed one soldier with a profile at a starting frame `play` (the second with `role` if you want step 12 and 13 to go faster). Start the campaign. Her DYNAMIC section (SQUAD tab) shows FRAME PLAY and a band. A soldier at `equal`, and one with no practice profile, never asks anything of the commander: no card, no chip, no `practice:` line in the prompt, the extractor's prompt in SYS › Debug carries none of the practice types. Look at the PRACTICES block in her section: it is not there yet.
2. **A task by DM.** Play a day or two. A message arrives from her in her voice asking for something small ("report to me at dusk", forms of address). The task card is in her thread and in a scene with her: her words, `Due:` as a day word, what it costs in bands, `[ ACCEPT ]` `[ REFUSE ]` `[ TALK ]`, and REROLL while it is still offered. Switch SYS › Dynamic › Words of her practices to "plain lines": the next task is the seed's own line, with no model call in SYS › Debug. Switch it back.
3. **The cost card.** A task that weighs "minor" lists, before the buttons, what it takes ("Takes 1 slot of the commander's time", "Would lapse: …", "Uses a little …"). A trivial one is a plain `[ ACCEPT ]`. Press ACCEPT on a minor one: the cost is spent, and the PRACTICES block of her dossier now lists it by summary with "on a button, with its cost" and a day word.
4. **A major accept in words.** A task that weighs "major" says "Say it in your own words." and shows who will know. `[ ACCEPT ]` only focuses the input. Say yes in the story ("I will, I'll wear it"): the task opens. A severe one then shows `[ SIGN ]` (press and hold): the CMDR card gains a record line under "Held by" ("Day N", the summary). A sealed envelope says only that it is sealed and, in some styles, its weight and what it is about; ACCEPT opens it: its words come out and the record says "Signed blank".
5. **A broken task and her punishment.** Leave a task unreported past its deadline (or tell her in words that it was not done). Within a day a message from her states the consequence, and a punishment card shows the same: `[ ACCEPT ]` `[ NEGOTIATE ]` `[ REFUSE ]`. ACCEPT carries it out the way the content switches allow (a held slot, a day without messages, her cold shoulder; a physical one is only reported unless `physical_discipline_shown` is on) and the scene's prompt tells the model once. The MEMORY view has a fact for it (`punishment_given`); a punishment before others adds `punishment_public`.
6. **NEGOTIATE.** On another punishment press `[ NEGOTIATE ]`: a one-to-one scene opens in her quarters without costing the slot, the card stays. Argue; when she agrees to something lighter the answer comes as words and the card is replaced by the agreed one. Pressing ACCEPT on the original afterwards still works.
7. **REFUSE and the escalation.** Refuse a punishment: a toast with its cost (her hold and Impunity move, a refusal noted once), and unless she is affectionate a heavier one follows, once. Refuse that one too: it ends there. An order about a soldier ("DO IT", "TAKE IT INSTEAD", REFUSE) goes through the squad vote when the squad votes on such a measure.
8. **UNDO.** Next to an answered punishment the chip `[ UNDO — not what I meant ]` takes the answer back (hold, favour and xp are restored, the restriction ends, a hold or a slap it started stops). It is refused once something moved (a day end, a frame, a slot spent, a ceremony step done, a slap that landed, a sealed accept signed blank).
9. **A demonstration in the mess.** With others at base a card arrives: she wants to show the commander's service before others, with who watches, "Mess hall, evening", and that it takes the slot. `[ GO ]` opens a group scene in the mess hall in the evening with her and the audience; the prompt carries `practice:demonstration:` (the form, how each watcher takes it, never a number) and, if she talks, "others address her; she answers for the commander". At the scene's end each watcher's disposition and the fact `demonstration_staged` follow. `[ REFUSE ]` costs by what it weighed.
10. **Protocol and the slip.** A protocol request becomes a rule proposal from her ("stand when I come in"). Accept it in the RULES list; her dossier's PRACTICES block lists it under PROTOCOL, and with household protocol and an appetite for service she also keeps a ROUTINE. Slip in a scene (do not stand, do not address her as asked): the extractor offers the slip, it is noted, and she may remark on it. `[ I DIDN'T ]` on the routine tells her it was not done today (cheaper than being found out). A verdict (pleased, adequate, displeased) lands at the scene's end and, with the commander's service judged well, her FAVOR reads WARM; badly, COOL; never a number, also with "show raw values" on.
11. **The permission chip.** While a rule of hers asks for permission (or a chastity-style item names one) `[ ASK PERMISSION ]` appears beside the other chips. Press it: her answer (granted, refused, not yet, or "earn it first", which opens a task) shows as a line under the chip and nowhere before it was asked. Doing the thing without asking is a slip against her rule. Asking again while the answer stands repeats it.
12. **A sensory hold and ASK TO BE LET GO.** Accept a hold task (one of "kept in her quarters", "lights out"). The HELD strip appears in the shell header with `[ ASK TO BE LET GO ]`; the hub's marker for the commander sits in her cabin; DMs, scenes with her and END SLOT go on, everything else is refused with a reason. In the sensory kind the strip becomes one plain line and the scene's text keeps only what is heard and felt. A hold with no stated end shows no "until". ASK TO BE LET GO: she releases, keeps the commander a while longer or refuses (cold ones in silence), and tells the model in her own way. A hold that runs to its end raises her restraint track; after two, restraint level 1 and `first_night_held` may be offered as a booking.
13. **The alarm on a launch and the run without the commander.** While held, launch an expedition: an alarm card in her voice. A duty she weighs above the hold is released (the card says so, OK); a `keep` shows `[ ACCEPT ]` `[ ARGUE ]` `[ DEFY ]` and, for a launch or a deadline, `[ SEND THE SQUAD ]`. ARGUE opens one exchange in her quarters; DEFY goes on with the launch at the cost of a defiance (an offence of hers; the hold ends, with no xp). SEND THE SQUAD opens the expedition setup for the mission without the commander: combat starts at command points 0 with no regeneration, no orders and no compel, the event nodes are decided by the squad (a card with ACKNOWLEDGE), the report says the commander was not there and her restraint and the squad's feelings move afterwards.
14. **ASK FOR A TASK, mocked.** With the commander's standing poor with her (favor COOL) or with a cruel or cold holder, press `[ ASK FOR A TASK ]` (or `[ ASK FOR PUNISHMENT ]`): she mocks the ask in a line, a fact `ask_mocked` is written (present, not spread) and nothing is issued. A warm affectionate holder refuses kindly, a ritual one accepts. Only one ask a day for each woman; a second is soft-disabled with a reason. With a woman who holds nothing yet, a yes only raises her hold (the dynamic starts); no task is written.
15. **The slap.** Needs `physical_discipline_shown` and SYS › Display with reduced motion off, and P15a's timing interaction on. Accept a punishment that includes the slap (or slip with a cold, cruel, drill or vindictive holder). A cut-in, the alert sound and a card: "Her hand is raised." Press Space (or `[ BRACE ]`) just before it comes down: braced. Early, on a feint or twice: flinched. No press: it lands. Her reaction is in the next reply (the `practice:slap:` line), favor moves by the outcome (never shown), a cruel one slaps once more after a flinch. Before subordinates it needs `public_humiliation` and costs her regard in their eyes. With reduced motion or timing off nothing is timed: it lands. With a text field focused the field keeps its Space.
16. **A handover of the device.** In a scene with a holder `[ GIVE HER … ]` opens the composer: THE DEVICE (one day), THE KEY (one to seven), THE CALENDAR (one to three). Send it: a card in her voice; say yes in your own words and sign. From then the commander's next message to another soldier opens her edit first (KEEP, REVERT) and nothing is sent until then; a key puts her in the commander's cabin now and then and a unit who sees it knows; a calendar books every free slot of tomorrow with her. `[ TAKE BACK … ]` is a refusal at severe and ends it. The HOME line names tomorrow when her hand has filled.

Also try `[ ASK HER ]` on a dilemma, a vote or an expedition event card: it marks her pick, choosing another costs her trust, and a holder at `rule` with the command pre-selects (only her pick, with `[ CONFIRM ]` and `[ DEFY ]`). Every card, chip, strip and slap overlay can be examined with `?`; the PRACTICES block of her section shows what she asked, her protocol, her routine, her favor as a word and her names for postures. In immersive mode (SYS › Display › "Hide the dynamic's numbers") the block goes and the cards in the scene and the thread stay. A campaign in which nobody holds at play looks exactly as before: no card, no chip, no `practice:` line, no new option in the extractor's prompt.

Report anything that shows a number she keeps, the words of a sealed envelope before it was opened, a punishment that says it was unfair or a task that says nothing was done, a hold that gives an end she did not state, a permission answer before it was asked, a slap that times itself with reduced motion on, a task that appears from a woman who is not holding, a second slap or alarm open at once, an UNDO that goes through after the day moved, or a pronoun for the commander.
