# Manual test — P16b service as a craft: disciplines, drills, postures, provocation, her feet, care, the programmes (in SillyTavern)

Play at **1920×1080** (and look once at 1280×800), in a fresh campaign whose WORLD PARAMETERS have **The dynamic** on, with five imported soldiers. Give three of them a practice section (a `practice` block with a primary style and a frame the setup seed puts at `play` or `role`): one `drill`, one `regal` (with a `manner` block, `curiosity +1` or the `regal` style itself), one `vindictive` (the commander's file must name a crime, and she must know it: `knows_crime`); a fourth woman with a practice profile in the frame `equal`; a fifth with no practice section. Let the profiles' categories include `restraint` and `service`, and turn on in SYS › Content `restraint`, `service` and, for step 17, `physical_discipline_shown` and the category your sparring partner's `armpits` worship rides on. SYS › Scene: leave **Game: the game of postures** and **Game: stillness under provocation** on, real-time physics on, reduced motion off (a second pass with it on is step 21). Turn **Debug mode** on (SYS › Debug lists every call and its prompt; the `craft:` lines of the main prompt are in SillyTavern's prompt inspector). Nothing here shows an xp number, a drift rate, her noticing chance, the settling-in count, the exam's pass mark, the lesson word before she says it, or the crime a sentence echoes before the echo has acted. Nothing gives the commander a pronoun or a feeling. Hover any control with `?` held: the analysis tool explains it. The prototypes in `docs/prototypes/` (`posture.html`, `her_activity.html`, `cards.html`) are the look to compare with (`npm run proto -- posture`).

1. **Off is unchanged.** In a campaign where nobody holds at `play` (only the `equal` woman and the one with no profile), play a day: no `craft:` line in the prompt, no craft card or chip, no drill focus except the three endurance drills, no `[ CARE ]` in the quarters of the woman with no profile; her DYNAMIC section has no HER POSTURES and no SENTENCE row. The CMDR file shows CRAFT with every discipline `untrained`. The `equal` woman with a dynamic block does get `[ CARE ]` (care is open toward her), and nothing else.
2. **CMDR › CRAFT.** Open the commander file: HOLDS is as before (its words are now the craft's: *untrained, raw, practised, steady, seasoned, masterful*), and CRAFT after it lists every discipline as a word and, for each woman something is written for, her rhythm as a word, the postures known and the foot signals she has named. A record is a margin line in her hand ("Day 12 — 3 minutes under my weight."). With "show raw values" on the xp appears; with it off, never. Every chip answers `?`.
3. **Drills alone.** OPS › TRAIN: the focus list has the stillness, recitation, rhythm and posture drills (and the three endurance drills) once a holder is at play; pick one and do it: one click, the slot is spent, CRAFT shows the discipline moving a step over a few days. Do the same drill again the same day: it pays nothing and still spends the slot. The posture drill alone asks for nothing else.
4. **A drill with her.** TRAIN with the `drill` woman as companion: a second select ("— just together —", the breath and weight drills, the posture drill) appears. Pick the weight drill: the scene opens at the training yard with a card in the dock, `begin` and `not now`, her conditions ("With me.", "It takes one slot."). `begin` stages the contact and starts the hold; at its end her verdict lands, the drill pays ×1.5, Regard rises if she was pleased. A companion on any other focus behaves as before. With another close woman (no holder) the drill is ×1.2; a holder who is not she may read it as being seen together and be jealous (Want grows faster that day).
5. **Records and lessons.** Hold a weight or breath run to its end: a new best sets a record in fiction time (identical at every marathon pace, in turn-based and real-time play) and the MEMORY view may carry a fact. Slip on purpose in a scene with a `drill`, `ritual` or `clinical` holder: her next line may name the point ("hands", "three windows"); a `cruel` one never does. The slip pays only half xp, and only if her punishment or her named point follows it; a slip nothing followed pays nothing (check CMDR › CRAFT with raw values).
6. **The game of postures.** In a scene with the `drill` woman take a posture from the dial's fan or let her call one: the dial grows to 300 px, her name for the posture is written under it with the preset's words and a fiction clock; the seven beads on the left (1 TORSO to 7 FEET) glow as a point drifts; a click on the joint or the key 1–7 corrects it (a loud correction under her eyes is fidgeting she sees; every third seen one is a slip); a point past its limit is a slip and she puts it back. Visitors, her undressing and a sound behind tempt the gaze; hold 6 to keep it down. "Look at me" not obeyed is its own fault (the eyes stay down). Under a blindfold the dial fades, the body speaks in feel lines. Release: a clean hold pays `posture`, the posture's knowledge and a record; a hold with no end shows no end. The stop word ends it at once, with no pay.
7. **Her call, schemes A, B and C.** With the commander's posture level low her call is the fan of cards (A). From level 2 (a few clean holds and drills) the call is B: her posture is a ghost until the posture is known, you put it together point by point against her window (her style's plus 2.5 s for each point that has to move) and it is taken the moment every point matches; hints thin out with mastery (the ring on the points still to move goes first); SYS › Scene "Posture call: always show every hint" shows them all. From level 4 it is C: a series of five calls, about three in ten with no prefix (holding still is right). A first call of a posture she introduced is a lesson: the ghost, no slip on a miss.
8. **Settling in.** After three clean posture holds in a row she may add a load her style likes: a hold with no stated end, or a clause in her directive (a count aloud, a speech rule). Nothing shows the count.
9. **HER POSTURES and PRACTISE.** Her DYNAMIC section lists only the postures she has named: her name in her hand, the mannequin, the points with their keys, the commander's knowledge as a word (never xp) and `[ PRACTISE ]`: a posture drill alone on that preset (the slot is spent; with no slot it is `aria-disabled` with the reason). A `ritual`, `drill` or `regal` woman names a new posture by her taste when her set has room (the first, a `regal` one, tends to display or worship; a `drill` one attention or endurance) and never one her limits or the humiliation her frame allows forbid.
10. **Stillness under provocation.** In her activity's frame (she sits, the commander kneels) with the game on: her touches, tickles, whispers and feints arrive as stimuli; any key but Space, a click or a fidget during one is a reaction she feels at once (her line; the stimulus ends); Space breathes through it for at most 4.5 s (let out loudly when it runs out). After two clean ones in a row she tries harder. Each stimulus let go by pays `stillness` a little. With **Game: stillness under provocation** off the frame has no inner game.
11. **Her feet.** In a scene with a foot contact (foot service under her desk): she names a signal the first time and gives it later; answering it trains `memory`, missing it is `foot_signal_missed` and a slip her discipline may follow; CRAFT lists the signals she has named (not the others). Her feet are how they are today (a hot, worn or sore foot, or bare in her quarters in the evening) and the directive may say so once. After a pleased foot or shoe service her combat form is a little better in the next run (and a little worse when she is sore and nothing was done): look at the combat build's passive. The foot service names its signals again in each session (known limitation).
12. **`on_hands`.** The contact "on hands" (her foot on the commander's open palms) is offered only while the commander holds a posture that supports it (`kneel_present`); in any other posture it is not in the chip's list.
13. **Unasked care.** In her cabin (hub room panel) or her DYNAMIC section: `[ CARE ]` lists what the commander may do for her without asking ("polish her boots", "tidy her quarters" …); one costs one slot, is refused while she is with the commander in that slot or a booking is due, and says nothing of whether she noticed. In her next scene or DM, a noticed care is shown in her way (a note card for one holder, a line for another; the MEMORY view and the journal have it); at `equal` that is all, from `play` favour and Regard follow. The fourth noticed care within seven days, for a `regal` (or curious) woman on the hold band `testing` with a manner, makes her name the service once ("fourth time this week …"). A care she did not notice never appears.
14. **Praise and the intents.** In a scene with the `regal` woman who has set the daily-praise rule (she proposes it at frame `role`; accept it in the RULES list), praise her in your own words: the extractor's prompt in SYS › Debug offers `praise_given` only then, and never the text of the praise. A repeat of an earlier line (similar words) is refused as a repeat; a day without a fresh one is a slip, noted by her; a fresh one raises her regard (hidden). `care_noticed` and `foot_signal_answered` are offered only while their subject exists.
15. **`[ANTICIPATE]`.** In a scene with a holder whose reading allows it (warm and sure enough; the chip is not there before): `anticipate her` in the chip row stages the commander's act as a narrated line and her answer follows in her voice by style; the chip is gone for the scene; her favour rises a little (hidden).
16. **The `drill` programme.** A day after she is at play her norms are set (in her hand on HOME, "My norm for the day: …"; no pass mark anywhere), and weekly an exam is booked on a free slot (never over another booking). Play it: a card (`begin`, `ask for time`) with her norms in her hand, then a hold for each norm; pass and the norms rise 10 %; fail and two more drill sessions are booked at the yard. Her verdicts are lessons.
17. **The sparring hold.** Lose a sparring wager (P9c) to a craft-live woman at `play` or more whose expectations put `armpits` at `enjoys` or `craves`: a scene opens at the training yard with her alone and the headlock contact staged, the scent instrument plays, the tap ends it. If others were at the yard they lose a little regard for the commander (hidden) and may come to suspect the dynamic. A woman at `equal`, or one who does not like it, never offers it; the wager's own result and fact are unchanged.
18. **The `vindictive` sentence.** With the commander's crime known to her, within a day she sets a term (7 to 30 days by the crime): a sentence card in her voice (`understood`), the DYNAMIC section shows `SENTENCE: N DAYS LEFT` and nothing about the crime until the echo has acted. A slip toward her adds three days. By crime: a liquidation takes a booking you made with another woman each week and puts a lost privilege first among her punishments; smuggling routes your DMs to others through her edit (KEEP, REVERT); an experiment gives her measured tasks; a purge a day of obedience; a mutiny a rule "before the squad, you answer to me first"; a strain trade a weekly tithe of Plyx or credits. Served to its end: a closing rise of her regard and the SENTENCE row goes; it is never set again.
19. **Removal.** Remove a woman who has a sentence, norms, a praise log or a posture hold from the service: the sentence's echo stops, her exam and session bookings are gone, her posture hold ends; the commander's own CRAFT stays.
20. **Her goal.** Praise a hobby she cares about in her presence (P14c) once a week: her regard rises a little (hidden), only for a craft-live holder.
21. **Reduced motion and the layout.** With reduced motion on: the dial does not animate its size, no jolt, no write-on; the cards arrive without motion. At 1280×800 the 300 px dial overlaps the reply by about 43 px (known). Run `npm run test:layout` after any CSS change.

Also check: with **Game: the game of postures** off no hold starts (`posture/take` is refused with a reason) and the call is as P16's; in turn-based play (real time off) a posture hold has no instrument, her directive stands and only the stop word or the scene's end closes it; a chat switch drops every `craft:` directive and every card remembered by scene; an old save opens with no craft key written; the PDA shows no number for any of this.

Report any xp number, drift rate, noticing chance, count, pass mark or crime shown early, a care that appears before she showed it, a lesson word before she said it, a `praise_given` offered with no praise rule, a drill that pays twice a day, a scene surface that does not match its prototype, a record that differs by marathon pace, a control without `data-analyze`, or a pronoun for the commander.
