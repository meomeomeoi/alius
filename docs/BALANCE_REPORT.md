# Balance report (P4)

Generated by `npm run sim -- --neutrality` (the headless harness in `src/sim/`). A squad of four (assault and defender in front, sniper and medic behind, level 5, skill points spent, standard-issue gear) fights seeded, generated encounters on AUTO. Every row puts one defect on **all four** units and is paired by seed with the control squad that has none; a row passes when its win rate is within ±5 percentage points of the control (overview P6: a defect changes *how* a unit is played, not *how strong* she is). The rule is **reported, not enforced by `npm test`**: tuning of the draft numbers continues after the package.

What the table cannot see: costs that live outside combat (double supplies for `hunger_loop`, daily deprivation stress for `imprint_hunger`, medbay upkeep for `graft_rejection` — the harness squad is *maintained* — relationship and social effects, refusals that need a commander order). Stress-only upsides such as `affect_dampener`, `imprint_fracture` and `reward_loop`, and the commander-only effects of `possessive_protocol`, never change a plain win rate, so a flat +0.0 there means "invisible to this harness", not "balanced".

Reading the two runs:

- **Danger 2** is the regular fight: the control wins 100 %, a combat lasts 4.2 rounds on average (target: at most 5) and every defect passes — the squad is too strong for the rule to bite here.
- **Danger 4** is the stress test (win rate ≈ 84 %, 9.5 rounds — longer than the 5-round target, which only holds for regular fights). Six defects leave the band: `graft_rejection` (+13.6 pp: +20 % to every stat while maintained, its price is the upkeep), `target_lock` (+9.8), `imprint_hunger` (+9.5: +10 % to every stat against +1 stress per round), `hunger_loop` (+8.0: healing 5 % per turn, its price is supplies), `compulsive_protocol` (+5.8) and `pain_inversion` (+5.7). None is negative: every defect is at least as strong in a fight as no defect, so the draft numbers lean generous. Expect the author to trim the upsides above or to add in-combat downsides.

## Commander Crisis HP (P7)

The Crisis HP of the commander changed from a flat **60** to **20 + 10 × endurance** (+ trait effects, at least 10): 30 (endurance 1), 50 for the default commander (endurance 3), 70 (endurance 5), 40 with `old_wound`. The default commander therefore has 10 HP less than before, and a 6 % × reflexes dodge chance (12 % by default) on hits that reach him. The harness above does not model Commander Crisis, so its numbers are unchanged; re-run it if the encounter tables are tuned for fights that expose the commander.
