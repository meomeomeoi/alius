# Regal: probing, stages and the far end (design ideas, 2026-11-26)

**Status:** ideas chosen by the author in the design session of 2026-11-26. Not specified yet: Part A goes into **P15b** (`docs/packages/P15b-her-manner.md`), Parts B–D into **P17g** (`docs/packages/P17g-worship-customs.md`) as a revision section; the spec wins where it differs from this draft. Each idea says what already exists, what is new and where it hooks in.

**What `regal` means here (author):** not a queen with titles, a coronation or a court. A vibe in which the commander **serves her and worships her as something higher**, shown more strongly than in the other styles. The approved throne (`docs/prototypes/throne.html`) is the reference: her life goes on above, the commander is the ground of her comfort, she barely notices the effort, her attention is rare. She is often silent, mostly because she is busy with her own things and simply uses the commander; when she is in the mood she plays with other kinks and talks out of boredom.

**Rulings of the session (author, 2026-11-26):**
1. Probing ideas go into P15b, added to the existing probing (P15 §15.1).
2. Any holder whose profile fits may grow these customs; `regal` weighs them double (P17g's principle). A custom that depends on her character reads her manner and values, not the style word.
3. The chamber (§C.3) is urine only; no new content category.
4. At the last stage of the whim (§D.1) a whim is absolute: it cannot be disputed, however strange it is. Not doing it is a heavy offence. It is not a break and does not end the dynamic.
5. Her delay of a run (§D.2) touches optional missions only.
6. Every stage is open by default; the player may lower a ceiling per custom.
7. Growth works by **stages on customs** (approach A below), not by the tracks alone.

**Dropped in the session:** carrying her (in arms or on the shoulders: the soldiers are far stronger than the commander); titles, coronation, petitions, pardons and anything that needs a court; the commander speaking of self in the third person (P17b §3, an earlier author ruling).

## A. Probing (→ P15b)

### A.0 How it is added
- **Today:** `pickAxis` (`src/engine/dynamic/probes.ts`) picks evenly over every `probeAxes` row of the band's tier (`dynamicRoll`, no rng draw). Adding rows as they are would shift the pick in every campaign and move seeded tests.
- **New:** a `probeAxes` row may carry `needs?: { styles?, drawnBy?, manner? }` (the same shape as P17g's `needs`). A row with `needs` enters her pool only when `bond.practice.manner` is present (P15b's gate) and the needs hold. A woman who matches none of them keeps the old pool byte for byte; old saves are untouched.
- **Weight:** a matching row with `styles` containing her primary style counts twice in the pool (two slots), so `regal` probes these more often. Nothing else in the pick changes.
- **Reaction:** reported by the existing `probe_answered { axis, response, tone }`; no new intent unless named below.

### A.1 New axes
| Axis | Gesture (the main model invents it from the hint) | Yield | Needs (draft) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `admiration` | "how do I look?", in a scene or a DM with a photo of her (new uniform, after the hair) | a sincere compliment (`tone: sincere`); `ironic` is a deflection | `regal`, or `drawnBy: power` with `warmth ≤ 0` |
| `deference` | lets the commander see she expects to go first, get the better seat, be served first; holds out her hand, half joking, "well?" (kissed = yield; shaken or laughed off = deflected) | the commander gives way | `regal`, `ritual`, `possessive` |
| `credit` | at the debrief she presents the commander's plan as hers | silence, or agreeing | `regal`, `drill` |
| `gaze` | holds the look to see who looks away; or, having caught the commander looking at her legs, crosses them and lets a shoe dangle from her toes | the commander looks away first / keeps looking at the shoe | `regal`, `playful`; the shoe needs `worship` |
| `corrected_praise` | the commander praises her and she corrects it: "not pretty. Say beautiful." | the commander repeats it as told | `regal` |
| `feet_in_lap` | in a shared evening (a P14c pastime, a film on the PDA) she puts her feet on the commander's lap as if only for comfort | the commander lets them stay or starts to rub them (`foot_rub`) | `worship` likes |
| `hypothetical` | in a DM: "would you kneel if I asked?" | a yes | `regal`, `playful`, `curiosity ≥ +1` |

- `hypothetical` writes the commander's answer as a fact she may quote later ("you said you would"): new event `hypothetical_answered { unitId, answer }` (memory: §E).

### A.2 Not axes
- **The joke that stopped being a joke** (a `first_yes` form). She plays at being a queen ("bring it, servant") on `testing`; one day she stops smiling and the commander still does it. A second `equal → play` milestone row with `requires.style: ['regal']`, offered as a `scene`; it replaces `first_yes` for her (the graph takes the first that fits). Text `AUTHOR TO WRITE`.
- **The first time below her.** An innocent occasion: her lace came undone after a run, and the commander kneels to tie it. She looks down and lets it last ("wait"). A P15a moment source `first_below` (her options: let it last / thank / tease), only once per pair, at `testing`; the CSS camera "from below" (P17c) is first used here.
- **She names the service before the commander does.** P16b's unasked care is counted already; at the fourth unasked care in 7 days (a `regal` or `curiosity ≥ +1` holder on `testing`) a directive offers her line once: "fourth time this week you've brought me coffee. Did I ask?" Nothing new is stored.
- **What wakes her (`latent`).** For a `regal` holder (or `drawnBy: power` with `warmth ≤ 0`) a latent inclination also wakes from **sincere admiration**: three `admiration` / `corrected_praise` yields or sincere P3 praise events in 14 days. A new cause in `latentCause`, gated like A.0.

## B. Stages on customs (→ P17g, general to every custom)

### B.1 Approach (chosen: A)
- **A, chosen:** customs get **stages**. A custom grows one stage at a time through her own offer, under conditions written per stage. The player sees the stage the pair is at and sets a ceiling.
- **B, rejected:** let P16's track levels cap the intensity the model generates and nothing else. It needs no authoring, but the growth would be the model's, with no ceiling per idea and no visible place on it.

### B.2 Data
`worshipCustoms[].stages?: Array<{ label, requires: { frame, tracks?, categories?, attitude?, capabilities?, milestone? }, stakes, promptHint, description }>`.
- `requires.frame`: `play` | `role` | `rule`.
- `tracks`: P15 track levels (`{ humiliation: 2 }`).
- `categories`: all enabled.
- `attitude`: her `as` for a category through `practiceOf` (`enjoys` | `craves`).
- `capabilities`: P15 capabilities she holds (read by capability, never by domain id).
- `milestone`: a milestone the pair has passed (`private_rule` for the far end).
- `stakes`: the P16 consent ladder (R.1) for the offer of this stage; `severe` writes the CMDR record line.

Stage 1 is the custom as introduced today; a custom without `stages` is unchanged.

**State:** `customs.active[].stage` (1-based), `stagedOn?: number` (day), `stale?: number` (hidden).

### B.3 How a stage grows
1. **Staleness (hidden).** Each time the custom is kept, `stale` rises by 1. Her threshold is `TUNING.customs.staleAt` (draft 6), scaled by her manner: `drawnBy: discovery` or `curiosity ≥ +1` lowers it (×0.6), `order ≥ +1` or `drawnBy: discipline` raises it (×1.5), with a hidden spread of `dynamicHash`. A fresh stage is not left early.
2. **Her offer.** When `stale` has reached her threshold, the next stage's `requires` hold, the stage is under the ceiling and the pacing allows, she offers the next stage as a first moment (the P17g mechanism), with the consent form of its `stakes`.
3. **Pacing.** At least 7 fiction days since this custom's last step. At most one new custom **or** one step-up per holder a fiction week, shared with P17g's introductions.
4. **Accepted:** the stage rises and `stale` returns to 0.
5. **Refused:** the custom stays where it is, with a 14-day cooldown for this custom. A refusal at stakes `major` or above is a refusal of hers in P16's sense (her discipline may follow). It is never an exit.
6. **Falling back.** A stage falls **one** step, never more at once, when its `requires` stop holding (the frame cooled, a track decayed, a category was switched off, her attitude drifted through P14b) or after 14 days without upkeep. Stage 1 falling is P17g's fading, as today.

### B.4 The player's hand
- Content categories are hard gates, as everywhere.
- **SYS › Dynamic › Customs:** besides the toggle per custom, a **ceiling** per custom (stages 1…N). Default: every stage open (ruling 6). Lowering the ceiling under the current stage drops the custom to the ceiling at the next day step, with no offence.
- Refusing in the fiction, the exit (P15 E2) and the stop word, untouched.
- The dossier's PRACTICE block shows each active custom with **its current stage label**, never the next one; analyze explains a stage after it happened.

### B.5 Prompt
The custom's `customs:` directive reads the current stage's `promptHint` (P17g §1.5: triggered, "if it fits, … once", gone once voiced, one custom a reply).

## C. The middle: new customs with stages (→ P17g)

Stakes per stage are drafts; `AUTHOR TO WRITE` for every label and hint.

### C.1 The salute (`salute`), idea 3
- **Needs:** her character, not the style: `order ≥ +1` (P15b), or a value of hers that reads discipline or rank (P8 `valueReactions`, read by field); `regal` ×2. Frame `play`.
- **Rule:** a P16 rule template like `stand_when_i_enter`: when she enters or passes, the commander takes `standing_attention` and salutes (a P17c gesture); she does not return it. Checked from blocking postures and her entry; a breach is `protocol_slip`.

| Stage | What | Requires | Stakes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | in jest, between them | `play` | trivial |
| 2 | seriously, before the squad | `role`, `public_humiliation` 1 | minor |
| 3 | kneeling, before anyone, and she never returns it | `role`, `humiliation` 2, `public_humiliation` 2 | major |

- A public salute counts as P15's `protocol_public` sign (suspicion, the nickname); nothing new.

### C.2 After her (`after_her`), idea 5
- **Rule kind** `after_her` (a P16 rule kind, like P17g's `height`) with `of: eat | sleep | wash`: the commander does it only after she has. Checked from both persons' slots and activities (P2, P14c whereabouts), no model.

| Stage | What | Requires | Stakes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | eats after her | `play`, `service` | trivial |
| 2 | goes to bed after her | `role` | minor |
| 3 | washes after her | `role`, `humiliation` 1 | minor |
| 4 | washes in her used bathwater | `role`, `humiliation` 3 | major |

### C.3 The chamber (`chamber`), idea 14 (urine only, ruling 3)
- **Extends** P17b's `watersports` (drink only from her, the bowl, `gulp`); no new category.
- **Needs:** `watersports` enabled and her `as: enjoys` or more. Frame `role`.

| Stage | What | Requires | Stakes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the commander holds the vessel for her and carries it out | `role`, `service` 2 | major |
| 2 | at night the commander is her chamber pot (the `gulp` physics) | `rule`, `humiliation` 3, `craves` | severe |

### C.4 Her cabin (`cabin`), ideas 15 and 52
- **Today:** `cabinMap` in `src/engine/basePlan.ts` derives cabins from the order of joining (no state); `roomOfPerson(…, 'commander')` already moves the commander for a hold and for `second_place`.
- **New:** a stackable reader `registerCabinOverride` (null without a live custom, so the plan stays byte-identical), fed by `customs.active[].data`.

| Stage | What | Requires | Stakes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | now and then she takes the commander's cabin for the night; the commander sleeps in hers | `play`, `second_place` | minor |
| 2 | the cabins are swapped: the plan names the commander's cabin hers | `role`, `household_protocol` | major |
| 3 | **no cabin** (idea 52): the commander lives as furniture of her room; on the plan the commander is drawn there, default posture `footrest` | `rule`, `objectification` | severe |

- **Cost (call):** at stage 2 a night on her old bunk is ordinary. At stage 3 a night on the floor adds a little commander stress (P7, no draw), unless she kept the commander at her bed that night.
- Each stage is seen by the squad (the plan): memory in §E.

### C.5 Waiting while she gets ready (`attendance`), idea 25

## D. The far end (→ P17g)

### D.1 The whim becomes absolute (`whim` gains stages), idea 67
P17g §2.14's whim (a pointless errand and its reversal) becomes stage 1.

| Stage | What | Requires | Stakes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a pointless errand and its reversal, as today | `play` | trivial |
| 2 | any whim of hers within the enabled categories and her limits; contradictory ("do it — no, undo it — why did you undo it?") | `role` | minor |
| 3 | **absolute**: the whim card has no REFUSE; at night too ("wake up, straighten my pillow") | `rule`, the `private_rule` milestone | severe (accepted once, for the stage) |

- **Absolute (ruling 4):** at stage 3 a whim cannot be disputed. However strange it is, it is not wrong. The card offers only ACCEPT. A whim not done by its deadline is a new offence kind `whim_defied`, entered at the **highest severity** of her discipline. It is not a break and does not end the dynamic.
- **Untouched:** the exit (P15 E2: "it's over" is always possible, at its cost) and the stop word (P15a §9.4).
- The consent is given **once, when the stage is accepted** (a `severe` accept with the signature and the CMDR line: "Every whim. Day N."). After it, each whim card is accepted by its button.
- Pacing: at most one whim a day stays (P17g); a night whim comes as a P5 night entry.

### D.2 Her prerogative (`prerogative`), ideas 69, 71, 72
A custom that grows from her bookings (`book_ahead`) to authority over the commander's calendar. Needs `private_time` (capabilities `book_ahead`), `regal` ×2, frame `role`.

| Stage | What | Requires | Stakes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | now and then she **cancels** a plan of the commander's (a training, a pastime, a free evening): the slot shows "cancelled by her" | `role`, `book_ahead` | minor |
| 2 | she cancels at will, scenes with other women included. The other woman learns it from her, not from the commander | `rule`, `relation_rules` | major |
| 3 | **veto**: everything the commander plans waits for her approval ("waiting for her" in the planner); she approves by mood | `rule`, `private_rule` | severe |
| 4 | **her whim over a run**: she delays the launch of an optional mission (source `side` or `rumor`, never `command` or `story`, never with `spineBeat` or `threadId`) by slots, within its deadline; it may expire, and the squad sees why | `rule`, the stage-3 veto held 14 days | severe |

- **Hooks:** `calendar/cancel_scene` (P14c already hooks it), P9c's booking conflicts (the other woman's booking), a planner status, and a dispatch guard for the launch beside `booking_due` / `held` (P16): `her_veto`. It refuses with her line, and only for a mission the row allows.
- **Cost:** a cancelled scene with another woman counts as the commander's broken plan toward her (P3 deltas through `eventDeltas`, journal phrase). An expired mission fails as P5 says (failure effects).

## E. Memory check (CLAUDE.md rule)

- **Probe answers:** bookkeeping of P15, as today, with no rule. Exception: `hypothetical_answered` gets a `memoryRules` entry (audience: her; spread none; importance low). The commander's words are her fact, quoted back.
- **The joke milestone and the first time below her:** P15's milestones and P15a moments already write what they write; no new rule.
- **A stage taken (`custom_stage { unitId, customId, stage }`):**
  - private stages: a `memoryRules` entry (audience: the pair; importance by stakes: minor low, major mid, severe high);
  - stages seen by others (salute 2–3, spit 3, the cabin 2–3, the mission delay): audience the witnesses or the squad, spread squad, importance mid;
  - rumours outside the squad only with `public_humiliation` (P15 B4);
  - `severe` stages also write the CMDR record line (P16 R.1).
- **`whim_defied`:** her discipline writes its own facts; no rule.
- **A plan cancelled by her** (`plan_cancelled { holderId, otherId? }`): with another woman, a `memoryRules` entry (audience: that woman; spread low); the relation event gets an `eventDeltas[].journal` phrase. Without another person: bookkeeping.
- **A mission expired by her delay** (`run_delayed { holderId, missionId, expired }`): a `memoryRules` entry (audience: the squad; importance mid); P5's failure writes its own report lines.
- **Chronicle:** none of this. **Prompt:** the `customs:` directive (B.5); no `group: 'memory'` provider.

## F. Hidden values and off

- **Hidden** (sentinels in `tests/fixtures/analyze/hidden.ts`): `stale` and her threshold, the next stage and whether it is ready, the probe axis and that a gesture was a probe (P15, as today), whether a whim is contradictory on purpose, the oversight suspicion of `foot_gag` 3.
- **Off is byte-identical:** no customs setting, nothing; a custom without `stages` behaves as today; `probeAxes` rows with `needs` enter no pool without a present `manner` and matching needs; `registerCabinOverride` answers null with no live custom; the `her_veto` guard passes when no `prerogative` stage 3 is live. No rng draw (`dynamicHash`, `dynamicRoll`). A seeded test that moves stops and reports.
- No text gives the commander a pronoun or a feeling.

## G. Calls made by Claude (the author may overrule any)

- Stages are written only for the new customs and `whim` for now; the other P17g customs may get stages later.
- Staleness is a counter on kept occurrences; it does not change upkeep or fading.
- Falling back is one stage at a time.
- The cabin's stress cost at stage 3 only.
- The prerogative's capabilities come from stages of a custom, not from new domains: P15's domain table stays as built.
- The absolute whim is consented to once, at the stage; the cards after it are buttons.
- `deference` and `gaze` take the old "hand to kiss" and "dangling shoe" ideas as gestures, not as separate axes.
