# Sparring finishers (draft, 2026-11-28)

What she does once she has won a sparring bout (`sparring.html`, P16c ruling 10). Approved by the author as a model on 2026-11-28 ("everything fits, I approve"); the numbers, the texts and the list itself are the author's to edit here. P16c rulings 11–12 hold the decisions.

## The scale

Every finisher has a `humiliation` from 0 to 5 (the scale of P16b's presets).

- **0**: ordinary, story only (she helps the commander up).
- **1**: story only; happens with no dynamic when she thinks poorly of the commander (mockery, a shake of the head, a shove on her way out).
- **2 and up**: the dynamic. The cap is her frame: `play` up to 2, `role` up to 4, `rule` up to 5 (as the presets').

## How she chooses

1. **The cap** by who she is to the commander: no dynamic and she thinks well enough of the commander (Affinity ≥ 0) → 0; no dynamic and she thinks poorly of the commander → 1; a dynamic → her frame's cap.
2. **Her level** under the cap by her style: `affectionate` −2 (no real cruelty), `playful` −1 (teasing), `drill` and `ritual` −1 (a lesson, a form), `clinical` −1 and from the "dismissive" kind (she notes the result and leaves), `cold` −1; `cruel`, `vindictive`, `possessive` at the cap; `regal` at the cap, and when the commander landed clean hits on her (an affront) she takes the harshest of her level. Her manner's `intensity` moves it ±1; never past the cap, never under 0.
3. **The finisher** of that level: those that pass the content gate and her limits; weighted up by what her expectations put at `enjoys` / `craves`; with witnesses at the yard (and `public_humiliation` on), weighted up for the ones that show her position (`display`); the pick is a hash (no rng draw).
4. **A finisher with a game** stages its contact with her as the holder in the same scene at the yard: the contact's `implies` sets both postures, and staging starts its physics (the approved instruments). A finisher without a game is one line in the digest for the model to play out (P16c ruling 5).

Off by default, like every category. Candidates to give it more uses (to decide with the author):

- **A burst in the scent game** (`saturation`) where she sits on the commander's face or the commander is under her seat (`face_to_bottom`, `under_her_seat`): a sudden strong stimulus she plays by style, the band jumping for a breath or two.

- **Under her desk** (her activity's frame): a mark heard and felt.
- **A punishment row** (P16): she makes the commander sit through it.
- **Her day** (P14c life events): a line of her own, story only.
