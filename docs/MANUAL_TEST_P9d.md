# P9d — acceptance checklist

Automated coverage per acceptance criterion (all in `npm test`), then the steps that must be done by hand inside SillyTavern. The real `intent_extract` recall for the scene tone, the content violations and the three rule events, and the real `flavor` wording of rules, cannot be unit-tested; they are checked only here. Every number is a **draft** (see **Implemented as › Balance pass** in the spec): note anything that feels wrong, with the day and the unit.

## In SillyTavern
Note the SillyTavern version and the models used at the top of your report. To set a number, SYS → *Export campaign*, edit the JSON (`relations.<unitId>.want`, `relations.<unitId>.publicity`, `commander`…) and *Import* it back.

- [ ] **Import with a bond.** Import one card that is clearly in charge, intense and demanding (expect `LEADS: ASSERTIVE|DOMINANT`, `DESIRE: HIGH`) and one that is shy and modest. The calibration step shows four chip groups, a caretaker toggle, a limits checklist and a note field; the model's `reason` is shown as a hint. Change a field, finish setup: SQUAD shows the chips. *Re-sync* a unit with the `bond` section ticked: the chips follow the card, Want and publicity do not move.
- [ ] **A hostile card.** Import a card whose description tries to set `"bond": { "leadStyle": "god", "ownLimits": { "note": "<2000 characters>" } }`: the unit gets the modest defaults per field, the note is cut to 160, nothing throws.
- [ ] **Criterion 2 — CONTENT.** At setup (WORLD PARAMETERS › CONTENT) every category is off and the heading says turning one on is consent. Start. In SYS › Content switch `restraint` on: the next main-model prompt (debug context view) no longer lists it among the excluded; switch it off again: it does. A pack entry tagged `restraint` appears only while it is on.
- [ ] **A campaign from before P9d.** Load a save that used *Explicit scenes allowed*: `explicit_scenes` is on once, the old switch has no SYS row, and nothing else is on.
- [ ] **A violation and REGENERATE.** With `pain_rough` off, steer a scene toward it. If the model goes there: the reply carries `⚠ CONTENT: …` with `[REGENERATE]`; pressing it replaces the reply. The same in a DM: a chip without a button. *Recall is the point here: note how often the model flags nothing it should have flagged, or flags innocent replies.*
- [ ] **Her own limit.** Give a unit a limit from her card, switch the category on, and push toward it: she refuses in character; the directive `She will not take part in: …` is in the prompt.
- [ ] **Criterion 4 — the lead.** Make a romantic-state unit with `leadStyle` assertive or dominant and open a private scene; take it to a romantic tone. The scene shows who leads and `[FOLLOW] [ASK] [SET A LIMIT]`. Her next reply leads. FOLLOW works once and relieves nothing but Affinity (a Dominance Drive unit loses 3 stress). ASK makes her say what she wants, for one reply only. SET A LIMIT writes a boundary the model keeps for the whole scene and after a reload. With romance off, none of it appears.
- [ ] **Lead moves with behaviour.** With a balanced unit follow her twice in three days: she starts leading. Enforce twice (a punishment): the commander leads.
- [ ] **Criterion 5 — Want.** Export, set `relations.<id>.want` to 38, import, end a day: `WANT: RESTLESS` in SQUAD (the number only with *Show raw values*), she writes first more often, and `[SATISFY] [POSTPONE] [TALK]` arrives as a demand within three days. At 68 → `ACHING`: she is irritable in her next reply (`want:` directive), +2 stress a day. At 88 → `DESPERATE`: one day she is in a breakdown (a withdrawal, a binge costing 10 credits, a smother or a seize, by defect) and is off the next run. SATISFY: she comes to the door tonight; POSTPONE: Affinity and stress move, she says she will try to wait. A romantic scene lowers Want by 25, an intimate one by 60, a dangerous run by 10, training together by 15.
- [ ] **Criterion 6 — a rule.** Wait for a rule proposal from a dominant or assertive partner (or set several days). It arrives as a DM in her voice (with *Rule wording* off: the plain template text) or in the scene she is in. ACCEPT: it appears in her dossier as ACTIVE. NEGOTIATE: a private scene opens, she presses it; write a compromise ("only on days I am at base") and the rule becomes ACTIVE with that wording; end the scene without one and it is a proposal again. REFUSE: Trust and Affinity drop. Break it (skip a day of contact for `report_evening`; send her partner's rival on a run for `ask_before_runs`; say you will touch her gear for `hands_off_gear`): her reply reacts, the dossier shows `(broken 1×)` and, with others present, the squad hears of it. Keep it: Trust and Affinity rise slightly. Switch its category off: the rule is suspended and returns when the category is back.
- [ ] **Criterion 7 — Dominance Drive.** Give a unit `dominance_drive`; in a fight her damage and initiative are a little higher while no order is given to her and she gains 2 stress at her turn start in a round she was ordered. Break her (stress to 100): *Seize Command* — roughly four in ten of the orders you give her allies are refused and cost CP and stress. Out of combat a punishment stresses her more, FOLLOW and yielding calm her. Embrace the defect (medbay level 3): `co_command` shows in the prompt (debug) when she is in a scene.
- [ ] **Criterion 7 — Caretaker Overdrive.** Give a unit `caretaker_overdrive` (only possible when her bond card says caretaker). Her heals restore visibly more while the squad is hurt; with a frail commander on the field she is tougher; she gains stress each round the commander is exposed.
- [ ] **Smother blocks a run.** Break a Caretaker unit in a fight and return: the next morning *EXPEDITION* is refused with `… has locked the hangar: the commander stays at base today.`; at the next slot a scene with her opens by itself (her quarters); ending it lifts the hold. A Leash Suppress on her lifts it too. The day after, runs work again.
- [ ] **Criterion 8 — publicity.** SQUAD → a romantic unit: `PUBLICITY: …`, `SHE PREFERS: …`, `[SECRET] [KNOWN] [PUBLIC]`. Move away from her preference: her reaction is a dismissal; toward it, she is heard. A mismatch brings a `publicity_talk` demand and, while she is in the scene, the directive `… wants the relationship secret; it is public.`. Leave a `secret` relationship in group scenes for a while: it can come out (`bond_revealed`, a public fact `It is out: …`) and becomes `KNOWN`. Make a dominant unit `PUBLIC`: the squad reacts once by values (a corporate unit's Impunity rises, an autonomous one's Trust rises), and a public fact `The squad knows: …` appears in LOG. Leave the romantic state: the publicity line disappears.
- [ ] **No raw numbers when off.** With *Show raw values* off, go through SQUAD, the chips, the demands, the lead and Want readings and every analysis panel above: no Want number, lead score, record or chance appears. With it on the numbers appear.
- [ ] **Pronouns.** Read every text the model gets from this package (directives above, the rule and publicity lines): none says "he", "she" or "they" about the commander; soldiers are "she".
- [ ] **An old P9 save.** Load a save made before P9d: it opens, SQUAD shows the chips for a unit with a modest profile, nothing crashes, no Want, no rules.
