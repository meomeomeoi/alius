# P10 — acceptance checklist

Automated coverage per acceptance criterion (all in `npm test`), then the steps that must be done by hand inside SillyTavern. The automated tests run in jsdom: they cannot judge how the hub, the casings and the voices *look*, whether a real `AudioContext` makes a sound, or what the browser requests; those are the manual lines. The visual look was also checked once in headless Chromium while building (hub at 1440 and 390 px, the three casings, COMMS and LOG, the dither pipeline); that is not a substitute for running it in SillyTavern.

## Automated
1. **The hub is the home screen; rooms, markers, objective/Tonight, resources render, with and without art:** `tests/ui/hub.test.ts`, `tests/ui/hubModel.test.ts`, `tests/engine/hub.test.ts`, `tests/app-hub.test.ts`, `tests/ui/analyzeCoverage.test.ts` (hub), `tests/content/hubSchemas.test.ts`.
2. **The PDA slides over the hub and keeps every tab:** `tests/ui/shellDevice.test.ts`, `tests/ui/shell.test.ts`, `tests/ui/motion.test.ts` (boot, glitch).
3. **Three CSS skins; the skin follows the corporation; an `image` skin positions the screen at two sizes:** `tests/ui/deviceSkins.test.ts`, `tests/ui/deviceChrome.test.ts`, `tests/content/deviceSkinSchemas.test.ts`, `tests/content/core.test.ts`.
4. **Each voice renders through `VoiceCard`; oversight is corporate, DMs personal, dossiers/LOG paper:** `tests/ui/voiceCard.test.ts`, `tests/ui/voices.test.ts`, `tests/app-hub.test.ts` (toast voices).
5. **Dithered portraits render, are cached and the toggle works:** `tests/ui/dither.test.ts`, `tests/ui/portrait.test.ts`, `tests/settings.test.ts`.
6. **Reduced motion and readable mode disable the specified effects:** `tests/ui/motion.test.ts`, `tests/ui/palette.test.ts` (contrast and lightness steps of the semantic colours), `tests/ui/scene.test.ts` (typing), `tests/ui/leashForm.test.ts` (review delay).
7. **No network requests besides extension files:** `tests/assets.test.ts` (fonts exist, no remote url or `@import`, no font CDN in the bundle, licences, size).
8. **Content and rules of the project:** `tests/content/pronouns.test.ts` (no pronoun for the commander in rooms, skins and `src/ui/hub/model.ts`), `tests/content/glossaryCore.test.ts`, `tests/ui/sound.test.ts` (sound), `tests/architecture.test.ts` (`src/engine/hub.ts` is pure).
9. **Old saves:** campaign state is unchanged (`version: 1`, no field); a save from before P10 opens on the hub with the same state.

## In SillyTavern
Note the SillyTavern version, the browser and the window sizes at the top of your report. Run the lines at a desktop width (≥ 1400 px) and again in a narrow window (about 360–400 px wide, or a phone).

- [ ] **Criterion 1 — the hub is home.** Open Alius: the outpost cross-section shows with its rooms, soldiers as rings with initials, the top bar (day/slot, resources, Leash), OBJECTIVE, TONIGHT, `OPEN PDA [TAB]` and `END {SLOT} ▸`. Without art the blueprint grid and `SECTION A-A · NOT TO SCALE` show. Put a `content/core/art/hub/base.png` in place and reload: the image replaces the grid and the rooms stay where they were.
- [ ] **Markers mean something, not only colour.** An injured soldier is in MEDBAY with `!`; a confined one in QUARTERS; a lover shows `♥`; every marker has a glyph. Hover or long-press a marker: the analysis panel explains her relationship state.
- [ ] **Room panel.** Click a room: who is there, `[ TALK ]` for each named soldier, the room's activity cards. `[ TALK ]` opens the scene at that room's location without the picker. A confined soldier's TALK is greyed with the reason. Escape, `[X]` or a click outside closes the panel.
- [ ] **END.** `END MORNING ▸` asks for confirmation and spends one slot; in a quiet night it sleeps instead; with a conversation, an expedition, a decision or a night visitor waiting it is greyed with the reason.
- [ ] **Criterion 2 — the PDA slides over the hub.** `OPEN PDA` or `Tab` raises it with a slide; every tab (SQUAD, OPS, MAP, COMMS, BASE, INV, LOG, SYS) works as before; `Tab` or `Esc` lowers it and focus returns to `OPEN PDA`. `1`–`8` on the hub open that tab. A short boot (three lines, under 0.6 s) plays on the first open and any key skips it.
- [ ] **Review focus 5 — keys while typing.** In the DM box, the scene input, a SYS number field and the state editor: `Tab`, `3` and `Esc` never raise or lower the device or switch tabs. With a modal open, `Esc` closes only the modal; then the next `Esc` lowers the device.
- [ ] **Criterion 3 — skins.** Start a Kestrel-Ward campaign: the steel casing with hazard stripe and hex bolts. A Vesper campaign: white ceramic with the teal ring. Another corporation: the olive-grey CCP casing with screws, bumpers, the `VOID IF OPENED` sticker. SYS › Display › `Device skin` overrides it at once, without reopening. The screen inside is always the phosphor terminal. (An `image` skin: needs author art; with a broken `frameImage` path the CCP casing shows instead.)
- [ ] **Review focus 1 — narrow window and touch.** At 360–400 px the device takes the full width, every room keeps a readable label, the room panel fills the cross-section and its buttons are reachable, and a long-press on a marker opens the analysis panel instead of the room (a tap opens the room).
- [ ] **Criterion 4 — voices.** COMMS: bubbles, hers on the left with her accent colour, yours on the right. LOG: chronicle posts are paper (typewriter font), the oversight log a cold light card. File a Leash request: the form is paper, the verdict a light card headed `{CORPORATION} · OVERSIGHT`. SQUAD unit header, CMDR card and the COMMANDER FILE header are paper. A Leash toast is a light card. Text reads clearly in all four.
- [ ] **Criterion 5 — portraits.** SQUAD shows the neutral sprite (else the avatar) as a dithered picture in the phosphor colours; switch the palette in SYS and it follows; SYS › `Dithered portraits (PDA)` off shows the plain image; scenes keep full colour. A card whose avatar is on another origin shows the plain image and no console error.
- [ ] **Criterion 6 — reduced motion and readable mode.** `Readable mode`: no scanlines, no flicker, no tab glitch, no dithering, text one step larger, colours kept. `Reduce motion` (or the system's reduced-motion setting): the device appears at once, no boot, replies show in full, the oversight form does not play out, the analysis panel shows its text immediately.
- [ ] **Semantic colours.** In all three palettes the ring colours differ in brightness as well as hue, and no meaning is carried by colour alone.
- [ ] **Sound.** Default volume is low and nothing plays at load. After a click: raising/lowering the device, a tab change and a toast each make a small sound; typing ticks while a reply types out; the verdict of a Leash request stamps. SYS › Sound: volume, `Mute`, `Ambient hum` (a very low hum while the shell is open; it stops on close). Nothing plays before the first interaction.
- [ ] **Criterion 7 — no network beyond extension files.** DevTools › Network, cache disabled, hard reload, open Alius and visit every tab: every request is to the extension folder (fonts, scripts, CSS, art) or to SillyTavern itself; nothing goes to a font or CDN host.
- [ ] **Review focus 2 — odd states.** No campaign in the chat (the hub says `NO ACTIVE CAMPAIGN IN THIS CHAT`; OPEN PDA still works), a read-only campaign (banner, no TALK or END), the whole squad out on an expedition (an AWAY strip, empty rooms, END greyed), a scene open while on the hub.
- [ ] **Review focus 3 — bad or missing content.** A pack room with an unknown location or facility, or a skin with a `screenRect` outside its frame, is reported as a content error at load (SYS › Debug), not a crash; a missing hub image or frame image falls back silently.
- [ ] **Review focus 4 — portrait failures.** No sprite, a cross-origin avatar, a canvas-less browser: the plain image shows, no repeated retries within 30 s, no console errors.
