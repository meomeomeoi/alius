# Manual test — P15b: her manner (in SillyTavern)

Play at **1920×1080**, with a campaign whose WORLD PARAMETERS have **The dynamic** on with a practice category (for instance restraint) and **Character development** on `normal`. Use two women whose cards give a practice profile with the **same style** (say `cold`) and **different axes** (give one `warmth −2` and `order +2`, the other `warmth +2` and `order −2`; set the dossier's edit lock off in SYS to edit). Nothing here gives the commander a pronoun. The author's art is never committed. Hover any control with `?` held: the analysis tool explains it.

1. **Two holders of one style.** Open each woman's dossier: PRACTICE shows the same `LEADS AS: COLD` and, under IN THE DYNAMIC, the six axes as five steps each (a step is a short word, `·` at 0), what draws her (`DRAWN BY`) and her quirks; under EVERYDAY her line and the contrast as a word. Nothing shows a number. Start a scene alone with each in the dynamic: the holder directive words them differently (the cold one with no comfort afterwards, the other tender; one by forms and counting, the other on impulse). Run a hold with each: the first gives longer windows and a slower warning, the second quicker ones (`tempo` +), a different share of counted cues and a different number of lines.
2. **A moody holder on two days.** Give a woman a `moody` temperament (WORLD PARAMETERS › LIFE EVENTS on). Play two different days with her: the directive may carry one extra clause about her day (never a number), and her windows, cues or lines differ by a step. The dossier's steps do not change (the mood is not who she is). A failed run the day before, or one of her bad life events, tilts her day the same way; the next day it is gone.
3. **The faces in company.** With her and the commander alone, or during a practice, a physics or a moment of hers: her manner toward the commander (`dynamic`). With another woman present who knows and before whom her `audience` lets her: the manner toward the commander, her everyday self toward the other. With one present who does not know and no romance (or a secret one): she keeps up the appearance (`mask`); with a known romance, her everyday self. In a DM: no face line. Check that her manner no longer leaks into every group scene (the fix).
4. **A quirk once.** Give her `QUIRKS` (`switch`, `during`, `after`, `address`). In a scene with her in the dynamic: the quirk line is worded "if it fits, once"; when the exchange shows it voiced it is gone for that scene and does not come back until a new scene; a `during` quirk waits for a run of hers and an `after` one for its end. A woman with no quirks has no line.
5. **The dossier: edit and lock.** With the edit lock off: press a step of an axis, a `DRAWN BY` kind (a third replaces the second), edit EVERYDAY and a quirk; a text that gives the commander a pronoun is refused. LOCK `manner`, `drawnBy`, `everyday` and `quirks`: a re-sync, a regenerate and a drift step leave the locked fields alone. REGENERATE the practice: the import asks for the new fields (SYS › Output budgets shows `Profile import: practice` 600 and `expectations` 400).
6. **Drift.** Let a few weeks pass with the dynamic in play (or fast-forward the calendar): after 28 days from the day her frame reached `play`, an axis may take a step. It is quiet (no toast, no cut-in); the dossier shows the step she is at now with a `SINCE JOINING` mark only once a scene she is in has ended (a step or a turn no scene has carried, or one a REROLL retracted, is not marked yet), the imported step staying the pressed one; the squad block's since-joining lines tell the drift in her words. UNDO of a step (or an edit) restores it. A locked axis never moves; no axis moves twice within 28 days. With development on `slow` the pace is halved; `off`: nothing moves.
7. **What draws her.** Do what one kind rewards a few times (hold past the warning for `pain`, accept a task at its first offer for `power`, a physics slip that is not a cheat for `reaction`, a clean end for `discipline`, a first practice of a kind for `discovery`): her favour rises a little, and over weeks `DRAWN BY` may take a second place or swap with the first (never replacing the first directly).
8. **The turn of her style.** In a campaign long run (or by editing her axes so her place is nearer another style by the margin), after two weekly reviews in a row and 56 days from `play` her style may turn: her dossier's `LEADS AS` keeps the imported style pressed, marks the style she leads as now with `SINCE JOINING: <STYLE>` (and the second row when the former one became her second), and her directive and every reader use the new style. Her axes are rebased, so nothing jumps. A turn that comes while a hold or a physics of hers runs waits for the run to end.
9. **Probing by manner (§12).** With a `regal` woman with a manner (and another style with a manner, for comparison): in a scene she may try the new gestures in the weeks at `interest` and `testing` (how do I look, going first, telling the commander's plan as hers, holding the look, correcting a praise, feet in the lap for a woman who likes feet, "would you kneel if I asked?"); the regal woman more often. A woman with no manner or matching none keeps the old ones. Answer the what-if: her memory keeps what was answered ("you said you would"), and a later scene may quote it. The commander's praise three times in two weeks wakes a latent regal woman who was not probing.
10. **The joke and the first time below her.** For a `regal` woman at `testing`, the card that opens (`scene` form) is the joke that stopped being a joke, not the first yes; refusing it cools it down and the first yes may open once her hold is back. At `testing`, in a scene with a close relation, `first_below` may come up once (her lace after a run): three options, never again for that pair.
11. **Off.** A woman imported before this package, or with no manner: the dossier shows her style only (the axes appear when a step is pressed), nothing in her scenes changes except the face rule, and her probes, milestones and moments are the ones she always had.
12. **The instruments.** In each mini-game (breath, weight, scent, her activity, the jaw, shoe service, gulp, the slap, the posture call) with a holder with a manner and one without: the one with a manner has a slightly different pace, warning and number of lines; with every axis at 0 it plays like the prototype.

Also check at 1280×800 and 2560×1440 that the PRACTICE block's new rows are not cut off or covered (`npm run test:layout`), and that no hidden value (a number of a step's accumulator, the mood, how near a turn is, which quirk was voiced, a style's place on the axes) appears in any panel, toast or reading.

Report a number where a word belongs, a holder with no manner whose scenes changed, her manner leaking into a scene before others who do not know, a drift step that came with a toast, a locked field that moved, a turn in the middle of a run, a control without `data-analyze`, or a pronoun for the commander.
