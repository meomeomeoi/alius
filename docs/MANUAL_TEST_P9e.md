## In SillyTavern
Note the SillyTavern version at the top of your report. To make a soldier bold, import a card with `LEADS: ASSERTIVE` or `DOMINANT`, or raise her Impunity (SYS → *Export campaign*, edit `relations.<unitId>.impunityRecord`, *Import*). A her-call happens with chance 0.35 at each event node of a template she qualifies for (more at *Squad assertiveness: high*): play several runs.

- [ ] **An assertive soldier triggers a notice card.** Take an assertive soldier on a run with several event nodes. Sooner or later an event node shows a card in her colour: `{Name} took the decision herself: …` with only `[ ACKNOWLEDGE ]`, no options. The effects (supplies, stress, wounds, loot) are already in the run when the card shows; ACKNOWLEDGE only closes the node. Reloading the page shows the same card and applies nothing twice.
- [ ] **A yielding squad never does.** A squad of yielding or balanced soldiers with disciplined stances sees ordinary events only, with options to pick.
- [ ] **A good call is hers.** On a good call: merit for her (a rise in STANDING in her dossier after the run), a little more Impunity, a line in the squad's memory; no chip on the mission and no oversight line on the card.
- [ ] **A bad call shows the chip and the oversight line.** On a bad call on a command mission the card adds `Oversight will want a name if this run fails.`, the run view and the mission card show `ON HER CALL: {Name}`. A side run or a point of interest is never marked: no line, no chip.
- [ ] **A failed run opens the case with the her-call wording.** Let the marked run fail (or retreat, put it back on the board and let its deadline expire): the decision panel names her as RESPONSIBLE and the setup reads `{Name} made the call herself in the field.`; both TAKE THE BLAME and SHIFT IT work as in P9b (with voting on, the answer goes to a vote first). A retreat on its own opens nothing.
- [ ] **A vote mark is kept.** With voting on, start a command mission on a passed vote, then let a her-call go badly: the case is still about the soldier who carried the vote (the setup says she carried the squad's vote).
- [ ] **ASK ME FIRST costs Trust and stops her calls.** In her dossier press `[ ASK ME FIRST ]`: her Trust falls once (CMDR/analysis shows the minor `decision_violated`), the chip `ASKS FIRST` appears and the button reads `[ LET HER DECIDE ]`. In a scene with her the main model treats it as a standing order. Take her on several runs: she never acts on her own. `[ LET HER DECIDE ]` is free. During a run the button is greyed: the order is given at the base.
- [ ] **A directive after the run.** After a run in which she decided, a scene with her mentions it once (`took the decision herself in the field; it went well/badly`) and the line ends with the scene.
- [ ] **Analysis.** Hover or press `?` on the card, the chip and the ASK ME FIRST button: each explains in words; with *Show raw values* on, the card's panel also shows the odds it would have gone well.
- [ ] **An old save plays as before.** Load a save from before P9e, ideally mid-run with a pending event and a vote mark: it loads, the event is answered as before, and the scapegoat text for the vote mark is the vote wording.
- [ ] **No commander pronoun anywhere.** Read the card, the chip, the directives, the glossary entries and the scapegoat setup: the commander is "the commander" or "you", never he/him/his.
