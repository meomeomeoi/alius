# Manual test — P15a scene blocking, moments and contact physics (in SillyTavern)

Run in a fresh campaign with four imported soldiers. Take one soldier to bond tier `close` (a confidant) and keep another a comrade. In SYS › Content turn on `restraint`, `worship`, `humiliation` and `service`; leave `breath_play` off for now. Turn **Debug mode** on (SYS › Debug lists every call to the mechanic model with its prompt; the `[BLOCKING]` block is in the main prompt: look at it in SillyTavern's prompt inspector).
The blocking is quiet: chips, a strip and a few lines of text. Nothing here shows a number she keeps (her skills, her rhythm, what she expects) and nothing gives the commander a pronoun.

1. **A confidant scene.** Open a scene with the confidant (a private place, e.g. the commander's quarters). The BLOCKING strip appears under the transcript with the commander's movement chips and `[ + ]`. With nothing set, the main prompt has no `[BLOCKING]` block. Pick a posture (SIT) and a place (AT THE DESK) with `[ + ]` and a worn item: the strip shows the chips, the next prompt carries `[BLOCKING]` with one line per person and the extractor's prompt (SYS › Debug) lists the closed vocabulary. A chip can be examined with `?` and says who set it and in which exchange.
2. **A scene with a comrade.** Open a scene with the one who is only a comrade. No strip, no `[BLOCKING]`, no moment, no physics, and the extractor's prompt is the same as before P15a (no staging block). The same for a scene with a grunt.
3. **A swipe after a chip.** Set a posture, send a message, then SWIPE the reply and read the strip: what you set after that reply is gone, what was set before it stays. Edit or delete a message the same way. A swipe during a running hold (step 6) changes nothing of the hold.
4. **A blindfold and the POV.** Put a blindfold (or a hood) on the commander. The next reply is written from what the commander can perceive: touch, sound, smell, no sight. Take it off and the line goes. A hood also muffles the interface sounds.
5. **Paw mitts lock the PDA.** Put paw mitts on the commander. Every tab of the device is locked and the scene says "The device is out of reach." Take the mitts off and the tabs open again; a tab the prologue keeps locked stays locked.
6. **A moment.** Let a visitor moment come (a third person at the door while something is set) or raise one with a few exchanges. The moment card shows three chips (hide / as is / show yourself, with the generic words first and the model's own labels from the next reading) and, where it applies, ASK HER TO LOCK THE DOOR / ASK HER WITH A LOOK. Choose one; the next reply reacts to it and nothing tells which answer she expected. Nothing outside the moment is moved by the choice except what the engine writes (a relation, a remembered event).
7. **Immersive mode.** SYS › Scene › Moments as `immersive`: the chips go, one dim line says something is at stake, and a free-text answer is read as the choice.
8. **A ring gag in both modes.** Put a ring gag on the commander (or a contact that holds the jaw). The physics panel appears with its gauge as a word, BREATHE / HOLD STILL buttons, `[ TAP ]` and `[ STOP WORD ]`. Turn-based (default): each press is one beat and the segment ends when the beats are used up. SYS › Scene › Real-time contact: the hold runs on a clock and Space is the main input; typing a message lapses the beats under it. Both modes: a low gauge shakes the screen unless motion is reduced.
9. **A slow model during a hold.** Start a hold and let the reply take a long time (or pause the mechanic model). The hold goes on and finishes its segments; the engine never waits for the model, and the outcome is the same if the model is quick. The stop word ends the hold at every moment: mid-segment, held, between phases, in either mode. The tap answers by her profile (at first she heeds it). Switch chat during a hold: the clock stops and nothing is dispatched into the new chat.
10. **Endurance drills.** On OPS › TRAIN the drills ENDURANCE: BREATH / WEIGHT / JAW can be done once a day each. The CMDR card shows `HOLDS — …` with a word for each (never a number); the same hold now lasts longer.
11. **A category off mid-scene.** While the ring gag is on and a hold is running, SYS › Content: turn `restraint` off. The worn item and its contact are stripped, the hold ends as stopped, and nothing tagged with it is offered again. Turn off `bodily_fluids` while `watersports` is on: both go off. `watersports` cannot be turned on while `bodily_fluids` is off (the row says "needs Bodily fluids").
12. **No close relation looks as before.** A campaign (or a week) where nobody is above comrade: no strip, no card, no panel, no new line in any prompt and the same day to day as before this package. Old saves load without a migration.

Report anything that shows a number she keeps, a line that tells which answer she expects, a hold that waits for the model, a stop word that does not stop, a tag that is switched off and still offered, or a pronoun for the commander.
