# P8 — acceptance checklist

Automated coverage per acceptance criterion (all in `npm test`), then the steps that must be done by hand inside SillyTavern.

## Automated
1. **Options are stored, locked after start (except `live`), and their lore lines appear in the context block within the token cap:** `tests/engine/worldOptions/options.test.ts` (`set_option refuses a locked option and changes nothing`, `buildCampaign stores every option…`), `tests/world/optionsContext.test.ts` (`the block lists the current lore lines in option order and fits 120 tokens`), `tests/app-world.test.ts` (`world options in the injected context`).
2. **`intent_extract` prompts are byte-identical across disposition levels:** `tests/scene/sceneController.test.ts` (`intent_extract prompts are byte-identical across disposition levels, offsets and stances`).
3. **The same intents give the documented differences between `kind` and `hazardous`:** `tests/engine/relations/disposition.test.ts` (multipliers, confidence floors, big gesture, stage timing), `tests/engine/relations/bands.test.ts` (shifted loyalty bands), `tests/engine/worldOptions/disposition.test.ts` (join offsets at the three join sites).
4. **Per-unit offset changes only that unit's effective level:** `tests/engine/worldOptions/disposition.test.ts` (`per-unit offset changes only that unit's effective level`, `offset clamps at both ends and grunts ignore it`), `tests/engine/loyalty/disposition.test.ts`.
5. **Mutiny is impossible at `kind`, possible from `resentful` at `volatile`+:** `tests/engine/loyalty/mutiny.test.ts` (`mutiny follows disposition (P8)`).
6. **`gender_norms` reaches a unit only through her stance; a `shares` unit's effect decays with days known (d = 0, 7, 21); stances are editable and persist:** `tests/engine/worldOptions/stances.test.ts`, `tests/ui/setup.test.ts` (`calibration edits offset and stances and they persist into build`), `tests/setup/wizard.test.ts` (`build carries the world, offsets and stances`).
7. **`oversight` changes Leash authorizations; `bloom_horror` can be switched mid-campaign:** `tests/engine/worldOptions/hooks.test.ts`, `tests/ui/shell.test.ts` (`SYS lists options read-only and switches bloom_horror`), `tests/app-world.test.ts`.
8. **Presets set every option; manual changes after a preset persist:** `tests/setup/wizard.test.ts` (`presets set every option`, `manual changes after a preset persist across back/next`), `tests/ui/worldParams.test.ts`.
9. **Review focus:** an old save without options (`options.test.ts` `an old save without options reads every default`, `disposition.test.ts` `a P7 save reads guarded with offset 0`); offsets at the edges (`offset clamps at both ends and grunts ignore it`); a live switch mid-scene (`optionsContext.test.ts` `switching bloom_horror replaces its directive`); the confidence setting at 0 and 1 (`relations/disposition.test.ts` `floor never exceeds any per-axis floor`); a disabled family left in a save (`families.test.ts` `a family clock in a save survives with the family off`).
10. **Import:** `tests/setup/profile.test.ts` (`the world sections of the import (P8)`), `tests/app-resync.test.ts` (`re-sync of the world sections`), `tests/app-world.test.ts` (`intake asks for stances under hunted…`).
11. **Analysis:** `tests/analysis/worldReading.test.ts`, `tests/analysis/hidden.test.ts` (runs over the world readings), `tests/ui/analyzeCoverage.test.ts` (`coverage: world parameters and disposition controls`).
12. **Balance harness:** `npm run sim -- --world brutal` (`tests/sim/harness.test.ts` `the world in the harness`).

## In SillyTavern
- [ ] **NEW CAMPAIGN → WORLD PARAMETERS:** after the dossier a step `02 WORLD PARAMETERS` shows presets (Gentle, Standard, Brutal; Standard is pressed), the option groups (SOCIETY, CORPORATION, PRESENTATION, SOLDIERS) with a choice card each, and `LOCKED AT START` on every option except Bloom horror (`CHANGEABLE LATER`). A preset press selects all six choices; changing one card unpresses the preset. `WHAT THIS CHANGES` lists a plain-language line per choice that differs from the default and never a number. Hover a card and the preset buttons: an explanation opens, nothing reads `NO DATA`.
- [ ] **Back and forth:** Next to the commander file, Back to the world step: your choices are still there.
- [ ] **Calibration, offset and stances:** with a card imported in a non-default world, the section `DISPOSITION` shows `World level: …`, five chips (−2 … +2) named after the level each makes her, the card's proposed reason and an editable `Reason`. In a world with `corporate_machismo`, `inverted`, `scorned` or `hunted`, the section `STANCE TOWARD THE WORLD` shows one row per such option with `Shares it / Indifferent / Rejects it`, `Mild / Strong` (greyed while indifferent) and a `Reason`. In a neutral world the stance section is absent. REGENERATE works on both sections.
- [ ] **A world change after calibration:** go back, change the world, return to the calibration: a unit shows `The world changed since this unit was imported` and `[ RE-IMPORT FOR THIS WORLD ]`; pressing it refreshes only the offset and the stances.
- [ ] **The WORLD SETTINGS block:** open a scene (or just check the context in the debug tools): the injected context carries `World settings: …` with one short line per option, outside a scene too. With Show raw values off nothing there is a number.
- [ ] **A live switch:** SYS › World options › `Bloom horror` → graphic. The next reply describes mutants and wounds more explicitly; the context line changes; switching back restores the restrained directive. The other five options are read-only text with `LOCKED AT START`.
- [ ] **Oversight:** in a `brutal` world the Leash shows one fewer authorization per week than the corporation's base; in a `lenient` world one more. A failed command mission costs more (or less) standing with its corporation.
- [ ] **Stigma:** under `scorned` mission rewards are about a tenth lower; under `hunted` a `Purge sweep` clock appears in the world, fills slowly (rumors, the director and the `Extraction`, `The census`, `Shelter` and `Sweep checkpoint` content), and when it fills every unit takes stress, more for a unit whose stance says she shares the stigma.
- [ ] **Gender norms:** in a `corporate_machismo` world start with a very feminine commander: your own corporation's standing starts lower (SYS › debug state), a unit whose stance is `shares` starts cooler and warms over about a week, a unit that `rejects` the norm starts slightly warmer. A unit that is `indifferent` is unaffected.
- [ ] **A hazardous run:** `Brutal` preset with a short campaign: Trust is slow to win and quick to lose, units refuse and desert more, a breakdown in a fight can turn on an ally or the commander (the combat log says `turns on herself` at the kind end), and one warning is enough for a mutiny. At the `Gentle` end the opposite holds. The SQUAD card shows `Disposition: <level>` for every unit; hover it for the explanation (no numbers unless Show raw values is on).
- [ ] **An old campaign:** load a P7 save: it plays as before (guarded, no stances); SYS lists every option as `LOCKED AT START`; Re-sync from card offers `How hard or kind she is for this world (disposition offset)` and, if a norm is non-neutral, the stance section.
- [ ] **Re-sync keeps the numbers:** `Re-sync from card` with the disposition and stance sections ticked changes the offset and stances only; Trust, Affinity and Attraction are untouched.
