# P10b — acceptance checklist

Automated coverage per acceptance criterion (all in `npm test`), then the steps that must be done by hand inside SillyTavern. The automated tests run in jsdom: they cannot judge how the telemetry, the night colours, the frosted door, the hand fonts or the stickers *look*, whether a real `AudioContext` plays the knock, or what the model really writes for the new `door` and `curt` modes; those are the manual lines.

## Automated
1. **VITALS renders lines from state; spikes and the Affect Dampener flicker appear; nothing hidden leaks:** `tests/engine/vitals.test.ts` (patterns, causes, flicker, hidden injury), `tests/ui/vitals.test.ts` (block, strip, trace, no digit outside the band texts), `tests/analysis/immersionProviders.test.ts` (reading in words; the hidden fixture over every registered provider; a hidden injury shows no fever).
2. **DM behaviours fire by their rules; voice messages reveal their text on click:** `tests/content/dmBehaviours.test.ts`, `tests/engine/dmBehaviours.test.ts` (stamp at receipt, curt, hesitation, receipts), `tests/llm/dmReply.test.ts` (`curt` and `door` wording), `tests/scene/comms.test.ts` (curt reply stamped), `tests/ui/livingDms.test.ts` (placeholder, burst, voice, LATE, receipts, typing, contact name).
3. **The night theme only in the night slot; the door with timer and three choices; silence = she leaves with consequences:** `tests/ui/shell.test.ts` (`data-night`), `tests/ui/palette.test.ts` (night contrast), `tests/ui/sound.test.ts` (`NIGHT_GAIN`), `tests/engine/door.test.ts`, `tests/ui/door.test.ts` (options, timer and its pauses, one exchange, silhouette), `tests/scene/comms.test.ts` (`throughDoor`, chat switch), `tests/app-immersion.test.ts` (a `want_visit` knock is a door, a confrontation keeps its panel), `tests/engine/notes.test.ts` (an unanswered knock leaves a note).
4. **Handwriting fonts assigned and changeable; notes on the hub; rule notes sign and tear:** `tests/engine/hand.test.ts`, `tests/ui/squad.test.ts` (HAND select), `tests/assets.test.ts` (fonts, licences), `tests/content/handNotes.test.ts`, `tests/engine/notes.test.ts`, `tests/ui/notes.test.ts` (note, rule note, quarters panel, INV), `tests/ui/hubModel.test.ts` (TONIGHT item).
5. **Stickers, wallpaper, contact name and pinned notes follow the access levels; peeling is confirmed and reported:** `tests/ui/home.test.ts`, `tests/ui/deviceMarks.test.ts`, `tests/app-immersion.test.ts` (PEEL / KEEP, CHANGE WALLPAPER, the HOME badge).
6. **Reduced motion and readable mode also stop night, door and typing animations:** the `motion` cases in `tests/ui/vitals.test.ts` (sweep), `tests/ui/livingDms.test.ts` (typing), `tests/ui/door.test.ts` (shake, bar, blur), `tests/ui/notes.test.ts` (tear), `tests/ui/home.test.ts` (wallpaper caption), `tests/ui/palette.test.ts` (night motion scale).
7. **Project rules:** `tests/content/pronouns.test.ts` (no pronoun for the commander in the new registries, glossary entries, reading texts and the `curt` / `door` modes), `tests/ui/analyzeCoverage.test.ts` and the coverage checks in each UI test file, `tests/architecture.test.ts`, `tests/assets.test.ts` (nothing from the network).
8. **Old saves:** every new field is optional and `version` stays 1: `notes`, `Unit.hand`, `DmMessage.behaviours`, `NightEntry.door`.

## In SillyTavern
Note the SillyTavern version, the browser and the window sizes at the top of your report. Run the lines at a desktop width (≥ 1400 px) and again in a narrow window (about 360–400 px).

- [ ] **Criterion 1 — VITALS.** SQUAD: every named soldier has a VITALS block (a trace, a pattern chip, `HR` and `TEMP`). Put one under stress 50 and one at 80: the pattern changes (ELEVATED, ERRATIC). Fight: the soldiers who fought show SPIKING for the day. A soldier with the Affect Dampener shows a FLAT LINE and a log of flickers (`DAY 005 MORNING`) after a warm moment. The hub's right column shows one small trace per named soldier under TONIGHT. Hover a chip: the reading says what the pattern can mean in words, with no number, with raw values on or off. A soldier hiding an injury shows no fever.
- [ ] **Criterion 2 — living DMs.** In COMMS watch a few days of messages with a soldier who is wanting and one who is resentful. Look for: `MESSAGE DELETED` before a bubble; a message split into two or three bubbles; a `VOICE MESSAGE` with `[PLAY]` (the words show only after the click and stay while the tab stays); `LATE` on a message she started in the evening or at night; the receipt under your last message (`DELIVERED`, `READ`, `READ · 2 SLOTS AGO`, `SEEN · NO REPLY`); `typing…` that stops. A resentful soldier answers in one or two words (**read what the model really writes in `curt` mode** and judge whether it is cold and short, not rude past the content settings). A contact with the `contact` decor shows her chosen name in the list and the real name in brackets in the header.
- [ ] **Criterion 3 — night.** When the slot becomes Night the whole shell turns warmer and dimmer, transitions are slower and the sounds quieter; in the morning it is back. **Judge the colours on all three palettes** (amber, green, white) and in readable mode (no dimming, the label stays). A knock that is not a confrontation is the door: a frosted pane with her blurred sprite (the ASCII door without a card), a short shake and a knock (with sound on, one knock; check it is not too loud at night). `[OPEN]`, `[SAY SOMETHING THROUGH THE DOOR]`, `[STAY SILENT]`. Let the 15 s timer run out: it counts as staying silent. Type through the door and wait: the timer waits while you type and while she answers. **Read what the model writes in `door` mode** (one or two sentences, still deciding). After the exchange only OPEN and STAY SILENT remain. Reload the page mid-knock: the exchange is still there. A mutiny or desertion at night still shows its own panel, not the door. SYS › Display › `Door timer`: 0 removes the timer, 60 is a minute.
- [ ] **Criterion 4 — handwriting.** SQUAD › HAND: six fonts (Caveat, Indie Flower, Patrick Hand, Shadows Into Light, Kalam, Nanum Pen Script) each render in the note (check the files load: no 404 in DevTools, no request to a font CDN) and the choice persists after a reload. In the room with the commander's cell (BARRACKS) the quarters notes wait with `[GOT IT]`; the INV tab starts with gear notes; an unanswered knock leaves a note under the door that shows in TONIGHT next morning and on HOME. A proposed rule (P9d) shows as her signed note with `[SIGN]`, `[NEGOTIATE]`, `[DON'T SIGN]`; an active one is signed by both; a rule broken since yesterday shows torn (the tear plays once). Readable mode shows the note in the type font.
- [ ] **Criterion 5 — device marks.** HOME is the first tab and the one the device opens on; `0` opens it, `1`–`8` keep SQUAD … SYS. It shows the wallpaper (her dithered portrait, faded and grey while her decor fades; readable mode shows only `WALLPAPER: NAME`), the day and slot, one `ACCESS L{n} NAME` line per soldier with access, and every note. Stickers sit on the casing: **check the three spots on each skin (CCP, Kestrel-Ward, Vesper) and on a narrow window: no sticker over the screen, the LED, the hazard stripe, the bolts or the home button; adjust `stickerSpots` in `content/core/deviceSkins.json` if one does.** `[PEEL STICKER]`, `[CHANGE WALLPAPER]` and the dossier `[PEEL]` all ask `PEEL HER {PART}?` first; KEEP changes nothing; PEEL removes the part and the soldier reacts. Stickers cannot be clicked. After a break-up the sticker and the wallpaper fade for a week.
- [ ] **Criterion 6 — reduced motion and readable mode.** With `Reduce motion` on: the VITALS trace is still, `typing…` is the static line `She started typing and stopped.`, the door does not shake and its bar jumps, the torn note is static, the night transitions are not slower. With `Readable mode` on: no blur on the door (a solid shape), no wallpaper image, hands replaced by the type font, night keeps its label but no dimming.
- [ ] **Narrow window.** At 360–400 px: HOME's notes and access lines wrap, the door's three options and the input stay reachable, the VITALS strip does not push END out of the side column, nothing scrolls the view sideways.
- [ ] **Old save.** Load a save from before P10b: it opens on HOME, nothing breaks, no note exists, every soldier has a default hand and sticker.
