# Manual test — P14c her life (in SillyTavern)

Run in a fresh campaign with LIFE EVENTS on NORMAL (the default of the WORLD PARAMETERS step) and **Units may start DMs** on (SYS). Four soldiers imported from cards, at least one with a TEMPERAMENT of `talkative` and one `silent`; give two of them a hobby and an opinion or two in the calibration.
Everything of her days is quiet: no toast, no cut-in, no chip. You should hear of it only from her, a DM, a note, or a line in the hub.

1. **Import with the three sections.** In the calibration check TEMPERAMENT, HOBBIES (one to three, with the GIFTED AT / HOPELESS AT lists) and OPINIONS (eight to twelve). Each has its REGENERATE; an overlap between gifted and hopeless is refused. In the dossier (SQUAD) the same blocks are read-only with the mechanics edit lock on: aptitudes are not drawn, an opinion she has not said is not listed.
2. **A week at NORMAL.** Play a week of ordinary days (activities, one or two scenes). About one news item a day squad-wide, never two soldiers on consecutive days, never the same kind twice in a row. A talkative soldier writes a DM in her own voice; for another, open a scene and she mentions what happened to her **once**: the line is gone from the prompt after she says it (check the next replies: she does not repeat it).
3. **The hub line.** On HOME / the plan a marker shows a line on hover for two days after something happened to her (not for what she keeps to herself). It also stands under her name in the room panel.
4. **A hobby milestone in her hand.** Keep a hobby going (put her where it is practised: her quarters, the workshop, the mess) for a few weeks. A milestone with a note trace leaves a note in your quarters in her handwriting (HOME › notes). In SQUAD › WHAT SHE DOES the milestone appears **only after it was told**, with a word (KEEN, STEADY, COOLING, PUT AWAY) and never a number; a prop trace reads "in her quarters: …".
5. **An opinion raised by a keyword.** In a scene say a word from one of her topics (the mess for food, the training yard for weapons, or the topic's label). She holds a view (formed on the spot if she had none). She says it **once** and it is not repeated in the next replies; THOUGHTS in her dossier now lists it in her words.
6. **A challenge over several scenes.** Argue against that view in three or four scenes. Her position moves **one step**, never two; THOUGHTS shows "She used to think: …". A `strong` opinion may harden instead ("will not change her mind"); an opinion you agree with a little warms her.
7. **A quiet hour.** On OPS › TIME TOGETHER (or the room panel `[ TIME TOGETHER ]`) pick a soldier and a pastime; the scene opens at the pastime's place and the slot is spent when it ends (END TRANSMISSION). Her Affinity rises a little and her stress drops. Inside any private scene with one soldier, `[ LET'S … ]` offers the pastimes that fit the place and the slot. A silent soldier lists the quiet ones first. Her hobby as a pastime: your hobby word on the CMDR card moves from NOT YET to A BEGINNER and on.
8. **A first moment.** In a private scene let something happen for the first time (a real laugh, tears, her past). It is kept once: the second time nothing changes. It reaches her dossier as a remembered fact.
9. **A running joke.** Bring the same remembered moment back three times inside a month. In a later private scene she may bring it back herself, lightly, once; the line is not repeated.
10. **SYS › World › Life events.** Turn it OFF: nothing new is picked, practised, queued or formed; everything already written stays. Turn it ON again later: it carries on. A campaign made before P14c reads OFF and is untouched until you pick a frequency.

Report anything that shows a number from the hobby or the opinion, an event she has not told, a line she repeats, a toast for her days, or a pronoun for the commander.
