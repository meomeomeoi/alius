## In SillyTavern
Note the SillyTavern version and the models used at the top of your report.

- [ ] **Criterion 1 — Impunity in words.** SQUAD → a unit → the `STANCE` chip and `Impunity: …` (a word: cowed / low / high / brazen). Hover or `?` on it: the panel names the main terms in words. With *Show raw values* off there is no number anywhere; with it on the numbers appear.
- [ ] **A fresh squad is not all fearful.** Start a campaign in the Standard world and import four different cards: the wizard (calibration and review) shows a `STARTING STANCE` line per unit; at least two different stances appear across a varied cast (e.g. a wary card `fearful`, a neutral one `indifferent`, an accepting one `disciplined`). Changing the disposition offset chip changes the stance line.
- [ ] **Acquaintance period.** WORLD PARAMETERS → SOLDIERS → `Acquaintance`: the slider runs 0…14 days. Set 14, play three days with a `fearful` unit: no covert sabotage and no demand arrives. Set 0 in a new campaign: they may.
- [ ] **Criterion 2 — a threat that lapses.** In a scene write a clear threat of the brig; the chip `THREAT REGISTERED — Brig (…) · …` appears (always, with raw values off). Do nothing; end the scene; advance two day ends. A toast says the threat lapsed and SQUAD shows her Impunity band unchanged or higher.
- [ ] **Criterion 5 — believes or not.** Repeat the threat with a cowed unit (recently punished, Leash used) and a brazen one: the chip says `She believes it` for the first and `She doesn't buy it` for the second, and her next reply acts accordingly.
- [ ] **Criterion 3 — stances in play.** Give an order to a `fearful` unit that she would refuse: the order shows as accepted, no refusal banner, her reply complies in silence; a day or two later an anonymous fact (e.g. `The supply count came up short. Nobody saw anything.`) and, in debug mode, a toast. No panel names who did it. A `defiant_loyal` or `open_defiance` unit refuses openly in her own form.
- [ ] **Criterion 4 — the menu.** SQUAD → unit → `DISCIPLINE` (and the scene-UI `DISCIPLINE` button): every measure is listed with its words; pick `Reprimand`: her next reply reacts to it in her stance; SQUAD shows the new Impunity band. Pick `Brig`: choose the days; she is unavailable (no scene, no companion, no expedition) and the squad screen says why. `Bench`: she is missing from the next expedition party. `Revoke leave`: she cannot be a companion except for `talk` and `medbay`. `Extra duty`: she is unavailable for one slot.
- [ ] **Criterion 4 — dialogue and UNDO.** In a scene write that you take away her rations; the chip `DISCIPLINE LOGGED — Rations revoked (…)` with `[UNDO — not what I meant]` appears. Press UNDO: the chip goes, her Trust/Affinity/stress and Impunity band return to what they were, and the chronicle/memory shows no entry for it (LOG may show both lines). Do the same in a DM thread: the chip shows in COMMS.
- [ ] **A threat read from a DM.** Write a threat in a DM; the chip appears in the thread; UNDO removes the threat.
- [ ] **The Leash is an enforcement.** File a Compel on a unit who defied: her Impunity band drops and her held defiance does not raise it afterwards.
- [ ] **Criterion 6 — a demand by DM.** After a few days a unit DMs a demand with chips `[GRANT] [REFUSE] [TALK]`; the unread count includes it. Words: with *Demand wording* on (SYS › Relationships) it is in her voice; off, it is the plain template text. `[TALK]` opens a private scene with the demand pressed; the chips stay. `[GRANT]` pays the cost (the credits, the supply, the item); `[REFUSE]` costs Trust/Affinity/stress, more at high Impunity. Ignore one for two days: it counts as refused.
- [ ] **A delegate one band short.** Delegate a task to a unit one Trust band short: she does it, and an `OWED` demand follows.
- [ ] **Criterion 7 — the first nickname.** Use the Leash four times (or enough punishments, or lose three conscripts, or protect three units); at the next day end a nickname is queued; in the next **private** scene with one named unit she calls the commander by it ("…for the first time"). A group scene does not introduce it. Afterwards the CMDR card shows `REPUTATION — ` and a chip (hover explains it), and the main model's commander line carries `Known in the squad as "…"` (debug context view). It carries no digit and no pronoun.
- [ ] **Nickname replacement.** Stop earning it for a week while a stronger pattern of the same kind grows: the new one is introduced the same way and replaces it.
- [ ] **Criterion 8 — disposition.** Same unit, same events in a `kind` world and in a `hazardous` one: Impunity bases differ in the readings and the same punishment costs more in the harsher world.
- [ ] **Mutiny and desertion.** A unit with high Trust never mutinies; a `breaking`, brazen unit can; a `breaking`, cowed one deserts instead.
- [ ] **An old P8 save.** Load a save made before P9: it opens, SQUAD shows stances, nothing crashes, and Impunity starts from the base with no record. The `acquaintance` option reads its default.
- [ ] **No raw numbers when off.** With *Show raw values* off, go through SQUAD, the DISCIPLINE menu, chips, COMMS, the CMDR card and every analysis panel above: not one Impunity, record, deed counter or chance appears.
