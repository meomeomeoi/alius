# P16c — Service Mini-games

**Status: spec (stub).** Release 0.2, after P16b. **Depends on:** P16b (the disciplines, fiction time, records, lessons, the posture game, the drills), P10e chunk (e) (the instrument pattern, normative prototypes), P15a, P16. **Read:** overview; P16b (its **Rulings of the planning session** and, once built, **Implemented as**); `docs/prototypes/README.md`; `docs/content/lore.md` (§0).

Origin: the P16b planning session of 2026-11-27 (author, ruling 1): these games were specified in P16b §6, §8, §9 and P16 §R.2 and moved here so that each is designed with the author as a live prototype first (`npm run proto`), then ported with its `fidelity.json`. Every new scene surface follows the scene's style (P16b ruling 2).

## Author's rulings so far (2026-11-27, before the plan; the plan's "Rulings of the planning session" takes them over)

1. **Each game is built together with its stage setup (author).** A game's port comes in the same chunk as its setup in the extension (P10e chunk (f), SYS › STAGE): its art slots enter `SLOT_KEYS` / `SLOT_INFO`, its prototype's fit panel is ported into `src/ui/stage/fit/` (declared in `src/ui/stage/fidelity.json`), its zones use the ported marker, and CHECK runs it on a `demo.ts` builder. A game is not finished while its art cannot be loaded, fitted, marked and checked in SYS › STAGE.
2. **Foot care: where (author: A).** The commander holds her foot up, sole to the face (art slot `held_sole`: one foot, the other mirrored; the leg fades out at the bottom; optional `held_sole.curled`, the same picture with the toes curled, an inpaint); she sits back on her bunk, her sprite behind the foot to one side; her face (her expression sprites) is a tell.
3. **Foot care: steps.** Round 1 starts barefoot (massage with her tempo, then cream); taking off her footwear waits for a matched pair of pictures (`held_sole.shod`, the footwear inpainted over the bare picture) so the shoe can slide off over the bare foot.
4. **Her faces in a prototype** are matched by the label a file name begins with (`admiration0003.png`, `joy-2.webp`), several per label allowed; the bottom of her sprite may dissolve into the room like the foot's leg (optional, `fade` in her fit, 0 = none); the change of face crossfades (author: kept). **Both are stage settings** (SYS › STAGE, her look in `stage.json`): whether her face crossfades and how long, and her sprite's bottom fade. **No game uses the face `desire`** (author: it is the label of sexual intent); a relaxed or melted holder reads `relief`, a right touch `approval`.
5. **The digest of a game, for the main model (author, 2026-11-27).** Today a game ends with raw counts (`physics:end`: "3 openings caught, 2 missed, the hold broke twice", P10e-e R28) or a bare verdict word (`service_rated`), which the model cannot weigh: it cannot tell bad from fine, nor bad from bad-as-she-meant-it (a cruel holder who set out to push the commander to the limit). P16c gives every game, the ported ones included (a shared task of its first chunk; `physics:end` and the service verdict move onto it), one digest in words, never a number or a hidden value, for her next reply:
   - **How the commander did against her expectations:** better than she expected / as she expected / worse / a failure, judged by the engine against the difficulty of her own plan (her style, her manner, the run's plan), not against the raw counts.
   - **What she meant by it and whether she got it:** her aim by style and manner (to be looked after, to test, to torment, to teach) and whether it came out; so "the commander barely held" reads "as she meant, and she is satisfied" for a cruel holder and "she went too far and knows it" for an affectionate one.
   - **At most two notable things she perceived** (an uncaught cheat is never one, P3b), each with a **weight** 0–1 = its own significance (a knot let go high, pulling her foot away medium, a caught cheat medium) × how much it matters to her style (cheats: `cruel`, `vindictive`, `ritual`, `drill` ×1.5, `affectionate`, `playful` ×0.5) × how unusual it was for that game (each game has a **norm** in data, per minute, for caught cheats, slips and misses: at or under the norm ×0.3, well over it up to ×1.3; where cheating is all but needed to win, its norm is high). The heaviest goes in when its weight is ≥ 0.5; any other only with a chance equal to its weight, a hash of the run's id (`fnv1a`, no rng draw: the same run always gives the same digest). The wording follows the weight: under 0.4 "in passing" ("as usual, she caught a stolen breath or two"), over 0.7 "she will make a point of it". The reason (author): the model fixes on any cheat it is told of, and in some games a caught cheat is close to certain.
   - **The commander's state afterwards as she sees it** (breathing hard, the jaw trembling): the body only, never a feeling (P15 ruling E), no pronoun.
6. **Copy, do not retell (author).** The executor copies a prototype's code into the extension almost word for word (copy and paste, then change only the wiring); a plan or a brief names the prototype file, the functions and tables to copy and the lines whose wiring changes, and never restates a function's body in its own words. A retelling breeds mistakes and liberties. (Also in `docs/prototypes/README.md` and CLAUDE.md.)
7. **Foot care is approved at this level** (2026-11-27: the massage and the cream on her bare feet, rounds 1–2; its normative section is in `docs/prototypes/README.md`); its further steps (footwear, drying, nails) wait for a later round.
8. **A slot from another woman (author).** SYS › STAGE gains "take this slot from another woman" on a slot of her page: the other woman's picture and its sidecar (fit and zones) are copied into her folder (`<slotKey>.<ext>` and `<slotKey>.fit.json`, through `src/st/` alone, as SAVE writes them) and stay editable there; nothing of the other woman's folder changes. It comes with P16c's stage setup (ruling 1). Today the only way is copying the two files by hand between the `alius/` folders (hand-placed files are read, P10e §16.1).
9. **The bout and its hold are one slot (author; the first task of the escape game).** Today `playSparring` spends the bout's slot before the controller opens the hold's scene, so the hold opens in the next slot (it reads as half a day later), `offerHeadlock` checks the yard is open in `NEXT_SLOT` and the witnesses are those at the yard during the bout. Fixed: when a hold follows the bout (`headlock_offered`), the bout spends no slot; the hold's scene opens in the bout's own slot with that slot's witnesses and the yard's own slots, and the slot is spent when that scene ends (or is aborted or cancelled, or the hold is not started that day); with no hold, the bout spends its slot as before (a campaign with no craft stays byte-identical).
10. **Sparring is a mini-game (author, 2026-11-28: scheme A, a timing duel; approved after round 1).** The AUTO bout of the sparring wager becomes `sparring.html` (its normative section in `docs/prototypes/README.md`), calibrated to the author's bands: an average commander against an average soldier never wins and draws from a top 10–15 % performance; a hardened one wins from a top 5 % and draws from a top 20–25 %. A draw is its own outcome. Not every lost bout ends in the hold; after her win her finisher is chosen by her (the hold when its conditions meet, or another humiliation, with or without a game): the finishers are designed with the author next, then the escape from the hold.
11. **Her finishers after a won bout (author, 2026-11-28: the model approved).** Each finisher has a `humiliation` 0–5 (the presets' scale): 0 and 1 are story only (1 with no dynamic when she thinks poorly of the commander), 2 and up belong to the dynamic, capped by her frame (`play` 2, `role` 4, `rule` 5); her level under the cap by style (affectionate −2; playful, drill, ritual, clinical, cold −1; cruel, vindictive, possessive at the cap; regal at the cap and the harshest of it after clean hits on her) and manner `intensity` ±1; the pick within the level by the content gate, her limits, her expectations and, with witnesses, the finishers that show her position; a hash, no draw. A finisher that is a contact with physics stages it with her as the holder in the same scene (its `implies` sets both postures; staging starts its instrument); the others are a line of the digest. The list, its game marks and its numbers are `docs/drafts/2026-11-28-sparring-finishers.md` (the author edits there).
12. **A new content category `flatulence` (author),** off by default; it needs more than the one finisher: candidates in the draft above (a burst in the scent game, the throne, under her desk, a punishment, her day), to decide with the author.
13. **`flatulence` in two games (author, 2026-11-28; this package, a small addition):** a very rare single event in the scent game (`saturation`, where she sits on the commander's face or the commander is under her seat) and on the throne (`human_chair` / her activity's frame); off unless the category is on and her limits allow it; a hash, no draw. Its looks and numbers are settled in a prototype round of each game.
14. **The digest of a game after a lost bout reads the commander's play in its situation (author).** In the hold or another finisher with a game: cheating (a relief taken, a caught cheat) means the commander still resists; failing the game means the commander gave in; holding it to the end is neutral (the clicks cannot tell serving her from not giving in). The finisher's digest says so in words (P16c ruling 5), with the stance of ruling 15 once decided.
15. **The hold and its three stances (author, 2026-11-28; `headlock.html` approved after rounds 1–2).** The hold is one game of the pair breath + saturation (it has no instrument today and falls back to the physics card): the air comes only as far as her arm lets it, her windows by style. The commander **fights** (strain and break free in her slack: worse with her, a smaller fall in the watchers' regard), **lets it happen** (neutral) or **submits** (goes limp and gives her a sign: her approval by style, a lighter offence, a larger fall in the watchers' regard); the tap is the way out. The **motive** of the bout (`put_in_place`, `punishment`, `play`, `grievance`, `freedom`) decides what each stance means; sparring itself is never an offence. Its normative section is in `docs/prototypes/README.md`.
16. **The interactive strokes (author, 2026-11-28: scheme B; `strokes.html` approved after rounds 1–2).** Her physical discipline (P16 §R.2) is a game in four beats per stroke: **hear** it coming (she lines it up with a tap, the implement goes up as her shadow and her sound, feints by style, the swish ends on the impact), **brace** (Space, the slap's tension, one try: a braced stroke throws 0.55 of itself), **damp** the flinch on the dial (each target throws its posture's points toward where the body jerks, by force × 10 − pain × 2 and the heat of the strokes before; past 100 the posture breaks, past 55 she sees it under "do not flinch"), **name** it (the count typed in her rhythm, with the thanks under her rule; a miss restarts, adds or does not count, by style). Pulling away is defiance on its own key, **X** (round 2: a mouse flick was caught by the move to the dial). Out of her sight (bent over, chest down, over her knee) she is her shadow, her sound and her hand. Its normative section is in `docs/prototypes/README.md`. **The port copies `strokes.html` word for word** (ruling 6): its functions and tables are pasted and only the wiring changes, so that no round has to be played again after the port (author: no revisions of it later).
17. **`ticklish` is a level (author, 2026-11-28: A, "a little flavour").** An optional COMMANDER FILE detail with four steps: not / a little / very / unbearably; in stillness under provocation a tickle grows ×1 / ×1.25 / ×1.45 / ×1.8 (`her_activity.html`, `PROV.ticklish`), and the first tickle that breaks through on a very or unbearably ticklish commander gets her line by style (`PROV_TICKLISH`). No places, nothing beyond this game. The ported `src/ui/scene/instruments/activity/provocation.ts` still reads a checkbox that is always unchecked: the port replaces `PROV.ticklish` with the table and its `#ticklish` with the commander's level (an absent detail = not ticklish, byte-identical).
18. **`flatulence` in the two games, as built (author, 2026-11-28; approved: saturation.html after rounds 1–2, throne.html after round 1).** A buzz you cannot mistake (round 2: a band of noise did not read), a narration line, at most once a session by style (regal at a small share, as a favour she expects thanks for, typed); in the scent game the air at the face turns thick for 9 s and the commander breathes it in, coughs, takes the mouth or holds it out; on the throne it is felt through the back, the smell a moment later, a flinch to hold down and a visitor who hears it. Normative sections in `docs/prototypes/README.md` (the `saturation.html` and `throne.html` additions).
19. **Balance is the tray in her service (author, 2026-11-28: scheme A; `balance.html` approved after rounds 1–3).** Not weight.html again: the commander kneels and holds her tray (two axes, the mouse as the hands under it, toward the weight); she and her visitors set things down (never on another), take them, pour, press its edge or feint, her hand's shadow the warning; the liquids keep level, slosh and spill, past a steep tilt things slide and fall; a level tray does not tire the arms (author); a grace of 1.6 s at the start (author); drawn as a walnut tray with brass and real glassware (author). The visitors' talk with her is generated in the extension, as in every game where others talk with her (author). Its normative section is in `docs/prototypes/README.md`; **the port copies `balance.html` word for word** (ruling 6).

## What moved here

1. **Tempo** (P16b §6.2): massage, foot care, licking; keep her tempo, judged only by her tells (her expectation profile's "how").
2. **Balance** (§6.3): her thing or a tray in the hands, a load on the back or the head; keep a marker centred while she pulls and pushes.
3. **Order from memory** (§6.4): her routine said once, repeated later in order.
4. **Patience** (§6.6): nothing happens; do not press "call her"; the fiction time grows.
5. **The edge** (§6.7, `denial` only): a bar rises with her actions; keep it under a line with breath and focus inputs.
6. **Reading her** (§6.8): choose the next act from her tells alone.
7. **The escape from the sparring hold** (§9): timing against her grip, the commander's strength and `stillness` (P16b builds the hold itself with the tap as the way out).
8. **The interactive strokes** (P16 §R.2): implement × target × posture, the flinch impulse on posture points, the count aloud, rules of conduct, marks by implement and `her_marks`.
9. **The further steps of foot care** (P16b §8): unlacing, massage, drying, nails, cream, each judged against her expectation profile; the gestures for kneel / go / look at me in her foot language.
10. **`ticklish`** (P16b §6.1), an optional COMMANDER FILE detail, if stillness under provocation wants it.

Each game maps to a P16b discipline, can be switched off in SYS › Scene and reads its numbers from the engine (P15b `bendRow` where it has a style row). Plan: to be written after P16b is implemented.

## Interfaces already provided by P16b (implemented — use, do not rebuild)

P16b (service as a craft) is implemented; see its **Implemented as** in `docs/packages/P16b-service-craft.md` (rulings as built, memory check, deviations, open items). Everything it adds is off, absent and byte-identical while no holder is craft-live (`craftLive` = `practiceLive`; unasked care has `careOpen`), and none of it draws from the rng (`craftRoll`, a hash, an index into a registry-ordered list). Keep new directive ids away from `craft:`; read presets, contacts, care rows, echoes and booking kinds by field, never by id; hooks you register must keep a campaign with no craft byte-identical (`tests/craft/unaffected.test.ts`); no text gives the commander a pronoun or a feeling.

- **The book:** `addCraftXp(draft, reg, key, xp, cause)` is the one writer of a discipline's xp (`craft_xp`, `craft_level`); `craftLevel` / `craftCapacity` read it (`pain` and `posture` add the P7 body route; `posture` is the only discipline whose capacity has a reader today); `Commander.craft` holds `rhythm`, `memory`, `composure`, `speech`, `pain` and `depth`, which have no consumer beyond CMDR › CRAFT: the games and strokes here are theirs. Drills (`drill_stillness`, `drill_recitation`, `drill_rhythm`, `drill_posture`, and the partner drills of breath, weight and posture) are `train` foci with a `drill: { key, partner? }` field; a new game adds its focus the same way and a `craftLessons` row (read by `slip` / `kind` / `interaction`) so a slip can teach.
- **Fiction time and records:** `fictionSeconds(physicsId, beats, level)` over `TUNING.craft.beatSeconds`, `noteRun` / `setRecord` (`record_set`, `Relation.craft.records`); a new game that holds counts beats and gets records, `drill` norms and the CRAFT margin for free.
- **A game with a hold of its own** follows the posture game's pattern: `registerPostureInstrument` / `PostureInstrumentDef` (`src/ui/scene/instruments/host.ts`, mounted beside the physics instruments, no `physics_outcome`), reports through `PhysicsPanelStaging` (`postureCorrect`, `postureSlip`, `postureEnd`, `signal`) to engine actions that judge, a toggle in `INTERACTION_TOGGLES` (`posture`, `provocation` are not physics interactions), a `fidelity.json` of its prototype. `startPostureHold` / `endPosture` / `PostureRun` are the hold; `physics/relief` has the kinds `react` (a reaction she always feels) and `endure` (a stimulus let go by pays `stillness`).
- **The escape from the sparring hold** goes inside the existing hold: the contact is the `blockingContacts` row with `headlock: true`, `Relation.craft.headlock` waits for the day, `craft/headlock_start` stages it and starts its physics, the tap is the way out, `registerAfterBout` offers it after a won bout (`headlockWished` decides by her expectation), the witnesses are charged in `noteHeadlockOutcome`.
- **Foot care:** `feetOfDay(state, reg, unitId)` (derived: `fresh`, `worn`, `hot`, `sore`, `bare`), `Relation.craft.footCare` (a pleased foot or shoe service, since her last run) and `careOfFeet` (±3 % in her next run); her foot language is `signalsOf` plus `Relation.craft.signals` (named once, `foot_signal_given`; answered trains `memory`; missed is `foot_signal_missed` and a slip that waits for her discipline) reported through `PhysicsPanelStaging.signal` → `physics/service { runId, signal }` → `registerServiceReport`. The gestures for kneel / go / look at me, and the further steps of care, are yours; the foot service's instrument re-names each signal each session (open item).
- **`ticklish`:** the provocation game (`activity/provocation.ts`, `attachProvocation`) reads a stillness level and a ticklish multiplier of 1; `provocationOpen` is its gate.
- **Hidden:** xp, drift rates, the settling-in count (kept for physics holds too, read by nothing yet: `settlingDue` acts only on posture holds), the lesson word before she says it, an unnamed signal; read through `Knowledge.craft`.
