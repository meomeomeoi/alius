# P10b — Intimate Immersion UI: Telemetry, Living DMs, Night Door, Handwriting, Device Marks

**Depends on:** P10 (voices, hub, device), P9c (device access levels — data), P9d (Want, rules). **Read:** overview; this file; P10.

**Analysis tool (P7a):** every new player-facing stat, chip, option and choice card in this package carries `data-analyze`, has a glossary entry or registry `description`, and live values get an analyze provider reading through `Knowledge`.

## Goal

Make relationships visible and felt through the interface rather than numbers: squad biotelemetry on the PDA, messenger behaviour that reflects state, a night interface built around a door, per-character handwriting, and the visual side of device access (stickers, wallpaper, pinned notes).

## 1. Telemetry

PDA → **VITALS** (inside SQUAD and as a mini strip on the hub): per named unit, a live line for heart rate and stress pulses, temperature as a small reading. Legitimate in-world data: corporate implants report it.

Driven by state (no LLM): baseline by stress band; spikes on events (combat, conflict, the commander entering her room, high Want); flat line for Affect Dampener with rare flickers (each flicker is a logged moment); erratic for breaking stress. Values are illustrative bands, never exact hidden numbers; P7a knowledge filter applies (no mind-reading through vitals). Analyze tooltips explain what each pattern can mean.

## 2. Living DMs (personal voice)

Messenger behaviours generated by the engine from state, around texts produced by `dm_reply`:
- typing indicator that starts and stops without a message (hesitation; more with `attachment: avoidant` or after conflict);
- "message deleted" placeholders;
- read receipts and "read 3h ago";
- double/triple texts and late-night messages when Want ≥ 40;
- voice messages: a waveform bubble, text revealed on click;
- curt one-word replies when Affinity drops or she's in `resentful`.
Rules for each behaviour live in registry `dmBehaviours` with conditions and probabilities.

## 3. Night interface and the door

Night slot switches the shell to a night theme: dimmer, warmer palette variant, quieter sounds, slower motion. Night visits (P2/P3 queue, P9d Want, P9c bookings) render as **the door**: a frosted-glass panel with her silhouette (sprite, blurred), a knock animation and sound, and a short timer: `[OPEN] [SAY SOMETHING THROUGH THE DOOR] [STAY SILENT]`. No answer before the timer ends = she leaves (a choice with consequences). Saying something opens a one-exchange scene through the door before deciding.

## 4. Handwriting

Each named unit gets a handwriting font at import (from a bundled OFL set of ~6 hand fonts; player can change). Used in the `paper` voice for her notes: left in the commander's quarters (appear on the hub when he enters), in his gear, under the door; relationship rules (P9d) render as a note she signed — he signs or not; a broken rule tears the note (animation).

## 5. Device marks (visuals for P9c access levels 1–2)

- Stickers on the PDA frame (registry `stickerArt`, per-unit flavour, SVG/CSS), placed in free spots of the current skin.
- Wallpaper: her dithered portrait (P10) on the PDA home.
- Renamed contact in COMMS.
- Pinned notes on the home screen in her handwriting.
- Peeling a sticker / changing wallpaper: an explicit action with a confirm, reported to P9c logic.
- After a break-up: faded sticker for 7 days.

## Acceptance criteria

1. VITALS renders lines from state; event spikes and Affect Dampener flicker appear; no hidden values leak (knowledge filter test).
2. DM behaviours fire by their rules; voice messages reveal text on click.
3. Night theme applies only in the night slot; the door appears for night visits with the timer and three choices; silence = she leaves with consequences.
4. Handwriting fonts assigned and changeable; notes appear on the hub; rule notes sign/tear.
5. Stickers, wallpaper, contact name and pinned notes follow P9c access levels; peeling is reported.
6. Reduced motion and readable mode (P10) also disable night/door/typing animations.
